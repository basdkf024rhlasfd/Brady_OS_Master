Filtered by Brady's constraints: automation-native, leverages existing capabilities, first-dollar in 30 days, no hiring, no new infra, ≤$5k startup. 3 ideas — quality over volume.

### 1. Consulting OS as a Managed Sublease

**Pitch:** Small consulting boutiques (2–8 person shops) pay Brady $2k–5k/mo to run their engagement OS — daily exec-intel-brief per client, weekly commissioner brief per practice, project-agent-standup as a service. They keep the client relationships; Brady's OS provides the substrate.
**Economics:** $2.5k/mo/practice · 4 practices = $10k MRR · churn assumption 15%/yr. First dollar at first sold slot, no build required (skills already exist).
**OS vs Brady split:** OS does 95% (all scheduled deliverables, all Notion writes). Brady does the sales call + monthly QBR (2 hrs/customer/mo).
**Path to first dollar:** Warm intro to one boutique via network. Free 30-day pilot with one client, then $2.5k/mo.
**Kill risk:** Boutiques treat their engagement rituals as proprietary and won't outsource them.

### 2. mception Insight Feeds (Paid Substack per Vertical)

**Pitch:** Package `exec-intel-brief` + `daily-whitepaper` into vertical-specific paid Substacks — QSR Ops (Panda-adjacent), Furniture Retail (1915 South-adjacent), Broker CPG. $29/mo/subscriber, one publication per week per vertical.
**Economics:** 50 subs/vertical × 3 verticals × $29/mo = ~$4.4k MRR. Zero incremental delivery cost — Musashi's tech scan + Exa research already runs.
**OS vs Brady split:** OS drafts + designs 100%. Brady approves the send button and edits the top-line take (30 min/week/vertical).
**Path to first dollar:** Launch one vertical (Furniture Retail) as free tier for 30 days, convert 10% at end of intro window.
**Kill risk:** The Substack audience wants operator voice, not AI-synthesized voice — you're competing with LinkedIn free content.

### 3. "Runway Radar" — Anomaly Alerts for Fractional CFOs

**Pitch:** Sell `financial-anomaly-review` as a productized alerting service to fractional CFOs / bookkeepers who manage 5–20 SMB clients. They plug in a Monarch or QuickBooks feed; Runway Radar pings anomalies daily via SMS/email. $99/mo/CFO firm, $10/mo/client under management.
**Economics:** 5 CFO firms × ($99 + 10 clients × $10) = ~$1k MRR at very early stage. Scales linearly with per-firm client count.
**OS vs Brady split:** OS does anomaly detection + delivery. Brady does one sales call per firm + one troubleshoot per month.
**Path to first dollar:** Post the 9-detector logic as a LinkedIn carousel → inbound from bookkeepers who see themselves in the pain description.
**Kill risk:** Bookkeepers are risk-averse tool adopters; convincing them to trust automated anomaly detection is a 12+ month sales cycle even at $99/mo.
