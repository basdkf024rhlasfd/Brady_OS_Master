# Digital Intelligence

Digital signals, social monitoring, and discovery surface analysis.

---

## Digital Signals

### Strongest current surface

**Product detail pages**

PDP hygiene appears materially stronger than browse and discovery surfaces.

### Highest priority fix

**Category and search pages**

The biggest digital risk is a weak crawlable browse layer, not a lack of product detail.

### Marketplace opportunity

**Mirakl onboarding story**

Use the site to explain how digital commerce strengthens dealers instead of bypassing them.

---

## What to Fix First

1. **Browse and category pages** — The crawlable browse layer is the weakest surface. Fix category page structure, internal linking, and content depth before anything else.
2. **Marketplace narrative** — The Mirakl B2B marketplace is live but the story of how it strengthens dealers is not being told on the site.
3. **Battery education** — Battery performance parity, runtime, and quieter operation need more explicit positioning across product and category pages.

## Social Monitoring

### YouTube

*Theme*

Battery runtime, torque, and practical job completion claims dominate comparison chatter.

> Runtime proof and real-use demonstrations are more persuasive than generic battery messaging.

### Reddit / forums

*Theme*

Dealer service remains a durable STIHL advantage whenever ownership experience is discussed.

> The service moat should show up more explicitly in digital copy and artifact language.

### Review surfaces

*Theme*

Noise, startup ease, and maintenance simplicity continue to shape battery conversation.

> That gives STIHL a better bridge story from gas leadership into battery adoption.
