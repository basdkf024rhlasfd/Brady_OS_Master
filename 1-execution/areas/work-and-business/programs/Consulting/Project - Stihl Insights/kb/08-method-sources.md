# Method & Sources

Where signals come from, how this briefing works, and what standards the content has to meet before it is shown.

---

## Operating Model

This is a private competitive briefing layer. It is meant to help Rob brief leadership faster, not to replace judgment or become a passive dashboard.

The model stays focused on a short list of high-signal questions for STIHL:

- competitor moves
- tariff exposure and pricing implications
- digital commerce quality
- launch activity
- category interpretation

Content is curated, not dumped into the system raw. A good entry is specific, dated, commercially relevant, and clear about what STIHL should do with it.

---

## Standards For Time-Sensitive Claims

Every current claim shown in the briefing should answer these questions:

1. What is the source?
2. What is the date or timing window?
3. Why does it matter to STIHL?
4. Does it imply an action, decision, or talking point?

If a claim cannot pass that test, it should move down a level, be rewritten, or be cut.
