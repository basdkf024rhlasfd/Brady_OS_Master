# Method & Sources

Where signals come from and how the service operates.

---

## Operating Model

This is a private competitive briefing layer — not a dashboard, not a chat tool, not a replacement for judgment. It's ammunition for it.

The model focuses on the highest-signal questions for STIHL: competitor moves, tariff exposure, digital commerce quality, launch activity, and category interpretation.

Content is curated, not automated. Every insight has a data point. Every recommendation has a "why." The surface gets smarter every week as the intelligence base compounds.

## Source Stack

### Tariffs and trade

- USITC DataWeb
- HTS schedules
- USTR actions
- Customs and import datasets

### Financial and company signals

- Annual reports
- Earnings transcripts
- Investor presentations
- Public market data feeds

### Digital and category monitoring

- stihlusa.com crawls
- Competitor site checks
- Search result monitoring
- Content diffs

### Social and voice of customer

- Reddit
- YouTube
- Forums
- Public review surfaces

### Geography and weather

- NOAA
- Regional weather APIs
- Storm activity
- Seasonality overlays
