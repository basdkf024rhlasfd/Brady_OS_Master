# Tariff Watch

Tariff signals, structural advantages, and the talking points STIHL can use right now.

---

## Current Read

### STIHL position

**Relatively advantaged**

The internal working assumption remains that STIHL's domestic sourcing footprint gives it a simpler cost story than more import-heavy peers.

### Peer pressure

**Still elevated**

Brands with heavier China exposure are still carrying more pricing and margin pressure through 2026 planning.

### What that means

**Use the gap**

The tariff story is only valuable if it becomes a commercial narrative: price discipline, sourcing resilience, and dealer confidence.

---

## Numbers to Use Carefully

| Signal | Working figure | How to use it |
|--------|----------------|---------------|
| STIHL domestic sourcing | 60% | Use as a proof point for relative insulation, not as a standalone boast. |
| Estimated peer cost gap | $20-40 per unit | Use for talk tracks on why import-heavy peers may need different pricing behavior. |
| TTI exposure | $2-3B estimate | Use as directional context, not as a headline claim without source backup in the final artifact. |

---

## What To Say

1. STIHL is better positioned to absorb tariff volatility without turning every conversation into a price increase conversation.
2. Dealers benefit when the manufacturer can defend value without reacting as sharply to import cost shocks.
3. The right use of the tariff story is calm confidence, not chest-thumping.
