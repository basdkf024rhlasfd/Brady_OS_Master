## New Since Last Update

### DeWalt price discipline remains a usable selling point

*Source: Stanley Black & Decker earnings framing · February 2026*

Stanley Black & Decker is still managing tariff and pricing pressure rather than declaring the issue behind it.

> So what for STIHL: Every peer price increase should be translated into plain dealer and leadership language about margin stability, sourcing resilience, and why STIHL does not need to chase the same pricing posture.

### Husqvarna keeps pushing battery credibility upmarket

*Source: Husqvarna product and launch watch · Q1 2026*

The 550i XP and 564 XP keep Husqvarna relevant in the pro-grade battery and chainsaw conversation even while broader performance remains pressured.

> So what for STIHL: The competitive risk is not only assortment depth. It is the story Husqvarna can tell about pro users moving into battery without compromise.

### STIHL has a live commerce proof point but the message is still underbuilt

*Source: stihlusa.com marketplace status review · March 2026*

The marketplace is live, but the site still does not fully explain how digital commerce supports dealers rather than routing around them.

> So what for STIHL: This is one of the fastest message fixes available. A clearer dealer-support narrative strengthens both channel confidence and digital credibility.

### Browse and search remain the quickest digital upgrade

*Source: current site review · March 2026*

Product detail pages are stronger than category, browse, and search surfaces.

> So what for STIHL: If the goal is near-term commercial lift, the highest-leverage digital work is still category structure, internal linking, and battery education on discovery pages.
