## TARIFF LANDSCAPE — APRIL 2026

### Current Tariff Framework

The tariff environment has shifted significantly since SBD's February earnings call:

1. **Supreme Court IEEPA ruling (February 2026):** Struck down President Trump's authority to impose tariffs under IEEPA. Fundamentally changed the tariff legal framework.

2. **Section 122 Global Tariff (effective February 21, 2026):** 15% tariff on all imports. Replaced the prior IEEPA-based tariff structure.

3. **Section 232 Metals Tariffs (effective April 6, 2026):** 50% tariff on aluminum, steel, and copper products. 25% on derivatives made substantially from these materials. 15% on industrial equipment containing these metals.

4. **Section 301 Investigations (March 2026):** USTR launched new investigations into excess capacity and forced labor. Hearings scheduled April–May 2026. Potential for additional tariffs.
