# STIHL Competitive Intelligence — What Success Looks Like

## Who This Is For
Rob Jenson, Director of eCommerce, STIHL USA

## The Problem We're Solving

You need competitive intelligence that's fast, specific, and ready to use — not raw data that requires hours of assembly before it's useful to leadership.

Your current process:
- Internal team manually assembles briefs when asked
- Coverage is inconsistent — some competitors are well-tracked, others are blind spots
- When leadership asks about a competitor move or tariff change, it takes days to produce a response
- Digital analytics are ad hoc — no systematic view of where stihlusa.com stands vs the field
- You spend time managing production instead of thinking strategically

## What You Get

A private intelligence briefing surface, updated weekly:

### Morning Brief
The single place to start every day. What changed, why it matters, what to do about it. Includes ready-to-copy prompts you can paste into ChatGPT or Claude for immediate analysis.

### Competitors
Living profiles on Husqvarna, Deere, Toro, Honda, Echo/Yamabiko, and Milwaukee Tool. Each profile tracks: current position, latest moves, why it matters to STIHL, supporting data. Updated when things change.

### Digital Analytics
Systematic view of where stihlusa.com stands vs competitors: site performance, search/SEO, marketplace presence, feature gaps. Analytical, not marketing — real findings, not vanity metrics.

### Examples
Finished, presentation-ready examples: executive one-pagers, tariff memos, battlecards, digital benchmarks. Each includes a prompt so you can regenerate or customize it on your own.

### Request Inbox
Need something specific? Submit a request — a custom brief, a competitor comparison, talking points for a meeting. Requests are captured directly in the surface and triaged within the week.

## How We Measure Success

These are the outcomes that matter — from your chair, not ours.

### 1. Speed to Brief
**Before:** Days to assemble a competitive brief for leadership.
**After:** Pull a leadership-ready brief from the surface in under 5 minutes.

### 2. No Blind Spots
**Before:** Finding out about competitor moves when your boss asks about them.
**After:** You know first. Zero "I'll have to get back to you" moments.

### 3. Time Reclaimed
**Before:** Hours per week spent assembling intelligence from scratch.
**After:** 75%+ reduction in assembly time — the surface has the substance, you add the judgment.

### 4. Quality of Output
**Before:** Variable format, inconsistent depth, some analysis strong, some thin.
**After:** Every artifact is presentation-ready. Every insight has a data point. Every recommendation has a "why."

### 5. Compounding Value
**Before:** Every analysis starts from zero.
**After:** The intelligence base gets richer every week. By month 3, the surface anticipates what you need before you ask.

## What This Is Not

- Not a dashboard to monitor passively
- Not a chat tool or AI product
- Not a replacement for your judgment — it's ammunition for it
- Not a ticketing system — requests are a conversation, not a queue

## How It Works Week to Week

1. Surface updates weekly with fresh intelligence
2. The Morning Brief tells you what changed and what to do
3. You copy prompts, open artifacts, browse competitors as needed
4. You submit a request when you need something custom
5. The surface adapts based on what you actually use — sections you rely on get deeper, prompts you copy get refined
