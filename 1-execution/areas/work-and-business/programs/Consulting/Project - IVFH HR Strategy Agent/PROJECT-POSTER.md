| WS3: Skills Inventory | Assess actual capabilities — industry-specific, not generic | IVFH-calibrated skills taxonomy with basic/proficient/expert for each skill |

| WS5: Knowledge Graph | Map ownership, expertise, bottlenecks (Phase 2) | Intelligent routing — right owner for the problem, not just the usual person |
| WS6: Workforce Optimization | CEO dashboard — invest, cut, automate, outsource (Phase 2) | Business judgment calls informed by data, tied to KPIs and cash generation |
