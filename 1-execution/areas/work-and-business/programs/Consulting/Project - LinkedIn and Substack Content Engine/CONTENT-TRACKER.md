## Editorial Guardrails

- **Privacy gates:** Bertha sessions (020), Manifesto (012), faith transition content contain deeply personal material. Extract frameworks and lessons — don't publish raw diary entries. Brady's explicit approval required.
- **Family content:** Mother's column (027, Thinking of Dawn) requires Susan Elzey's permission. Kids' names/details should be anonymized or approved.
- **Voice:** Brady's natural writing voice is direct, honest, slightly self-deprecating, and punctuated by clean one-liners. Don't over-polish. The rawness IS the brand.
- **Notion sources:** Diary DB, Journal DB, Streaming Notes DB, Context Vault, Reference Layer. All live under the OS 2.0 Memory Layer.
