# Small Town America — LinkedIn Series

## Core Thesis

AI is the first technology wave that reverses the 30-year talent and capability drain from small-town America.

## Why Brady Wins This Lane

Almost no one in the AI conversation has this combination:
- Small-town origin (Danville, gravel road, King Video)
- Firsthand loss narrative (dad's business wiped out by tech shifts)
- Elite exposure (Chicago Booth, Walmart data science, Kroger, COO seat)
- Then stepping away to rebuild from scratch

Brady isn't an AI cheerleader. He's a translator between two worlds that don't understand each other. That's the brand.

## Tone (per post, not mixed)

- Grounded
- Plainspoken
- Slightly defiant

- Optimistic but not naive
- Less Silicon Valley hype, more "guy at a diner who happens to be right"
