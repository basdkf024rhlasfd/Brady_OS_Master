## LinkedIn Opinion Post

I made a song for the Danville, Virginia humane society using AI. Took maybe an hour. They posted it online. PETA reached out about it.

That's not a flex — it's a data point.

I also built AI agents for a buddy back home who does real estate. Knowledge base, lead gen, email drafting, calendar management. Stuff that would've cost him $40K/year in SaaS subscriptions he'd never fully use — knocked out in a weekend.

Small town tradespeople are about to have access to tools that used to require an enterprise sales team and a six-figure contract. That shift hasn't fully landed yet, but it's coming fast.

I keep thinking about this: what if a thousand people read this post and went and did a thousand short vibe coding projects for a family member, a friend, or a local nonprofit?

Help a church put together an employee manual. Help a local gym make social media posts. Help your cousin's landscaping company draft better estimates.

These aren't hypotheticals. I've done versions of all of them. Most take a weekend. Some take an hour.

It reminds me of trying to set up Dropbox for my dad years ago. The value was so obvious — but the technology wasn't accessible enough for it to stick. That gap doesn't exist anymore.

Your town has a hundred organizations that could use this kind of help. You don't need a product. You don't need a business plan. You just need to ask your AI a question and see what comes back.

So — what's the nonprofit in your town that could use a weekend of your time?
