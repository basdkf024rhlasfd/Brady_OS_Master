## Slide 3: Why Traditional Innovation Fails

**The Workshop Model Is Structurally Broken**

```
[Insights Dump] → [Trend Deck] → [Brainstorm] → [Committee Filter] → [Business Case] → [Dies in Follow-Up]
       ↑                                                                                        ↑
  Most creative energy                                                               Most ideas die here
  spent here (no data)                                                               (12-18 months later)
```

**3 fatal flaws:**
1. **Front-loaded thinking, back-loaded validation** — creative energy is spent before any consumer signal exists
2. **Groupthink by design** — workshops over-index on safe, familiar ideas
3. **No real-time feedback** — by the time focus groups report, the trend has peaked
