## Sport
Professional services — advisory and consulting engagements leveraging COO/operations expertise in food retail, grocery, and AI-native business operations.

## Team
Client management capability, engagement delivery, proposal development, thought leadership.

## Coach
To be assigned at Cycle review.

## Strategic Outcome
Generate recurring revenue through advisory engagements that leverage existing expertise without requiring full-time employment.

## Operating Principles
1. Consulting is services, not products — distinct from Ventures
2. Each engagement must have a clear scope, timeline, and fee before starting
3. Referral networks (AlphaSights, Prosapient, direct) are the pipeline — nurture them
4. If an engagement evolves into a venture opportunity, spin it off into the Ventures Program
5. Thought leadership (case studies, published work) feeds the pipeline long-term

## Key Metrics
- Pipeline value (total potential revenue from active leads)
- Active engagements count
- Revenue per cycle
- Client satisfaction / repeat engagement rate

## Milestones
- First paying client
- First recurring client
- Revenue target per quarter
