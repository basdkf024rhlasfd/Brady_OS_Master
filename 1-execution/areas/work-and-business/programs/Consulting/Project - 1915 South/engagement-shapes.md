# Engagement Shapes — Internal Prep for Next Justin Conversation

**Purpose:** Three concrete engagement options Brady can speak to fluidly if Justin asks "so what would four weeks actually look like?"

**Internal only.** Do not send. These are pocket answers, not proposals. Each is a 1-paragraph shape with a deliverable and a price range.

---

## Option A — M&A Playbook Intensive (4 weeks)

**Shape:** Codify 1915 South's M&A operating model while Yulee + Fineman are still fresh. Brady sits between Justin and the VP Corp Dev role, operates as deal-team principal.

**Deliverables:**
- 40-item diligence checklist (financials, real estate, inventory, staff, customer base, vendor contracts, Ashley corporate considerations)
- 90-day integration runbook with RACI, milestones, kill criteria
- First target dossier — full workup on one named candidate (recommend: Ashley HomeStores of Southwest VA, per SFDR-010)
- Capital partner intro map — 2-3 named PE firms / family offices Brady introduces informally for 18-24 month optionality

**Price range:** $35–50K flat fee or $12K/week.

**Positioning:** "Not fractional CFO. M&A-and-capital operating partner with the AI decision layer underneath."

**Best if:** Justin is most activated on the acquisition muscle / VP Corp Dev role and wants immediate leverage on the next deal.

---

## Option B — GMROI Diagnostic Pilot (4 weeks)

**Shape:** Brady + internal team build the execution layer on top of Ashley's GMROI Excellence Framework. Proves the agentic decision-layer concept in the one place Ashley corporate is already watching.

**Deliverables:**
- 30-store GMROI baseline (current state, by state, by category)
- Execution-layer architecture — sits on top of Zapsight, not in it
- Three pilot recommendations scored by impact × effort (using the scatter plot framework from the Innovation Workshop)
- Weekly-cadence dashboard prototype for store-manager-level action

**Price range:** $25–40K flat fee or $8–10K/week.

**Positioning:** "The operating layer between Zapsight's data and Ashley corporate's mandate."

**Best if:** Justin wants the most concrete near-term ROI measurable against Ashley's own framework — and wants to show Wanek-level progress at the next corporate review.

---

## Option C — Agentic Decision-Layer Sprint (4 weeks)

**Shape:** Broadest of the three. Architect the full decision layer — GMROI + assortment + M&A diligence triage + role redefinition framework — as an integrated operating system, not three point solutions.

**Deliverables:**
- Decision-layer architecture document (1915 South-specific, not generic)
- Three working use cases: GMROI store-level action, regional assortment editor, M&A target triage
- Integration plan with Zapsight (RACI + SOW revision)
- 18-month roadmap tied to Ashley corporate's $2B innovation commitment

**Price range:** $30–45K flat fee or $10K/week.

**Positioning:** "The 3-5 year AI mandate Wanek keeps talking about, translated into something a 30-store licensee can actually run."

**Best if:** Justin wants to think big and position 1915 South as Ashley corporate's showcase licensee (the elevation move).

---

## Option D — "Paid Listening Tour" (2 weeks, low-risk lead-in)

If Justin is warm but non-committal, this is the softer entry:

**Shape:** 2 weeks paid discovery, no hard deliverable other than a re-scoped engagement proposal at end.

**Deliverables:**
- Week 1: Brady meets with Justin + VP Corp Dev + current CFO/controller + Zapsight lead
- Week 2: Brady writes one crisp recommendation — what to do next, what not to do, scope for deeper engagement
- Optional: 30-min share-out with Russell at end

**Price range:** $10–15K flat fee.

**Positioning:** "Paid option-to-continue. No pressure on you or us."

**Best if:** Justin says "I'm interested but not sure yet" — this avoids both sides negotiating scope in the abstract.

## How to pick when Justin asks

Listen for the activation signal in his reply:

| Justin mentions | Recommend |
|---|---|
| The new VP Corp Dev role, FRG real estate, acquisitions, Yulee debrief | **Option A (M&A Intensive)** |
| Ashley corporate / Wanek / GMROI pressure / the monthly reporting grind | **Option B (GMROI Diagnostic)** |
| "How does all this connect" / "we need a plan" / Zapsight scope concerns | **Option C (Decision-Layer Sprint)** |
| "I need to think about it" / "not sure what shape this takes" | **Option D (Listening Tour)** |

## Pricing thought

None of these exceed $50K for 4 weeks. That's inside the "scoped project" budget any $182M retailer can approve at the Justin level without board drag. Above $50K, Russell has to sign — which is a different conversation with different timing.

## What NOT to say

- Don't quote retainer rates (monthly) — that signals fractional CFO framing Brady's explicitly moved away from
- Don't quote equity / ownership — that's a Russell conversation, not a Justin one
- Don't open pricing first — let Justin's scope signal drive it
- Don't negotiate downward from the top of the range in the first conversation — the number is a placeholder until scope is locked
