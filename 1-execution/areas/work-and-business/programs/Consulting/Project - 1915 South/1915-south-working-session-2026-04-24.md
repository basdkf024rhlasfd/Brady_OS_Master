##### Thesis 2

## GMROI Execution: The Gap Is the Decision Layer

Zapsight handles the data foundation. The layer that translates data into store-level decisions — that's not built yet.

**What's missing between Zapsight and the store floor:**
- Weekly GMROI dashboards designed for manager action (not HQ reporting)
- Execution protocols: how a DM responds to a slow-turn cluster vs. a velocity gap
- Regional assortment logic: when to edit vs. hold the national planogram
- AI-enabled decision triage: systematic vs. judgment-call boundaries

**Why this matters to Ashley corporate:**
Wanek's GMROI directive is specific. 1915 South building a visible execution layer on top of Zapsight positions it as the showcase licensee — the model Ashley points to at the next franchisee forum.

*Positioned on top of Zapsight. Not instead of it.*

##### Thesis 3

## Sleep Vertical: A Built-In Tailwind, Specific to Southeast

Tempur Sealy closed the Mattress Firm acquisition Feb 2025. As part of the deal, they committed 25% of MFRM floor space to third-party brands for 5 years. That's a structural opening for Ashley-branded sleep on floor.

**The A1 concept (from Innovation Workshop):**
Sleep Lab specialty format — within or adjacent to existing Ashley stores. Trained sleep advisors, AI-matched mattress recommendation, distinct in-store experience. Takes share in a category where traffic is intentional.

**Why it fits 1915 South specifically:**
- Southeast markets overlap heavily with Tempur Sealy's MFRM footprint
- Ashley manufacturing scale gives 1915 South cost structure MFRM can't match post-acquisition
- 2–3 store pilot is executable within one operating quarter

*Lowest acquisition cost in the category. Highest intentional-purchase signal.*
