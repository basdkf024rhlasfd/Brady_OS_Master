# Brady's 4-Week Commitment Framework (Personal)

**Purpose:** Decide — in real time, with a pre-thought rubric — whether to commit to any engagement longer than four weeks before Justin responds on 1915 South.

**Audience:** Brady only. Not for clients, not for Fran's client-facing work.

**Why this exists:** Brady told Justin he's "generally not committing beyond four weeks" with two other client conversations tomorrow. One of those is angling for longer-term. Without a rubric, that decision gets made under pressure in the moment. This is the rubric.

---

## The five dimensions

Score each opportunity 1–5 on each. Higher = better. Sum = gut-check. Override only with a written reason.

### 1. Urgency (1–5)
How fast does this client's problem need to be worked? 
- 1 = they're shopping, no deadline
- 3 = there's a calendar event in 3 months driving urgency
- 5 = something breaks for them in <30 days without action

### 2. Cash (1–5)
Near-term $ to Brady. 
- 1 = <$10K/month or long-payment-cycle
- 3 = $10–20K/month with 30-day payment
- 5 = $25K+/month with retainer upfront

### 3. Learning (1–5)
Does this engagement sharpen Brady's AI consulting edge?
- 1 = commodity work Brady could have done 5 years ago
- 3 = operator-grade work in a domain Brady already knows
- 5 = builds a new capability or credentialing artifact (new industry, new AI pattern, new operator story)

### 4. Relationship (1–5)
Does the client's network multiply Brady's reach?
- 1 = silo'd, no crossover with other Brady targets
- 3 = operator-grade relationship, good for referrals
- 5 = opens a network of 10+ peer operators / PE firms / family offices / strategic buyers

### 5. Optionality preservation (1–5)
Does this engagement preserve Brady's ability to say yes to 1915 South if it lands?
- 1 = full-time, long-term, geographic relocation, blocks everything else
- 3 = 3-month engagement, ≥50% of Brady's available hours
- 5 = 2–4 week scope, <30% of available hours, naturally completes

**Scoring rule:** Any opportunity scoring <3 on dimension 5 auto-fails unless it also scores 5 on dimensions 1 AND 2 combined. 1915 South optionality is the hardest-to-replace asset in the portfolio right now.

---

## Decision thresholds

| Total score | Decision |
|---|---|
| 20–25 | Commit, even if >4 weeks. Write a term sheet tonight. |
| 15–19 | Commit only to a 4-week scope. Revisit after that. |
| 10–14 | Decline politely. Offer a referral if you have one. |
| <10 | Pass. Don't respond with a counter-offer. |

---

## Soft rules (harder to score but real)

- **If 1915 South lands while you're in a >4-week engagement:** you can run both only if the non-1915 South work is scoped at <40 hours/week AND the client explicitly agrees to a flexible schedule. Anything less = one of them suffers.
- **If both tomorrow's conversations score 15+:** don't commit to both. Pick one. The other gets a "let me come back in three weeks" response.
- **If one scores 20+ and offers equity/revenue share:** the equity component dials up optionality-preservation by 1 point (because it aligns incentives for both sides to want Brady flexible when 1915 South lands).
- **If Justin responds with enthusiasm in the meantime:** drop the tomorrow calls to confirm-only mode. Don't commit to anything new until 1915 South's shape is defined.
