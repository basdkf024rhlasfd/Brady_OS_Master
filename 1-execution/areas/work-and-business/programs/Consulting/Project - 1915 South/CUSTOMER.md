## Who We're Working With

**1915 South** is a top-50 US furniture retailer operating 30 Ashley HomeStore locations across the Southeast, with 3 distribution centers and HQ in Thomasville, GA. Founded in 1915 as a Pelham, GA hardware business; transformed into a furniture franchise in 2002 when Russell Turner opened the first Ashley HomeStore. Fourth-generation family ownership, record sales and profit in 2025, first non-family President (Justin Woods) hired January 2025.

## The Opportunity

1915 South sits at the intersection of three forces that each demand operating-layer capability:

1. **Ashley corporate's GMROI franchise directive** — published December 2024, driving measurement and margin discipline down to every franchisee. Responding well requires analytics infrastructure most franchisees don't have.
2. **Todd Wanek's AI mandate** — *"3–5 years to figure out AI or get left behind."* 1915 South has engaged Zapsight for a data foundation + agentic AI — a strong vendor move that still leaves an internal operating layer open.
3. **Continued acquisition-driven growth** — Jacksonville (2022), Yulee (2025), and the consolidation path Russell has stated publicly. Scaling operating consistency across fast-acquired stores is the recurring challenge.
