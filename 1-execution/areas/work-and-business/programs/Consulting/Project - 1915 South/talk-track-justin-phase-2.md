## Positioning Language (use after listening, not before)

Once Justin has activated on one of the three priorities:

> "Here's how I'd think about it: over the next four weeks I could build [specific deliverable from engagement-shapes.md matched to his activation]. Scoped and paid, not fractional CFO — this is operating-partner work. We'd know at the end of four weeks whether the engagement continues or wraps clean. If it works, the question of role is a Russell conversation. If it doesn't, you've got a concrete artifact either way."

**Key phrases to actually say:**
- "Operating partner" (not "advisor" or "consultant")
- "Four weeks" (not a month, not a retainer)
- "Scoped and paid" (confirms this is a transaction, not a favor)
- "Russell conversation" (acknowledges the ownership boundary)

**Key phrases to avoid:**
- "Fractional CFO" (Brady explicitly moved away from this framing)
- "Retainer" (monthly recurring signals fractional)
- "Embedded" (sounds like he wants full-time without saying it)
- "Partner" without "operating" modifier (sounds like he wants equity)
