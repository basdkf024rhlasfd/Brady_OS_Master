## Methods Used (from full-stack-ideation)

Ten methods chosen for breadth across product, ops, and org design:

- **#3 First Principles Thinking** — re-derive what a furniture retailer's backoffice actually needs at $182M/30-store scale
- **#5 Value Chain Expansion** — where in the chain (showroom → DC → final-mile → service) is value leaking
- **#8 Constraint-Based Ideation** — accept Ashley corporate's mandates as hard constraints, innovate within
- **#9 Blue Ocean Strategy** — category reframing (Ashley franchisee → regional retail intelligence platform)
- **#32 Platform Thinking** — 30-store footprint as a data + testing + format platform
- **#41 Second-Order Thinking** — what happens AFTER Zapsight, AFTER the sleep launch, AFTER the store-of-future
- **#45 Bottleneck Analysis** — TOC-style identification of the constraint step in every ops flow
- **#49 Margin Stack Thinking** — where does each dollar of the ~$2,500 avg ticket go, and what's recoverable
- **#55 Category Design** — define a new retail-ops category where 1915 South can be the pattern
- **#57 Downmarket Disruption** — what a well-run $50M competitor would do differently with today's tooling

## Operator Wisdom Cards Loaded

Five frameworks applied across the ideas:

- **Sam Walton retail doctrine** — single-source-of-truth store-level P&L; know every store's numbers; merchandise as the lever
- **Foran radical simplification** — what if we eliminated every process that didn't directly serve the customer or the order
- **Thorndike capital allocation** — the CEO's real job is deploying retained earnings; 1915 South's 14% EBITDA = $25M annual capital allocation question
- **TOC five focusing steps** — identify, exploit, subordinate, elevate, repeat — applied to GMROI
- **Bezos shareholder letters + PR/FAQ** — launching sleep vertical and store-of-future as press-release-first concepts
