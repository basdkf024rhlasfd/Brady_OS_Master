## PropMatic

### What It Does
AI-powered marketing and leasing platform that helps multifamily properties get discovered and convert renters through both traditional search and AI channels (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini).

### The Problem It Solves (Two Customer Pain Points)
Mark hears two phone calls from prospects:
1. **"My marketing costs are through the roof."** Ad costs are skyrocketing. Organic clicks down 25% across the industry. Google moved from referral engine → answer engine → action engine, and in doing so, made ads more important than ever. Operators pay agencies $3K/month in soft costs per community on top of $4K+ ad spend.
2. **"Search is changing — what's our AI strategy?"** Renters are starting to use ChatGPT, Claude, and agentic search to find apartments. Properties without AI presence are invisible in this new channel.

### The Two Middlemen Being Deleted
1. **Listing aggregators** (Apartments.com, Zillow) — don't add value in the age of agentic search. AI prefers first-party data from the property over third-party scraped data.
2. **Marketing agencies** — the hidden middleman. Agencies charge thousands per community to manage ads, websites, SEO. PropMatic's durable agents do this at a 10th of the cost.

### How It Works — The Durable Agents Model
PropMatic builds persistent, always-on marketing agents that monitor and optimize 24/7:

| Agent | What It Does |
|-------|-------------|
| **Ad Agent** | Monitors ad performance, recommends budget adjustments based on ATR (available-to-rent), creates new campaigns from identified high-impression keywords |
| **Website Agent** | Monitors site performance scores, recommends and implements technical fixes (schema, speed) |
| **SEO & Discoverability Agent** | Optimizes for both traditional search and agentic search (E-E-A-T signals, MCP connectivity) |
| **Landing Page Agent** | Creates landing pages matched to ad campaigns — "if this is what the ad looks like, this is what the landing page should look like" |
| **Reputation Agent** | Monitors and manages online reputation |

**Human-in-the-loop model:** Agents recommend optimizations → human approves before execution. Example: "Hey, we're going to adjust your ad budget because ATR is going from 5% to 8%. Do you approve?" or "I created a new ad with these keywords and this landing page. Want me to run it?"

**Skill file customization:** Every customer starts with intelligently defaulted skill files. As they engage, skill files diverge and customize to each operator's preferences. ("You don't want me to use the word 'tenant'? Okay, I'll use 'resident' in my skill file so I always use resident.")

- **38% reduction in ad costs** for Camden (current client) using automated ad + landing page agent combo
- **Organic search clicks down 25% across multifamily industry** — Google's shift to answer engine is structural, not temporary

- **Current pilot:** 3,000-unit portfolio, 12 properties, operated by 3 third-party asset managers. Total team: owner, money guy, analyst. Everything else outsourced.
- **Enterprise pilots:** 5 large operators running point solution pilots (enterprise won't rip-and-replace their whole stack)
- **Buyer persona:** Owner/operator, VP Marketing, VP Leasing, or third-party property management company

### Positioning
- **Tagline:** "Delete the Middleman. Lower your Cost per Lease."
- **Key framing:** "Your next lease will come from ChatGPT."
- **Go-to-market:** Point solutions for enterprise (less scary than full stack replacement). Full agent suite for small operators (replaces agency entirely).
- **First-party data thesis:** AI will always prefer first-party data. If you own the apartment, AI wants to answer the question from YOU, not Apartments.com. "When someone says 'tell me about Camden Midtown in Atlanta,' it's going to first look to see if Camden can answer the question before Apartments.com."

### Product Capabilities
1. **Conversational Concierge** — AI leasing assistant on property websites. Recommends units, answers questions with visuals, schedules tours, creates prospect profiles
2. **Smart Listing Sites** — AI-generated landing pages optimized for high-intent keywords and SEO/paid search. Compete against aggregators without their budgets
3. **MCP-as-a-Service** — Built on Model Context Protocol. Makes listings discoverable through ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini. Agent-to-agent communication: renter's AI talks to property's AI, no listing site in between
4. **Durable Agent Suite** — The 5 persistent agents described above (ad, website, SEO, landing page, reputation)

### The Chirp Origin Story (Mark's Go-To Narrative)
Mark returns to the Chirp founding story as his template for how to build companies:
> "Don't tell me the solution. Tell me your expected outcomes and the constraints that I need to innovate around."

Chirp was born from sitting with apartment operators for 3 months, hearing: "It has to be the key. We need to stop issuing keys and fobs. It can't be Wi-Fi based. Has to be easy to retrofit. Don't design something where we have to break into sheetrock."

Result: Bluetooth-only solution, easily retrofitted, pennies relative to competitors. Everyone else was Wi-Fi or Z-wave.

**This narrative pattern — constraint-driven innovation — is one of Mark's most powerful content formats.**
