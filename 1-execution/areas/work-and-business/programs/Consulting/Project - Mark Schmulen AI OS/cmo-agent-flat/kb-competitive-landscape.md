# Competitive Landscape

**Status:** Pre-researched from public sources (2026-03-31) — NEEDS MARK'S VALIDATION

## PropMatic

### Direct Competitors
- **Listing aggregators (the incumbents):** Apartments.com (CoStar), Zillow Rentals, Rent.com — these are the "middlemen" PropMatic positions against. They charge rising fees and own the renter relationship
- **Knock CRM / Funnel Leasing** — AI-powered leasing platforms with CRM components
- **Hyly.ai (now RealPage)** — AI marketing/leasing assistant for multifamily
- **EliseAI** — AI-powered communications platform for multifamily (text, chat, voice)
- **Remarkably** — AI content generation for multifamily marketing

### Indirect Competitors
- **Yardi / RealPage** — legacy property management platforms expanding into AI. Mark called out from the industry: "We owe the multifamily industry an apology... we blamed the 'Old Guard' for multifamily tech being broken" (post he liked)
- **ILS platforms broadly** — any Internet Listing Service that sits between property and renter
- **Generic AI tools** — property managers using ChatGPT directly for leasing tasks without a structured platform

### What competitors do well:
- Aggregators have massive traffic and brand recognition with renters
- EliseAI has strong conversational AI and deep integrations
- ⚠️ ASK MARK for his nuanced view

### What competitors get wrong:
- Aggregators extract margin without providing direct renter relationships back to operators
- Legacy platforms (Yardi, RealPage) are slow to innovate — "Old Guard" framing resonates in the industry
- ⚠️ ASK MARK

### PropMatic's differentiator (the wedge):
- **MCP-as-a-Service** — unique positioning. No one else is making listings natively discoverable through AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini). This is a greenfield category
- **Both worlds:** Competes in traditional search AND positions for AI-first search. Not betting on one future
- **Cost framing:** "Lower your cost per lease" — direct ROI language, not feature-based selling
- ⚠️ VALIDATE WITH MARK
