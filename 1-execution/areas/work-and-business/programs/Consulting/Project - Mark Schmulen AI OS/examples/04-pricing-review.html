Recommendation: Add a Pro Tier + Raise Growth
Growth $799 → $899/mo. The $100 increase is within willingness-to-pay for current customers (see below). Growth is your most popular tier (62% of new deals) and is significantly underpriced vs. competitors.

New "Pro" tier at $1,299/mo fills the gap between Growth and Enterprise. Includes API access, custom workflows, and priority support. Captures the 500-1,500 unit operators who don't need full Enterprise but want more than Growth.

Keep Starter at $299. It's your entry point and conversion driver. Raising it adds friction without meaningful revenue.
