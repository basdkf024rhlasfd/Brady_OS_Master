Landing Page CRO Audit

TARGET: propmatic.com/demo
62
/100
Below benchmark. The page communicates product capability but fails to build trust or create urgency. Biggest gaps are social proof and value prop specificity. Estimated +35% conversion lift achievable with the three recommendations below.
7-Point Analysis
5
/10
Value Prop Clarity
"AI-Powered Marketing" is generic and tells the visitor nothing specific. Every competitor says the same thing. Need to lead with a measurable outcome: vacancy reduction, response time, conversion rate.
3
/10
Social Proof
Zero customer logos, no testimonials, no case studies, no "trusted by X communities" badge. For a B2B SaaS page, this is the single biggest conversion killer. Property managers need peer validation before booking a demo.
7
/10
CTA Strength
"Book a Demo" is clear and action-oriented. But it sits below the fold on desktop and requires scrolling. Add a sticky header CTA or move the primary button above the fold.
8
/10
Page Speed
LCP 1.8s, FID 45ms, CLS 0.02. Solid Core Web Vitals. Images are properly compressed and lazy-loaded. No major performance issues.
6
/10
Mobile Experience
Layout adapts but feature comparison table breaks on screens under 400px. Form fields are too small for thumb input. Demo video doesn't autoplay on mobile (missed engagement window).
4
/10
Trust Signals
No SOC 2 badge, no integration logos (Yardi, RealPage, Entrata), no security language. Property managers handle PII — they need to see compliance before they'll talk to sales.
7
/10
Form Friction
4 fields (name, email, company, portfolio size) is reasonable. Calendar embed for scheduling is good. Consider adding "No credit card required" microcopy to reduce hesitation.
Top 3 Recommendations
01
Add a logo bar + one testimonial above the fold
Place 4-6 customer logos and a single quote with headshot directly beneath the hero. "PropMatic cut our vacancy from 8% to 3% in 90 days." This alone could lift demo requests 20-30%.
High Impact
02
Rewrite the headline to lead with the outcome
Replace "AI-Powered Marketing for Multifamily" with a specific, measurable claim. See before/after below. Specificity builds credibility and filters for qualified visitors.
High Impact
03
Add integration logos + SOC 2 badge to footer area
Show Yardi, RealPage, Entrata, AppFolio logos with "Integrates with your PMS" label. Add SOC 2 Type II and "256-bit encryption" badges. Removes the silent objection around data security.
