Demo Request Page — propmatic.com/demo — Generated Apr 14, 2026
Hypothesis
Changing the hero headline from feature-focused to outcome-focused will increase demo requests by 15%, because multifamily operators care more about vacancy reduction than technology capabilities.
Control (A) — Current
“AI-Powered Marketing Automation for Multifamily”
Feature-focused. Describes what the product is. No outcome signal.

“Fill Vacancies 3x Faster — Without Hiring Another Leasing Agent”
Outcome-focused. Addresses pain (vacancies) + constraint (headcount). Uses proof number.

Risk Assessment
Low Risk
Sample pollution — Cookie-based assignment prevents cross-contamination. Returning visitors locked to original variant.
Low Risk
Seasonality — April is mid-cycle for multifamily leasing. No major industry events during test window.
Medium Risk
Traffic volume — At 267 visitors/day, a 20% traffic dip would extend runtime to 22 days. Monitor weekly.
Medium Risk
Novelty effect — Returning visitors may react differently to new copy. Segment new vs. returning in analysis.
