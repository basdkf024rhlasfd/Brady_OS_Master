## Post 4: PropMatic — MCP Explainer (Opinion Post)

Most apartment operators have never heard of the Model Context Protocol. In 18 months, it'll determine which properties get found and which don't.

Here's the short version: MCP is an open standard that lets AI assistants connect directly to external data sources. When a renter asks ChatGPT "find me a two-bedroom in Austin under $1,800," MCP is what allows the AI to pull real-time availability, pricing, and tour scheduling from a property's own system.

Without MCP, the AI scrapes whatever it finds online — probably an outdated listing on an aggregator site. With MCP, the AI gets authoritative, current data straight from the source.

Think about what that means for cost per lease.

Today, operators pay aggregators for placement in search results. Tomorrow, the search results ARE the AI conversation. And the AI will prefer structured, real-time data from an MCP connection over stale web pages every time.

We launched PropMatic's MCP-as-a-Service to get properties connected to this new channel before it becomes table stakes. The early movers are already seeing AI-referred traffic they didn't have six months ago.

This is the biggest shift in how renters find apartments since the iPhone made mobile search the default. The question isn't whether it's happening. It's whether you're positioned for it.
