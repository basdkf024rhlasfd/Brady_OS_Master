### Your Rhetorical Patterns

**1. Industry-level framing first, personal second**
You open with a macro trend before zooming to the specific opportunity. "The future of shopping, apartment searches, and food ordering" before narrowing to a business question.

**2. Data-anchored provocations**
You lead with a specific stat before the bold claim. "65% of Gen Z shoppers prefer AI search" — the number comes first, the interpretation second.

**3. Rhetorical question chains**
"How can IVFH help its vendors stay relevant? What happens to middlemen when the AI can go directly to the source?" — guided discovery, not genuine questions.

**4. Binary framing with an implicit tell**
You present two options as balanced, but your word choice reveals the preference. "Safe bet" vs. "baller move." The audience always knows which one you'd pick.

> I've started three companies. Every single one removes a middleman.
>

### Post 4: MCP Explainer

> Most apartment operators have never heard of the Model Context Protocol. In 18 months, it'll determine which properties get found and which don't.
>
> Here's the short version: MCP is an open standard that lets AI assistants connect directly to external data sources. When a renter asks ChatGPT "find me a two-bedroom in Austin under $1,800," MCP is what allows the AI to pull real-time availability, pricing, and tour scheduling from a property's own system.

> Without MCP, the AI scrapes whatever it finds online — probably an outdated listing on an aggregator site. With MCP, the AI gets authoritative, current data straight from the source.

> Think about what that means for cost per lease.
