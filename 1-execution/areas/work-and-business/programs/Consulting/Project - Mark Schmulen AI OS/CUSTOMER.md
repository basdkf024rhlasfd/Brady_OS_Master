# Mark Schmulen AI OS — What Success Looks Like

## Who This Is For
Mark Schmulen — Entrepreneur, CEO/Founder at Contour

## The Problem We're Solving

You understand AI agents — your company builds them for your clients. But you haven't built your own. Every day you're opening Gmail, Calendar, Notion, Slack, Stripe, and QuickBooks separately. You repeat context to Claude or ChatGPT every session. You're hiring a marketer to do work that a well-configured CMO agent could handle.

Your current process:
- Using conventional chat with no persistent memory — every session starts from zero
- Context-switching between 6+ tools daily with no central command layer
- Marketing strategy, content, and GTM materials require manual assembly every time
- Financial questions require opening QuickBooks and manually pulling data
- Meeting follow-ups, email drafts, and calendar management are manual
- You know exactly what the solution looks like — you just haven't had the time to build it

## What You Get

A working AI operating system on your machine, configured for your business, connected to your tools, and designed to get smarter every week.

### Executive Assistant Agent
Your daily command center. Connected to Gmail, Calendar, Slack, and Notion. Handles email triage, meeting prep, follow-up drafts, scheduling, and task tracking. You talk to it — it handles the rest.

### CMO Agent
Understands your business, manifesto, GTM strategy, competitive positioning, and brand voice. Produces marketing content, landing page copy, campaign strategies, investor updates, and website revisions — all in your voice, without re-explaining your business every time.

### CFO Agent (Phase 2)
Connected to QuickBooks and your financial data. Handles journal entries, answers investor questions, produces financial summaries, and flags anomalies. You ask a question — it pulls the answer from your actual books.

### The Compounding Layer
Every agent includes a memory system that learns your preferences and corrections over time. The more you use them, the fewer errors they make. By week 3, they anticipate what you need before you ask.

### Skills Library
Your repeatable processes — proposal writing, investor updates, content syndication, client onboarding — packaged as skills that execute the same way every time. Written once, used forever.

## What This Is Not

- Not a platform or SaaS product — it's your personal workspace, on your machine
- Not a replacement for your judgment — the agents draft, you decide
- Not a one-time setup that goes stale — the system is designed to compound
- Not dependent on one harness — the context files work across Claude Code, Cowork, Codex, or whatever you prefer
