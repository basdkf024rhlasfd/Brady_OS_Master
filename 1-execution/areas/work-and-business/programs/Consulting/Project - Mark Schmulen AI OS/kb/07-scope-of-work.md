# Scope of Work — AI Operating System

> Personal AI agent stack for Mark Schmulen — built on your machine, connected to your tools, designed to compound.

## Engagement Summary

Brady builds and delivers a working AI operating system: purpose-built agents connected to your actual tools, with persistent memory that learns your preferences over time. You stop context-switching between six apps and start working through one command layer.

## Deliverables

### Phase 1 — Core Agents

| Deliverable | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Executive Assistant Agent** | Email triage, calendar management, meeting prep, follow-up drafts. Connected to Gmail, Calendar, Slack, Notion. |
| **CMO Agent** | Marketing content, landing pages, GTM materials, investor updates — all in your voice. Connected to your marketing tools. |
| **Skills Library** | Your repeatable processes packaged as reusable skills — proposal writing, investor updates, content syndication, etc. |
| **Memory Layer** | Persistent context that learns your preferences, corrections, and standards over time. |
| **Tool Connections (MCPs)** | Direct integrations to Gmail, Calendar, Slack, Notion, and other tools you use daily. |

### Phase 2 — Optional

| Deliverable | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **CFO Agent** | Connected to QuickBooks and financial data. Journal entries, investor Q&A, financial summaries. |

- **Ongoing (optional):** Monthly maintenance retainer — new skills, context tuning, optimization

## What's Included

- All agent context files, memory structure, and skills — delivered to your machine
- Tool connections (MCPs) configured and tested
- Training session on using, extending, and maintaining your agents
- One week of post-delivery support for adjustments

## What's Not Included
