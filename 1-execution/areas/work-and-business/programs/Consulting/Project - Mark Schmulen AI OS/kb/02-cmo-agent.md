# CMO Agent

> Understands your business, your voice, and your strategy. Produces marketing output without re-explaining context.

## What It Does

The CMO agent is built around your business context — your manifesto, competitive positioning, GTM strategy, brand voice, and target audience. It produces marketing materials that sound like you wrote them, not like AI generated them.

### Content Creation
- **Blog posts and articles** — Long-form content in your voice with your positioning
- **LinkedIn posts** — Short-form content that matches your style and cadence
- **Email campaigns** — Drip sequences, announcements, investor updates
- **Website copy** — Landing pages, product descriptions, feature pages

### Strategy
- **GTM materials** — Go-to-market briefs, positioning docs, competitive messaging
- **Campaign planning** — Channel strategy, messaging hierarchy, content calendars
- **Competitive analysis** — What competitors are saying and how to differentiate

### Investor Relations
- **Investor updates** — Monthly or quarterly updates from your latest metrics
- **Deck content** — Slide copy, talking points, narrative structure
- **Q&A prep** — Anticipated questions with drafted answers

## Context It Knows

After the interview and build phase, your CMO agent will know:

- **Your manifesto** — What you believe, why your company exists, what you're building toward
- **Your competitive positioning** — How you differentiate from alternatives
- **Your GTM strategy** — Target segments, channels, messaging, pricing narrative
- **Your brand voice** — Tone, vocabulary, formatting preferences, what you never say
- **Your target audience** — Who you're talking to, what they care about, how they buy
- **Your content history** — Past posts, articles, and materials that define your style

## Why This Matters Right Now

You're hiring a marketer. A well-configured CMO agent doesn't replace a marketer — but it changes the job description. Instead of hiring someone to produce first drafts, you hire someone to think strategically. The agent handles production; the human handles judgment.

The agent also ensures consistency. Your brand voice doesn't drift because the agent's context files define it precisely. Every piece of content starts from your positioning, not from scratch.
