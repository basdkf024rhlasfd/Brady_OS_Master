### Advisory Partnership — Questions for Mark

In exchange for the complimentary pilot, I'm asking Mark to think with me on a few strategic and technical questions that will shape how I package and deliver AI agents to future clients:

1. **Delivery Vehicle:** What is the best delivery vehicle for packaging and delivering AI agents to clients at scale? Is there a platform, framework, or model that lends itself to repeatability without heavy custom work per engagement?

2. **Infrastructure vs. Value:** I'm currently hosting agent scaffolding on GitHub repos, but I feel I'm spending disproportionate time on infrastructure (repo management, DevOps, environment setup) vs. the actual value — the deterministic logic and agent-level tinkering. How do I rebalance?

3. **Client Collaboration:** How should I handle feedback loops, visual deliverable sharing, and lightweight collaboration — without running into enterprise security or compliance friction for small advisory engagements?

4. **Scalable Architecture:** What's the right technical approach if I want to rinse-and-repeat this CMO agent across multiple clients? What should be templated vs. customized per engagement?

These don't need formal answers — just your thinking. A call, a whiteboard session, or async notes all work.
