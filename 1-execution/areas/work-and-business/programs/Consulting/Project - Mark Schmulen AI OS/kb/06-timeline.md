## What Success Looks Like

### By Week 2 (Phase 1 Delivery)
- Executive assistant handles email triage and meeting prep without opening Gmail or Calendar
- CMO agent produces marketing content that matches your voice without re-explaining context
- You've completed at least one real workflow through each agent

### By Week 3
- Agents are noticeably better than week 1 — corrections have stuck, preferences are learned
- You've identified at least 3 new processes that should become skills
- Daily tool-switching time has dropped measurably

### By Month 2
- The system anticipates what you need before you ask
- Skills library has grown from starter set to 10+ daily-use commands
- The agent is a natural part of your workflow, not something you remember to use

## How We Work Together

1. **Week 1 — Interview & Build:** Brady interviews you to extract business context, preferences, processes, and tool inventory. Builds your AGENTS.md, MEMORY.md, and starter skills. Connects your MCPs.
2. **Week 2 — Deliver & Train:** Working agents delivered. Brady walks you through using them, adding skills, and how memory compounds. You start working through agents daily.
3. **Ongoing (optional):** Monthly check-in to build new skills, tune context files, and optimize the stack. The goal is you maintaining it yourself — the retainer is insurance, not dependency.
