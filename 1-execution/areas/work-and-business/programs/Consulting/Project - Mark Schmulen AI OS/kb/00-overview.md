# Your AI Operating System

> A working AI command layer on your machine — agents that know your business, connect to your tools, remember your preferences, and get smarter every week.

## What This Is

You understand AI agents — your company builds them. But you haven't built your own. This engagement stands up your personal AI OS: three purpose-built agents connected to your actual tools, with persistent memory that compounds over time.

The result: you stop context-switching between six apps and start working through one agent harness that handles the coordination for you.

## What You Get

| Agent | What it does | Connected to |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| **Executive Assistant** | Email triage, calendar management, meeting prep, follow-up drafts | Gmail, Calendar, Slack, Notion |
| **CMO** | Marketing content, landing pages, GTM materials, investor updates — in your voice | Same + marketing tools |
| **CFO** (Phase 2) | Journal entries, investor answers, financial summaries | QuickBooks, Google Sheets |

Plus a **skills library** of your repeatable processes and a **memory layer** that learns your preferences over time.

## How It's Different From Chat

| | Conventional chat | Your AI OS |
|---|---|---|
| Context | Starts from zero every session | Knows your business, voice, preferences |
| Tools | Copy-paste between apps | Connected directly via MCPs |
| Memory | Forgets everything | Corrections stick, preferences compound |
| Processes | Re-explain every time | Packaged as reusable skills |
| Output quality | Generic | Calibrated to your standards over time |
