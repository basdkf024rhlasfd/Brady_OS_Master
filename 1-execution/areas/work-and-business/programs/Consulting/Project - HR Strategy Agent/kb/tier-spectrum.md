# SRP Engagement Spectrum

Four tiers of workforce intelligence engagement. Each tier stands alone. Each tier creates natural pull for the next.

---

## Tier 1: "The Drip" — Free Content

Weekly email cadence — AI-generated, domain-calibrated content delivered to leadership.

**What the client gets:**
- Management best practices tailored to their industry
- Role clarity frameworks and org health self-assessments
- Workforce planning prompts and operational tips
- Lightweight "rate your team on these 5 dimensions" exercises

**What it does for us:**
- Builds trust and demonstrates domain credibility before any engagement
- Warms every prospect in the pipeline at zero marginal cost
- Creates awareness of what a real workforce intelligence system could surface

**The transition to Tier 2:** "You liked the content — want to see your own data?"

---

## Tier 2: "The Mirror" — Self-Service Assessment

No interviews. No consultants. Pure digital. Deploy surveys and AI-guided questionnaires — employees respond on their own time, the system generates deliverables on a set cadence.

**What the client gets:**
- Structured surveys capturing role reality, time allocation, skills, workflows
- AI-conducted async follow-up conversations that probe deeper based on responses
- Automated deliverables:
  - Week 2: Current-state org snapshot — role clusters, title-vs-reality gaps
  - Week 4: Skills & capability heat map — depth by team and function
  - Week 6: SOP drafts + gap analysis — what people do vs. what the org needs
  - Week 8: Full assessment with recommendations
- Leadership dashboard with real-time roll-up
- All assessments calibrated to the client's specific industry context

**What it does for us:**
- Scalable and repeatable — the AI does the work
- Highest margin tier
- Deploys to the entire org immediately, not 3–5 pilot roles
- Zero disruption to client operations

**The transition to Tier 3:** "The surveys showed gaps — want to understand why?"

---

## Tier 3: "The Deep Dive" — Expert-Led Discovery

Everything in Tier 2 + human interviews + financials + strategic analysis. Surveys serve as the intake layer; a trained interviewer follows the thread.

**What the client gets (beyond Tier 2):**
- Expert interviewer conducts structured follow-ups targeting:
  - Tribal knowledge and exception handling
  - Cross-functional handoffs and dependencies
  - "Where does it break?" conversations surveys can't capture
  - Sensitive topics: role fit, team dynamics, bottlenecks, political landmines
- Financial integration:
  - Comp data mapped against capability (not just title)
  - Time allocation analysis — admin vs. strategic vs. technical work
  - Cost-of-role vs. value-of-role framing
- Expert-in-the-Loop validation:
  - All AI-generated outputs reviewed before delivery
  - Political sensitivities flagged
  - Recommendations calibrated to org reality
- Richer deliverables:
  - Full SOPs with exception handling documented
  - Ownership matrix and knowledge graph
  - Comp benchmarking report (leadership-only)
  - Role-fit analysis with recommendations

**What it does for us:**
- Higher-touch, higher-value engagement
- Domain advantage is undeniable — generic tools can't do this
- Creates the depth of insight that sells Tier 4

**The transition to Tier 4:** "You have the diagnosis — want to operationalize it?"

---

## Tier 4: "The Operating System" — Embedded Implementation

Everything in Tier 3 + systems integration + ongoing operation. Not a project — a permanent organizational capability.

**What the client gets (beyond Tier 3):**
- Dedicated implementation project with defined build phases
- Systems integration:
  - ERP connectivity
  - Item master / operational platform integration
  - HR systems (comp, performance, org structure)
  - Communication tools (Slack/Teams) for routing and knowledge access
- Intelligent routing system (live):
  - Employee-facing: "Who do I go to for this?" — real-time answers
  - Leadership-facing: bottleneck detection, expertise concentration alerts
- Continuous monitoring:
  - Quarterly re-interviews (AI-conducted, expert-reviewed)
  - Automated SOP updates when processes change
  - Skills inventory refreshes
  - Environmental scanning — regulatory, labor market, industry shifts
- CEO dashboard:
  - Where to invest, cut, automate, outsource
  - Performance visibility tied to KPIs
  - Workforce planning scenarios

**What it does for us:**
- Recurring revenue and stickiness
- The reference implementation that sells everything else

---

## The Ladder

```
Tier 1 → Tier 2:  "You liked the content — want to see your own data?"
Tier 2 → Tier 3:  "The surveys showed gaps — want to understand why?"
Tier 3 → Tier 4:  "You have the diagnosis — want to operationalize it?"
```

Each tier is a standalone value prop. Nobody has to climb. But each tier's output naturally raises questions that the next tier answers.
