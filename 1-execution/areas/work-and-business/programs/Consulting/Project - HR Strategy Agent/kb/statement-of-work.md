## Engagement Overview

Brady Smallwood provides AI-powered workforce intelligence services to executives who need to see their organization as it actually operates — not as the org chart says it does. This Statement of Work covers a complimentary pilot engagement to deploy an HR Strategy Agent for IVFH — a self-service workforce assessment system that captures role reality, maps capabilities, generates SOPs, and surfaces organizational gaps through structured surveys and AI-guided interviews.

No consultants in your conference rooms. No disruption to daily operations. Your people respond to structured assessments on their own time. The system does the analysis. You get a leadership dashboard.

This pilot serves two purposes: (1) deliver immediate workforce intelligence to Gary by deploying the assessment system across IVFH's ~70-person organization, and (2) validate the SRP (Strategic Resource Planning) delivery model as a reference implementation.

---

## How It Works

1. **Structured surveys** deploy to employees — role reality, time allocation, skills self-assessment, workflow capture
2. **AI-guided follow-ups** probe deeper based on individual responses — async, chat-based, no scheduling required
3. **The system generates deliverables** on a set cadence — org maps, skills heat maps, SOP drafts, gap analysis
4. **Gary gets a leadership dashboard** with real-time roll-up of organizational intelligence

All survey questions and skills assessments are calibrated to IVFH's specific industry context — specialty food distribution workflows, broker network dynamics, multi-platform retail complexity, item setup processes, pricing logic. Not generic HR frameworks.

## Engagement Tiers

This engagement is a **Tier 2** deployment. For context on where it sits:

| Tier | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **1 — Content Drip** | Free weekly content — training, org health tips, management frameworks |
| **2 — Self-Service Assessment** | **This engagement.** Surveys + AI interviews, automated deliverables, leadership dashboard |
| **3 — Expert-Led Discovery** | Tier 2 + human interviewer follow-ups, financials, comp benchmarking, expert validation |
| **4 — Embedded Implementation** | Tier 3 + systems integration, live routing, continuous monitoring, dedicated build |

Tier 2 is designed to stand alone. If the data reveals questions that require deeper investigation, Tier 3 is available — but nothing about this engagement assumes or requires it.

## What Brady Needs From Gary

To deploy the system and maintain forward momentum:
