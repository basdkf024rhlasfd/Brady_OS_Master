# IVFH HR Strategy Agent — What Success Looks Like

## Who This Is For
Gary Schubert — CEO, IVFH (International Foodsource Holdings)

## The Problem We're Solving

You're running a ~70-person specialty food distribution company where roles are built around people, not needs. Too much knowledge lives in people's heads. When someone leaves, the replacement discovers processes by trial and error. You can't answer basic questions — who actually does what, what capabilities exist across the org, who owns which problems — without asking around.

Your current reality:
- Roles exist because of who was hired, not what the company needs — title says one thing, actual work says something different
- Tribal knowledge is the biggest risk — too much institutional memory walks out the door with turnover
- Problem routing is broken — a "pricing issue" could involve four different people depending on context, and nobody knows who owns it
- Compensation decisions are disconnected from actual capability and market value
- The org structure doesn't match where the business is headed — ERP rebuild, digital channel expansion, and AI strategy all change what the org needs to look like
- No structured way to capture how work is actually performed, where bottlenecks form, or where automation makes sense

Your organizing principle: *"I should not be hiring for people. I should be hiring for roles. Those roles should be defined by what the company needs to achieve, not by the preferences, limitations, or historical habits of the people currently in them."*

## What You Get

A living system — an AI-powered HR Strategy Agent — that assesses your workforce, maps capabilities, captures organizational knowledge, and supports continuous improvement. Not a one-time audit. Not a generic framework. A system calibrated to how a specialty food distributor actually operates.

### Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1–6)

**Role Architecture & Org Mapping**
Clear picture of who works here, what role they're in, and what they actually do. Current-state org map with role clarity assessment. The gap between titles and actual work made visible.

**Work Intelligence & SOP Capture**
Structured interviews that go deeper than "I enter data." Captures decisions, handoffs, systems touched, time allocation. Generates SOPs, process maps, and handoff documentation. Preserves the tribal knowledge that currently lives only in people's heads.

**Skills & Capability Inventory**
Industry-specific skills assessment — not generic HR categories, but the capabilities that actually matter at IVFH: item setup proficiency across retailer platforms, pricing logic, broker network fluency, ERP competency, digital channel operations, food safety/compliance knowledge.

**Phase 1 Deliverables:**
- AI-powered interview capture system (deployed)
- Draft SOPs for 3–5 pilot roles
- Current-state org map with role clarity assessment
- Skills and capability inventory (initial)
- Validated interview framework for full org rollout

### Phase 2: Deeper Org Design (Reassess at Week 6)

Scope depends on what Phase 1 reveals. Likely candidates:

- **Compensation & Role-Fit Benchmarking** — Capability-adjusted comp benchmarking against external market data. Leadership-only deliverable.
- **Knowledge Graph & Issue Routing** — Ownership mapping, expert identification, bottleneck analysis. Employee-facing navigation + leadership analytics.
- **Workforce Optimization** — Where to invest, where to cut, where to automate, where to outsource. CEO's dashboard tied to KPIs and cash generation.
- **Target-State Org Architecture** — Desired-state and 3-year future-state org design informed by ERP rebuild, digital expansion, and AI strategy.

## How We Measure Success

These are the outcomes that matter — from your chair, not ours.

### 1. Role Clarity
**Before:** Roles defined by who was hired, not what the company needs. Title-vs-reality gaps everywhere.
**After:** Clear org map showing actual work, not just titles. Roles defined by business need.

### 2. Knowledge Capture
**Before:** Tribal knowledge lives in people's heads. When someone leaves, processes are rediscovered.
**After:** SOPs, process maps, and handoff documentation that preserve how work actually gets done — including the workarounds and exception handling.

### 3. Capability Visibility
**Before:** No structured view of what skills exist across the org or where the gaps are.
**After:** Industry-specific skills inventory benchmarked against role requirements. Clear picture of build, buy, or develop decisions.

### 4. Informed Decisions
**Before:** Workforce decisions based on institutional memory and gut feel.
**After:** Data-informed answers to "who owns this?", "what happens if this person leaves?", "are we structured for where we're going?"

## What This Is Not

- Not surveillance — understanding workflow reality, not monitoring employees
- Not a replacement for human judgment — the AI captures data and surfaces patterns, people make the decisions
- Not generic HR — every framework, interview question, and skills category is calibrated to specialty food distribution
- Not a one-time audit — the system is designed to learn and improve continuously
- Not based on board materials — Brady contributes general industry expertise and information you share directly as CEO
