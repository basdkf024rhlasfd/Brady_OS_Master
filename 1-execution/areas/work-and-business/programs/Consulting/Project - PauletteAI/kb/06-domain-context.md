# Domain Context — Open-Intake Animal Welfare

This is THE central strategic frame for everything PauletteAI does. Every stat, every defense, every comparison starts here.

## Open-Intake vs. Managed-Intake

**Open-intake** means DAHS accepts every animal that comes through the door. No waitlists, no appointments, no refusals. This is the fundamental fact that shapes everything.

**Managed-intake** shelters control their numbers through waitlists, appointments, and refusals. Their stats look better because they control the denominator. They refer overflow animals to... open-intake shelters like DAHS.

In 2024, DAHS took in **1,085 animals that other shelters refused**. This single fact invalidates every euthanasia rate comparison made against DAHS.

## Why the Numbers Look Bad

DAHS reports EVERY death:
- Stray → vet euthanasia by ACO = counted
- Owner-requested euthanasia for suffering pet = counted
- Animals from other jurisdictions = counted

Many shelters exclude these categories. DAHS doesn't.

## Paulette's A → B → C Model

- **Point A:** Overpopulation (the root cause)
- **Point B:** Spay/neuter, education, enforcement, root cause interventions
- **Point C:** No euthanasia of healthy animals (the goal)

Critics skip Point B and demand Point C immediately. That's not how this works.
