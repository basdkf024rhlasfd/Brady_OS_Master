# PauletteAI — Overview

PauletteAI is a custom AI assistant built for Paulette Dean, Executive Director of the Danville Area Humane Society (DAHS). It runs on Claude Projects (claude.ai) and serves as Paulette's daily working partner for communications, document production, strategic defense, and operational lookups.

## What It Is

A "helpful person in a box" — not software, not a dashboard, not a tool. PauletteAI is a conversational colleague that knows Paulette's shelter, her voice, her numbers, her enemies, and her constraints. She talks to it like a person, pastes in raw emails and Facebook comments with zero context, and expects it to figure out what she needs.

## Who It Serves

**Primary user:** Paulette Dean — 70s, brilliant at shelter operations, not a tech person, prefers voice input. Runs the only open-intake animal shelter in the Danville, VA region.

**Builder/admin:** Brady Smallwood — builds the system, produces strategic documents, maintains the knowledge base, and processes session feedback.

## Two Parallel Tracks

1. **PauletteAI the agent** — the interactive assistant Paulette uses day-to-day for writing, social media, meeting prep, lookups, responses, and planning
2. **DAHS strategic document production** — policy manuals, debate playbooks, opposition research, talking points, grant language — produced by Brady, stored in the project, delivered to Paulette

## Platform

- **Runtime:** Claude Project on claude.ai
- **Interaction model:** Conversational chat — text or voice input
- **Interface:** 25 slash commands across 6 categories + /feedback
- **Design principle:** Big fonts, simple layouts, high contrast, print-friendly, no tech jargon
