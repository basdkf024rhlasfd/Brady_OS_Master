- **Restaurant design** — New store prototypes, remodel programs, interior/exterior design
- **Real estate** — Site selection, lease negotiation, market planning
- **Construction** — New builds, remodels, project management at scale
- **Facilities management** — Maintenance, energy management, smart building tech across ~2,500+ US locations

### Known Initiatives Under His Watch
1. **Panda Home prototype** — New store design debuted in Dripping Springs, TX (May 2023). Features cultural heritage design language, expanded BOH, 15% smaller dining room, enhanced digital pickup areas, immersive drive-thru pictograms.
2. **GridPoint smart building deployment** — Announced Jan 2024. Deploying IoT energy management across all ~2,500 US locations within 18 months. Based on successful 200-store pilot. Controls HVAC/lighting, provides real-time performance data.
3. **Drive-thru expansion** — 75% of recent new builds include drive-thru, pivoting from Panda's historically mall-centric footprint.
4. **Format rollout** — North Platte, NE became first city with the updated restaurant design as of March 2026.
5. **Tea Bar integration** — Physical design for in-restaurant tea bar concept (39 locations and growing).

### What CDO Means at a Restaurant Company
CDO is NOT a digital/tech role. At a restaurant group, Chief Development Officer owns the **physical plant**:
- How many stores get built per year (pipeline)
- What those stores look like (format/prototype)
- Where they go (real estate/site selection)
- How much they cost to build (construction management)
- How they're maintained (facilities/smart building)
- How the box evolves over time (remodel programs)

This is a capital-intensive, operationally complex role — James likely oversees hundreds of millions in annual capital expenditure.
