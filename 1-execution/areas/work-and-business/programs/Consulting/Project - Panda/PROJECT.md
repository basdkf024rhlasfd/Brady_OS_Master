## Customer Problem

**Who specifically:** James Ku, Chief Development Officer at Panda Restaurant Group. Owns store design, real estate, construction, facilities, and adjacent operational strategy. Connected to Brady via Walmart tenure overlap (Jan 2019–Dec 2020).

**The real brief (surfaced April 16, 2026 call):** James is standing up a new **Operations Innovation team** at Panda. It's a centralization play — innovation currently lives in pockets across the org and lacks a unified mandate. He's looking for a short-term consulting leader who can help:
- Define the function's mandate, scope, and operating model
- Identify where binding constraints sit across Panda ops (cost structure, process inefficiency, non-value-added work, labor efficiency)
- Benchmark peer Ops Innovation functions — Walmart, Starbucks, McDonald's named explicitly
- Surface the problems worth solving before committing build cycles
- Possibly help scope the permanent leader hire (Brady offered; James didn't decline)

**Specific pain points James raised:** Order accuracy on digital orders. Labor efficiency via automation / layout / process redesign. Cross-functional change velocity.

**Current workaround:** Innovation is decentralized. Teams solve local problems without shared prioritization, shared taxonomy, or shared accountability to enterprise KPIs.

**Cost of status quo:** With ~2,800 locations, $6.5B+ system sales, 42% digital mix, and ~123 new stores planned for 2025, every month without a centralized Ops Innovation function is compounding cost drag and missed throughput. Peer chains (CFA, Chipotle, Taco Bell, Sweetgreen) are pulling ahead on format and kitchen innovation while Panda's program remains fragmented.

- James's April 18 response: Esmeralda scheduling a longer session "next week"

- **Victory Condition:** Panda stands up an Ops Innovation team with a sharp, pre-validated problem portfolio — not a list of pet projects — aligned to enterprise KPIs that the Cherng family and executive team care about.

  - KR1: 15-25 validated problem statements passing the 5-criteria sharpness gate, tied to measurable KPIs
  - KR2: Clear operating model proposal for the Ops Innovation team (mandate, scope, reporting line, staffing, sequencing)
  - KR3: Peer benchmark — how Walmart, Starbucks, McDonald's, CFA, Chipotle structure their equivalent functions, with defensible recommendations

### Panda Scoreboard (KPIs the work will be scored against)
- **Cost structure:** SG&A as % of revenue, labor as % of revenue, cost per transaction
- **Throughput:** Orders per hour (dine-in, drive-thru, digital), kitchen cycle time, speed of service
- **Digital fulfillment quality:** Order accuracy rate (online orders specifically), on-time ready rate
- **Labor productivity:** Sales per labor hour, transactions per labor hour
- **Guest experience:** NPS, complaint rate, repeat visit rate
- **Growth economics:** AUV, same-store sales growth, digital mix %
- **Build economics:** Cost per new store, time from site selection to open, remodel ROI

### Internal Goals (Brady's goals)
- **Victory Condition:** Convert James Ku reconnection → scoped short-term consulting engagement leading Panda's Ops Innovation team stand-up. Prove Brady can run a research-first, operator-grade diagnostic at a major QSR — becoming the reference story for the consulting practice.
- **Key Results:**
