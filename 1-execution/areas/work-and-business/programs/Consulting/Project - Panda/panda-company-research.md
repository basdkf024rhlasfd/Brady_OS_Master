# Panda Restaurant Group — Company Deep Research

**Date:** April 15, 2026 | **Depth:** Standard | **Sources Analyzed:** 25+ | **Research Type:** Company

---

## Executive Summary

Panda Restaurant Group (PRG) is the largest Asian-concept QSR chain in the United States, with ~2,800 locations and estimated system-wide sales approaching $6.5–7B. The company is private, family-owned by founders Andrew and Peggy Cherng, and has no plans to go public. PRG is in a period of aggressive expansion (123 new company-owned outlets planned for FY2025), format innovation ("Panda Home" prototype), and technology investment (GridPoint smart buildings, automated woks, kitchen display systems). The recent CEO departure (role previously held by external hire) leaves the Cherng family reasserting direct control. Digital sales now account for ~42% of total revenue — a massive channel shift that is reshaping the box.

### Brand Portfolio Detail

- **Panda Express** — The flagship. ~2,500+ US locations. American Chinese fast food. Steam table / made-to-order format with signature Orange Chicken. Drive-thru expanding rapidly.
- **Panda Inn** — Full-service Chinese restaurant. ~5 locations in Southern California. The original concept that predates Panda Express.
- **Hibachi-San** — Japanese teppanyaki concept. Very limited presence, primarily in malls.
- **Panda Tea Bar** — Emerging concept-within-a-concept. Bubble tea, artisanal tea infusions, pastries. Expanded to 39 locations by 2025. Designed by a Taiwanese tea master. Being tested as an add-on to Panda Express locations.

## 4. Store Footprint & Growth

### Location Types
- **Traditional freestanding** — Majority of new builds. Drive-thru becoming standard.
- **Inline / strip center** — Historically the dominant format (mall food court + strip center).
- **Non-traditional** — Airports, universities, military bases, hospitals, stadiums, theme parks. ~170 licensed locations (not company-operated).
- **Drive-thru penetration** — 75% of recent new builds include drive-thru. This is a major strategic shift from Panda's mall-heavy heritage.

### International Presence
- **Mexico** — Largest international market (~24 locations). First opened 2011 in Mexico City.
- **Canada** — Small presence, Calgary and Ontario.
- **Guatemala, El Salvador** — Central American expansion.
- **Japan, South Korea** — Asian markets.
- **Saudi Arabia, UAE** — Middle East presence.
- **Philippines** — Southeast Asian market.

### Growth Strategy
- **Company-operated model:** All units are company-operated except ~170 nontraditional licensed locations. No franchising. This gives PRG complete control over store design, operations, and brand execution.
- **Suburban drive-thru pivot:** Historically mall-centric, now aggressively building freestanding drive-thru locations in suburban markets.
- **International cautious:** International expansion is deliberate, not rapid. Most growth remains domestic.

## 5. Digital & Technology

### Ordering & Digital
- **Digital sales:** ~42% of revenue by early 2026 — a transformative channel shift
- **Mobile app:** Panda Express app for mobile ordering, pickup
- **Delivery partnerships:** DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub
- **Kitchen Display Systems (KDS):** Rolling out across system — digital screens replacing paper tickets
- **Digital pickup screens:** In-store screens for guests to track orders

### Kitchen Technology
- **Automated woks:** Deployed to improve consistency and speed
- **Clamshell grills:** Faster-cooking equipment for protein items
- **Innovation Kitchen:** Testing ground in Pasadena, CA — "living laboratory" per founders. Tests new menu items, formats, and equipment.

### Smart Building / Facilities
- **GridPoint partnership (Jan 2024):** Deploying smart building technology across all ~2,500 US locations over 18 months
  - Controls HVAC and lighting automatically
  - Real-time operational and environmental performance data
  - Based on 200-store pilot showing measurable energy cost savings and CO2 reductions
  - Restaurants use 5–7x more energy per square foot than other commercial buildings
  - **James Ku's domain** — this falls directly under the CDO's purview

## 6. Menu & Product Strategy

- **Core:** Orange Chicken (accounts for ~30% of all orders), Beijing Beef, Kung Pao Chicken, Chow Mein, Fried Rice
- **Innovation:** Beyond Orange Chicken (plant-based collab with Beyond Meat), regional LTOs, seasonal items
- **Tea Bar expansion:** 39 locations with specialized bubble tea, artisanal infusions
- **Pricing:** Average check ~$14.50, positioned as affordable but premium to McDonald's/Taco Bell
- **Philosophy:** "No best. Only better." — continuous small improvements rather than dramatic pivots

## 7. Competitive Position

### Direct Asian QSR Competitors
| Chain | US Locations | Sales | Notes |
|-------|-------------|-------|-------|
| **Panda Express** | ~2,500+ | ~$6.5B | Dominant. No close second. |
| **Teriyaki Madness** | ~150 | ~$200M (est.) | Franchise model. Teriyaki bowls. |
| **Yoshinoya** | ~90 (US) | ~$100M (est.) | Japanese. Shrinking US footprint. |
| **Bonchon** | ~100+ (US) | ~$150M (est.) | Korean fried chicken. Growing. |
| **Waba Grill** | ~200 | ~$150M (est.) | Southern California focus. |

**Key insight:** Panda has no real direct competitor at scale. The competitive set is more meaningfully Chipotle (format comp), Chick-fil-A (operational comp), and Wingstop (growth comp) than any other Asian concept.

### Format Competitors (QSR broadly)
- **Chipotle:** $9.4B+ sales, Chipotlane digital drive-thru in 80% of stores by 2026
- **Chick-fil-A:** $21B+ sales, elevated drive-thru prototype with 4 lanes
- **Wingstop:** Fastest-growing QSR, digital-first, small footprint
- **Sweetgreen:** Infinite Kitchen robotic makeline, automation pioneer
