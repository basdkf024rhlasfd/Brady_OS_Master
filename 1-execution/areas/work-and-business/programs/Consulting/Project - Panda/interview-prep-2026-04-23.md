## Brady's 90-Day POV (lead with this if asked how you'd start)

**Days 1–30 — Listening tour, not a strategy deck.**
- Field ride-alongs in 4–6 markets (flagship + struggling + recent remodel)
- Interviews: ~20 GMs, regional leaders, BOH/FOH crew, facilities, supply chain
- Map the existing "innovation" efforts hiding across the org (Cherng Forward Foundation? R&D? Individual regions running pilots?)
- Output: a one-page map of where opportunity concentrates and where the culture will tolerate intervention

**Days 31–60 — Three pilots, not thirty.**
- Pick 3 problems with clear ROI and low political risk. Order accuracy is one candidate. The others get chosen in Phase 1.
- Each pilot gets: a measurement baseline, 2 operators embedded, a weekly cadence, a 60-day go/no-go
- Parallel: stand up the **operating rhythm** — weekly innovation council, monthly steering with Cherng family + James

**Days 61–90 — Institutionalize one win.**
- Whichever pilot shows the strongest signal becomes the template
- Playbook written, rollout plan scoped, first 3 team hires defined by what the winning pilot actually needs
- Hand-off plan: what does a permanent Head of Ops Innovation need to look like after Brady cycles out

**Why this shape:** Family businesses kill Ops Innovation teams by letting them over-scope and over-centralize year 1. The antibodies win. Three pilots, one institutionalized win, one permanent team template — that's a year-1 outcome the Cherngs can see and keep paying for.

## Questions to ask James (in priority order)

1. **Scope and cadence.** "What does 'short-term' look like to you — 3 months? 6? 9? And how many days a week are we talking?"
2. **Engagement form.** "Are you thinking 1099 consulting, W-2 interim, or something else? Any precedent at Panda for a role like this?"

5. **Political geography.** "Who are the three people I need to build trust with fast? And who historically kills ideas that come from outside?"
6. **Budget signals.** "Has this team been budgeted? What's a realistic team size by end of year?"
7. **Cherng family involvement.** "How involved are Andrew and Peggy in team formation? Is Peter Cherng in the picture?"
8. **Existing efforts.** "What innovation is already happening — R&D, Cherng Forward Foundation, regional pilots? What do I need to respect vs. rationalize?"
9. **Heather Haber.** "When should I loop in with Heather? And is she a champion or a gatekeeper?"
10. **What 'good' looks like at 90 days.** "If we ran the tape 90 days from now and you were happy, what did we do?"
