What AI optimizes

Lane Analysis & Carrier Benchmarking

Module 09 — Growth & Hedge

Strategic Optionality Layer

You're in a cost crisis. But cost crises create openings for people who are paying attention.

Resin Hedge — Product Level

PVC-O: Use 15-40% Less Resin Per Meter

Molecularly oriented PVC (PVC-O) uses 15-40% less resin per meter of pipe while delivering higher pressure ratings. When resin is up 41%, a product that uses a third less of it is a direct cost hedge — not a sustainability play. The AI models the capex, time-to-market, and margin improvement at current resin prices.

Margin Expansion

CPVC: Higher Margin, Same Equipment

CPVC (chlorinated PVC) serves hot water, fire sprinkler, and industrial applications. Higher margin than commodity PVC pressure pipe. Some of it runs on the same extrusion lines you already have. The question isn't "should you do this" — it's "what's the volume threshold where it's worth the changeover time?"

Regulatory Tailwind

Recycled Content Positioning

Multiple states are moving toward recycled content mandates for construction materials. Getting ahead of this isn't altruism — it's positioning. If you can use recycled PVC compound at lower cost than virgin resin AND meet upcoming mandates, you've turned a compliance requirement into a cost advantage.

Demand Upside

Electrical Conduit for Data Centers

888 planned US data center projects. $100B construction spending in 2026 alone. Virginia (136 projects under construction) and North Carolina are your backyard. PVC conduit holds 36% market share — US plastic conduit growing 13.9% CAGR. Atkore and Hubbell are expanding capacity to serve this market. Conduit pricing already up 9% from Gulf Coast resin outages + demand. The AI sizes your addressable slice and tracks procurement contacts before RFQs go out.
