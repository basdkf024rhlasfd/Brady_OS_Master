## Part 1: Simplicity — Where Kroger Is Today

Greg, you already know this, so I'll say it fast.

Kroger doesn't have a management system. It has 20 divisions running 20 different versions of the same company. Capital decisions went to committee votes. It took six months to approve a McKinsey engagement. Your TMO was a meeting cadence, not a transformation office — and when Ron killed it, nobody missed it because it wasn't producing decisions.

Your marketplace partners — Instacart, DoorDash, Uber — have a pipeline of ideas they want to execute with you, and your organization can't convert a single one into action.

But you're doing them without a unified operating model — which means every function is being fixed in isolation, on its own timeline, with its own definition of accountability.

The problem isn't strategy. You know what needs to happen. The problem is Kroger has no operating system to execute it through.

I was building the management operating system for a retail merchandising organization.

Some I've gone deep on — marketplace partnerships, AI capability gap, competitive intelligence. Others I've built the governance skeleton and the right domain expert fills the content. The swimlanes are the *what*.

This is the *how* — and it's the piece Kroger has never had. Legacy at Walmart had this. Kroger's TMO was a ghost of it.

**Layer 3 — The accelerator.** This is the part nobody else can offer. I've built and personally operate an AI-native management system — agents that handle recursive learning, competitive intelligence gathering, document production, decision support, and accountability tracking. Not as a concept. As working infrastructure I run my own operation on every day.

The accelerator layer is what turns a 12-month transformation into a 6-month one. It's the difference between "we need to hire a McKinsey team to benchmark our vendor negotiation process" and "the system already has the competitive data, the framework is templated, the OKRs are structured — now your merchant team fills in the specifics and we're running in weeks."
