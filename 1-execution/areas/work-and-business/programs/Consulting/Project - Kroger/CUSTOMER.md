# Kroger Strategic Playbook — What Success Looks Like

## Who This Is For
Greg Foran — CEO, The Kroger Co. (February 2026–present)

## The Problem We're Solving

You're two months into running a $150B grocery company and the inbox is full. A $2.5B Ocado write-down. Five customer fulfillment centers with uncertain futures. An e-commerce operation that grew 20% last year but still bleeds money at the margin. A technology and AI capability gap that widens every quarter as Walmart ships tools like WIBEY across 4,700 stores. An org chart built for the last CEO's priorities, not yours. And a capital allocation question that changes now that the Albertsons deal is dead and the Ocado anchor is off your neck.

You ran Walmart US. You know what a stores-first transformation looks like. What you don't have is a team on the outside that understands both sides — Kroger's internal wiring and Walmart's playbook — and can hand you a sequenced operating plan instead of a 200-slide deck.

## What You Get

A strategic playbook covering the full operating model — written by operators, not consultants. Twelve sections plus appendices, each with concrete recommendations, sequencing, and the trade-offs laid bare.

### Merchandising
Pricing architecture, promotional strategy, assortment rationalization, and private label positioning. Where Kroger is strong, where the gaps are, and what to prioritize in Year 1.

### E-Commerce & Fulfillment
The store-based fulfillment pivot in detail. How to sequence delivery partnerships (DoorDash, Instacart, Uber) for maximum coverage at minimum margin erosion. What $1.5B+ in delivery sales actually requires operationally.

### Ocado Exit Sequencing
CFC-by-CFC disposition strategy for the five remaining sites (Monroe, Dallas, Atlanta, Denver, Detroit). Phoenix go/no-go framework. Financial impact modeling for each scenario. How to extract residual value without signaling weakness to partners.

### AI & Technology Roadmap
Where Kroger stands today vs. Walmart on AI, data infrastructure, and developer tools. Specific capability gaps, hire targets, and technology investments that close the gap on a realistic timeline.

### Org Structure & Talent
Who stays, who goes, what new roles you need. The leadership team through the lens of the transformation you're running, not the one the last CEO was running.

### Portfolio Rationalization
Banner-by-banner assessment. Which banners earn their place, which are capital traps, and what divestiture sequencing looks like.

### Supply Chain & Logistics
Post-Ocado supply chain reconfiguration. Where the fulfillment network needs investment vs. rationalization.

### Retail Media & Alternative Revenue
How to accelerate Kroger Precision Marketing and alternative revenue streams as a profit offset during the transformation.

### Culture & Operating Cadence
How to install an operating rhythm that matches a stores-first transformation. Lessons from the Walmart Legacy model adapted for Kroger's culture.

### Competitive Positioning
Where Kroger sits relative to Walmart, Amazon, Costco, Ahold Delhaize, and the regional players. What's defensible, what's not, and where to place your bets.

### Implementation Roadmap
First 100 days, Year 1 priorities, Years 2-3 horizon. Sequenced action plan with milestones and decision gates — not a vague transformation arc.

### Why Us
What this team brings that no consulting firm can replicate: Kroger insider knowledge, Walmart operating experience, and the political landscape awareness to know which recommendations will actually land.

## How We Measure Success

### 1. Strategic Clarity
**Before:** Multiple consulting decks, internal strategy reviews, and inherited plans — none written by people who've operated inside both Kroger and Walmart.
**After:** A single playbook that sequences the first 12 months with clear priorities, trade-offs acknowledged, and recommendations you can act on Monday morning.

### 2. E-Commerce Profitability Path
**Before:** $400M e-commerce operating profit improvement target with no outside operator perspective on feasibility and sequencing.
**After:** Validated roadmap with delivery partnership economics, CFC disposition timeline, and store-based fulfillment scaling plan that connects to the $400M target.

### 3. AI Capability Gap Closed
**Before:** Knowing Walmart is ahead on AI but lacking a concrete gap analysis with hire targets and technology recommendations.
**After:** Specific roadmap: what to build, what to buy, who to hire, and in what order — written by people who know both sides.

### 4. Ocado Clean Exit
**Before:** Five CFCs in limbo, a Phoenix decision pending, and no outside framework for sequencing the wind-down.
**After:** CFC-by-CFC plan with financial modeling, timeline, and communication strategy that minimizes market signal damage.
