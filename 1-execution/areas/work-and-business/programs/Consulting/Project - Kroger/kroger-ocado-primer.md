## Confidentiality Framework (C/V Scoring)

All sensitive content is scored on two dimensions:

- **C (Confidentiality):** 0–10. How much legal/professional risk if this leaks. 10 = lawsuit territory.
- **V (Verification):** 0–10. How certain we are this is true. 10 = direct public quotes or firsthand experience.

**Routing rules:**
- **Greg-ready:** High V, low C. No scores, no source headers, no editorial notes.
- **Bill-facing:** Same substance, no scores/delta tables/action items.
- **Chad-facing:** Full version with scores, insider deal context, political notes.
- **Verbal-only:** Anything C:7+. Never written down for external audiences.

Brady's firsthand deal knowledge (Uber ~$140M offer, DoorDash Dashmarts, Instacart MFC deployment) scores C:5–6 / V:10. These stay in Chad-facing docs and verbal conversations — not in Greg-facing materials.

## Ocado Short Thesis Summary

**Core thesis:** Negative flywheel — partner departures deter new partners, shrinking the addressable market for Ocado's technology licensing model.

**Validated by:**
- Kroger $2.5B impairment + strategic pivot away
- Sobeys Calgary CFC closure
- FY25 tech revenue guidance miss (~17% below Street)
- Analyst consensus adoption of "negative flywheel" framing
- Stock down ~92% from 2020 peak

**Remaining catalysts to watch:**
- Disposition of 5 remaining Kroger CFCs (Monroe, Dallas, Atlanta, Denver, Detroit)
- Phoenix planned site — does it get built?
- Any new partner announcements (or lack thereof)
- Cash burn trajectory and potential capital raise
- Potential bankruptcy pathway
