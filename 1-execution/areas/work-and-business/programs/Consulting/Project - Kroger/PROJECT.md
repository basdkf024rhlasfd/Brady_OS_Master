## Customer Problem
- **Who specifically:** Greg Foran, CEO of Kroger (took office Feb 9, 2026). Former Walmart US CEO. Stores-first operator inheriting a broken e-commerce strategy, a $2.5B Ocado impairment, an AI capability gap vs. Walmart, and an org structure that needs reshaping.
- **Trigger moment:** Foran is ~2 months into the job. He's already taken the Ocado write-down, pivoted to store-based fulfillment + delivery partnerships, and completed an e-commerce strategic review. He's moving fast but doesn't have an outside operator team that understands both Kroger's internal politics and Walmart's playbook. Bill Bennett (Foran's former colleague at Walmart) initiated direct outreach on March 4, 2026.
- **Current workaround:** Relying on internal strategy teams and legacy consulting relationships. No one on the outside combines Kroger insider knowledge (Brady), Walmart pricing/e-com expertise (Bill), and political landscape awareness (Chad).
- **Cost of status quo:** Foran makes the transformation without the operator playbook. He either moves too slow on Ocado exit sequencing (stranded capital), misses the AI gap (Walmart pulls further ahead), or restructures the org without insider knowledge of who's blocking what. The window for outside influence is the first 6–12 months of his tenure.
- **Evidence:** Bill's direct Foran relationship via Walmart. Brady's 4+ years at Kroger e-commerce with firsthand deal knowledge (Uber, DoorDash, Instacart). Kroger FY25 earnings confirming the strategic pivot. Bill's "What's Broken at Kroger" whitepaper already drafted.

## Competition
- **Direct:** McKinsey, BCG, Bain — traditional strategy firms that Kroger already uses. They lack operator experience and insider knowledge.
- **Indirect:** Foran's own internal team figuring it out without outside help. This is the most likely competitor.
- **Time pressure:** First 6–12 months of a new CEO tenure is the window. After that, Foran's own team solidifies, external advisors get locked in, and the openness to outside perspectives narrows. Bill's outreach is live now.

## Confidentiality Framework

| Audience | Rules |
|----------|-------|

| Bill-facing | Same substance. No scores, delta tables, or action items. |
| Chad-facing | Full version with scores, insider deal context, political notes. |

The playbook compounds as news breaks. Every Kroger/Ocado development (earnings, CFC closures, partnership announcements, leadership changes) gets absorbed into both the pitch and the short thesis. The C/V scoring framework ensures new intel gets properly classified before routing to any audience.
