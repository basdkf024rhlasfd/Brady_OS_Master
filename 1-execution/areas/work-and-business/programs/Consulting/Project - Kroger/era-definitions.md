# Era Definitions

Stock performance eras (WMT vs KR, Jan 2000–present) and Greg Foran career overlay.

---

## WMT / Kroger Stock Eras

Key finding: Kroger is actually slightly ahead — +1,017% vs +991% cumulative return. Essentially neck and neck after 26 years, but the shape of how they got there is wildly different.

### Era 1: Kroger Pulls Ahead (2000–2003)

Both stocks sink post-dot-com, but KR recovers faster. Kroger's grocery-pure-play holds up as a defensive asset while Walmart's supercenter expansion is capital-heavy. By end of 2003, KR is back to ~+6%, WMT is flat.

### Era 2: Walmart's Dead Decade Begins / Kroger Grinds (2004–2011)

Walmart goes nowhere for nearly a decade — still flat to barely positive by 2011. Kroger is choppy but generally holds +20-50% territory. Neither stock is exciting. This is the period where Wall Street basically ignores both names.

### Era 3: Kroger's Supercycle (2012–2016)

The blowout. Kroger goes from ~+40% to +470% by mid-2015. Walmart is stuck at +70%. The gap is enormous — KR is outperforming WMT by 400 percentage points. McMullen era, identical sales streak, private label dominance. Kroger is a Wall Street darling.

### Era 4: The Reversal — Amazon Shock & Ocado Bet (2017–2019)

Kroger gets cut in half — from +470% down to ~+200%. Amazon/Whole Foods panic. Walmart starts climbing as investors credit its e-commerce pivot. The Ocado deal is signed (2018) as Kroger's strategic answer. Gap narrows dramatically.

### Era 5: COVID & the Choppy Middle (2020–2023)

Both benefit from pandemic grocery demand. KR bounces around +300-600%. WMT steadily climbs from +120% to +300%. Kroger still leads, but the trajectory favors Walmart. Albertsons merger announced and then blocked.

### Era 6: Walmart Goes Parabolic / Convergence (2024–Present)

Walmart explodes — from +300% to +991%. AI narrative, advertising, marketplace, Walmart+ all firing. Kroger also runs to +1,017% but from a higher base. They've essentially converged after 26 years of completely different paths.

**The punchline:** A dollar invested in Kroger in Jan 2000 is worth $11.17 today. Walmart, $10.91. Nearly identical — but Kroger took a roller coaster to get there while Walmart took the stairs and then an elevator.

## Greg Foran Career Eras (Stock Overlay)

### Foran Era 1: The Woolworths Apprenticeship (1978–2011)
**→ Stock Eras 1–2: Kroger's Early Lead + Walmart's Dead Decade**

Foran is invisible to both companies during this period. He's spending 33 years at Woolworths AU/NZ, rising to divisional CEO, and getting passed over for the top job at age 48 (~2009). Meanwhile, both KR and WMT are going nowhere — Walmart is dead money for a decade, Kroger is choppy. The rejection at Woolworths is the catalyst that sends him to Bentonville.

**Key insight for the pitch:** Foran was being forged as an operator while both of these companies were drifting. He didn't inherit a playbook — he built one from the ground up across 33 years of store-level execution.

### Foran Era 2: Walmart International / Proving Ground (2011–2014)
**→ Stock Era 2 (tail end): Walmart's Dead Decade**

Foran joins Walmart in 2011, runs China, then Asia. WMT stock is still dead money — hovering around flat to +13% cumulative after 11 years. McMillon handpicks Foran for the US CEO role in July 2014. Kroger is at +47%, about to enter its supercycle. The irony: Foran is being positioned to fix Walmart US at the exact moment Kroger is about to become a Wall Street darling.

**Key insight for the pitch:** Foran arrived at Walmart when the stock was at its most unloved. He was the fix, not the beneficiary of momentum.

### Foran Era 3: Walmart US CEO — The Glory Years (2014–2019)
**→ Stock Eras 3–4: Kroger's Supercycle + The Reversal**

This is the big one. Foran takes over WMT US in mid-2014 with five straight quarters of negative comps. He delivers 20 consecutive quarters of positive comp growth, remodels 500+ stores, raises wages, grows ecommerce 44%.

But here's what the stock chart reveals: **Kroger was massively outperforming Walmart for the first half of Foran's tenure.** KR peaked at 5.08x (Apr 2015) while WMT was at 1.69x. Foran's wage investment announcement in Feb 2015 actually tanked WMT stock 26% that year — while Kroger was ripping.

Then the reversal: Amazon buys Whole Foods (June 2017). KR crashes. WMT starts climbing on ecommerce credibility. By 2019, Foran's WMT is up ~55-60% from his start, while Kroger has given back a third of its supercycle gains and signed the Ocado panic deal.

Foran departs Oct 2019 — outmaneuvered politically by Marc Lore, never in line for the Inc. CEO job. WMT at ~$120 vs ~$75 when he started.

**Key insight for the pitch:** Foran literally ran the playbook that reversed Walmart's trajectory while Kroger was making the strategic mistakes (Ocado, defensive posture) that would haunt it for the next seven years. He watched from inside Walmart as Kroger panicked. Now he's on the other side.

### Foran Era 4: Air New Zealand — The Complicated Homecoming (2020–2025)
**→ Stock Era 5: COVID Through Merger Failure**

Foran is out of retail entirely. Air NZ stock declines every single year of his tenure (-42%, -14%, -22%, -15%). He navigates COVID, returns the airline to profitability, but the cultural friction is severe — five C-suite departures, "grumpy grocer" nickname, hierarchical style clashes with NZ corporate culture.

Meanwhile at Kroger: COVID boom, volatile stock, Albertsons merger announced (2022) and eventually blocked. McMullen forced out over ethics violation (March 2025). KR bouncing between +200% and +500%.

Walmart during this period: steady climb from +100% to +280%, methodically closing the gap.

**Key insight for the pitch:** Foran's Air NZ tenure is the cautionary tale and the setup. The cultural friction — mass exec exodus, command-and-control style, "slightly scary" reputation — is the risk Kroger's board knowingly accepted. They wanted the operator, not the diplomat. The question is whether Kroger's promote-from-within culture and UFCW union environment can absorb his intensity without the same blowback.

### Foran Era 5: Kroger CEO — The Redemption Play (Feb 2026–Present)
**→ Stock Era 6: Convergence**

KR +7.7% on announcement day. The stocks have converged to nearly identical cumulative returns after 26 years. Foran inherited a company that just wrote down $2.5B on Ocado, had its merger blocked, lost its CEO to scandal, and had an 11-month leadership vacuum.

His first earnings call signals: margin compression, store-based fulfillment pivot, delivery partnerships (DoorDash/Instacart/Uber for $1.5B+ in 2026 sales), Legacy-style transformation cadence. He's running the exact Walmart US playbook — lower prices, invest in stores and people, fix operations.

**Key insight for the pitch:** The convergence in the stock chart isn't a coincidence — it's a market bet that Foran can do for Kroger what he did for Walmart. The stocks are saying: *same operator, same playbook, same opportunity.* The difference is Foran now has the AI/data gap to close, the Ocado mess to unwind, and a culture that's never had an external CEO. The pitch document is the roadmap he needs to execute all of it.
