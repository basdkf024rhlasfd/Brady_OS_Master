# Greg Foran — Instagram Introduction Post

**Source:** Instagram (Greg Foran personal account)
**Date:** ~February 2026 (shortly after CEO appointment)
**Format:** Video/text post addressing Kroger associates

## Full Transcript

Hello, everyone. I'm Greg Foran, and I'm truly excited to be here with you. Before we talk about the business, I want to tell you a little about me. I started my career stacking shelves in a supermarket. I've worked in stores and distribution centers and alongside frontline teams for most of my life. Retail is something I learned on the floor, one customer, one aisle, one shift at a time, and that experience shaped how I lead. I know the people closest to our customers understand this business better than anyone. Whether you work in a store, a warehouse, or a manufacturing center, or an office, your work matters, and our jobs are simple. Take good care of our customers and take good care of each other. We need to be fresh, fast, friendly, affordable and personal. In the next few months, you'll see me in the divisions and across our business. I'll be visiting stores, talking to teams, asking questions and listening. I'm not coming with the answers. The best ideas are already here. You can expect me to focus on the fundamentals, great stores, great products, great service. Again, I am so excited to be part of this team. Now let's get to work.

## Strategic Analysis

**What he's signaling:**

1. **"I'm one of you" framing.** Leads with shelf-stacking origin story, not Walmart CEO title. Deliberate move to defuse the outsider narrative in a promote-from-within culture. He knows the first-ever external CEO label is a liability with associates.

2. **"I'm not coming with the answers."** This is the opposite of his Air NZ approach, where five C-suite execs left within months. He either learned from the cultural blowback or he's packaging the same playbook differently. Worth watching whether the listening tour is genuine or performative.

3. **"Fresh, fast, friendly, affordable and personal."** Five words — this is the operating mantra. Maps directly to his Walmart US playbook ("clean, fast, and friendly"). "Affordable" signals the margin compression he announced on the earnings call. "Personal" is the differentiator from Walmart — Kroger's loyalty/data advantage.

4. **"Focus on the fundamentals."** No mention of digital, AI, ecommerce, Ocado, or technology. Pure store-ops messaging. Consistent with his operator identity — but notable given the AI/data gap is Kroger's biggest strategic vulnerability.

5. **"Now let's get to work."** Action bias. No vision statement, no 5-year plan, no corporate mission rewrite. Just execution. This is how he opened at Walmart US too.

**What's missing (and that's the pitch opportunity):**

- Zero mention of technology, data, or digital capabilities
- No reference to competitive threats (Walmart, Amazon, Costco)
- No mention of the Ocado situation or fulfillment strategy
- No forward-looking strategic vision beyond "fundamentals"

This is exactly the gap our pitch fills — he's got the store-ops playbook locked, but he needs the AI/data/competitive intelligence layer that he doesn't talk about publicly because it's not his native language.
