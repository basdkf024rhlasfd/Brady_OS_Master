# Section 11.2: Year 1 Priorities — Sequenced Action Plan with Milestones

## Situation
You've landed the first 100 days. The org has momentum. Your C-suite is in place. Pricing is locked. Fulfillment partners have signed SLAs. Your Chief AI Officer is already in the building. Wall Street sees a different Kroger.

But momentum without strategy is just activity. Year 1 is where the first-100-days decisions compound into operational leverage. Merchandising drives margin. Fulfillment at scale proves store-based economics work. AI moves from pilot to adoption. Your org structure actually delivers on the interdependencies you've created. This is the year you prove the thesis works.

## Complication
Year 1 demands execution rigor that the Kroger org hasn't shown. McKinsey and Bain are writing reports. Walmart is competing on every level. KTD is still a 1,000-person organization that hasn't moved the needle on velocity. Your new executives need to operate together, not in silos. And every quarter, investors ask: "When do we see the margin improvement? When does comparable-store sales stabilize?"

You can't answer those questions with strategy. You answer them with results. The complication is that you have seven domains running in parallel, and one failure cascades across the others. Wrong fulfillment network breaks merchandising profitability. Slow KTD execution means AI doesn't ship. Org structure breaks if people don't have clear accountability. Year 1 is where these become non-negotiable.

## Resolution: Year 1 Roadmap — Three Quarters, Seven Domains

### **Quarter 1 (Jan–Mar FY27): Foundation Locked**

**Objective:** Move from decision to implementation. Build the operating rhythm. Lock the system underneath the strategy.

#### Merchandising
- **Milestone:** Complete category-by-category margin analysis. Identify 5–8 SKUs per category where Kroger is uncompetitive vs. Walmart. Lock permanent price/promo edits.
- **Why:** By Q1 end, every category leader has a clear margin roadmap. Sales teams know what to promote. Customers see sustained competitive pricing, not one-time promos.
- **Owner:** Chief Merchant. Metric: margin improvement % by category.

#### E-Commerce & Fulfillment
- **Milestone:** First 100 stores live on full Instacart pickup network. DoorDash delivery active in top 30 metro areas. Last CFC closure executed.
- **Why:** You're proving the store-pickup + third-party delivery model works at scale. Customers see it. Wall Street can model the unit economics.
- **Owner:** Chief Ecommerce Officer. Metric: pickup adoption rate, delivery GMV, fulfillment cost per unit.

#### AI & Technology
- **Milestone:** SKU-level personalization engine live in Kroger mobile app. Initial A/B testing complete. Early results show 3–5% lift in digital basket size.
- **Why:** First tangible customer-facing AI benefit. Proof point that AI is revenue, not just cost. Associates start seeing LLM tools in Kroger systems.
- **Owner:** Chief AI Officer. Metric: personalization lift %, associate adoption rate.

#### Org Structure
- **Milestone:** All 13 new roles filled (C-suite complete from 100-day mark, six domain VPs hired in Q1). First all-hands with new org chart. Weekly decision cadence locked.
- **Why:** People know who's accountable. Weekly reviews surface blockers in real time, not monthly.
- **Owner:** Chief People Officer. Metric: time-to-hire, onboarding velocity, first 360 feedback.

#### Portfolio & Banners
- **Milestone:** Investment banker engaged. Two banners deep-dived (one for potential sale, one for strategic investment). Decision framework locked.
- **Why:** You're not making moves yet, but the decision process is clear. Employees in reviewed banners have line of sight to the decision.
- **Owner:** CFO. Metric: deal pipeline clarity, stakeholder confidence.

#### Supply Chain
- **Milestone:** DC network redesign approved and executed. Supplier consolidation roadmap locked. Logistics cost targets set by store/region.
- **Why:** Supply chain is now the operational backbone for fulfillment partnerships. Every delivery partner has predictable inbound logistics.
- **Owner:** Chief Supply Chain Officer. Metric: DC utilization, supplier cost reduction, logistics cost per unit.

#### Retail Media
- **Milestone:** Data infrastructure procured (CDP/DMP). KPM brand launched with 50+ first-party advertiser relationships. Revenue target set for FY27 ($100M+).
- **Why:** Retail media is separate P&L, not afterthought. CPG partners see it's real; revenue is visible.
- **Owner:** VP Retail Media. Metric: advertiser count, revenue run rate, brand lift metrics from test campaigns.

### **Quarter 2 (Apr–Jun FY27): Execution at Scale**

**Objective:** Prove the operating model works across multiple domains. Generate early Wall Street wins. Close the feedback loop on Year 1 changes.

#### Merchandising
- **Milestone:** Margin improvement of 20–30 bps vs. prior year (category mix + price realization). Competitive position strengthened in 80%+ of categories vs. Walmart. Promo effectiveness up 10%.
- **Why:** Wall Street sees hard results. Not margin-per-unit theater—actual P&L improvement. Customers see consistent, competitive pricing.
- **Owner:** Chief Merchant. Metric: reported P&L margin %, competitive price position, promotional ROI.

#### E-Commerce & Fulfillment
- **Milestone:** 300+ stores on pickup network. DoorDash in 60+ metros. Fulfillment cost per unit down 8% vs. FY26 average (due to mix shift from CFC to third-party).
- **Why:** Unit economics validate the store-based model. Third-party partnerships are cheaper than your own Ocado CFCs were. Wall Street models path to profitability.
- **Owner:** Chief Ecommerce Officer. Metric: network coverage %, delivery cost trend, ecommerce GMV, fulfillment margin improvement.

#### AI & Technology
- **Milestone:** Supply chain optimization live (AI-driven inbound logistics, demand forecasting by SKU/store). Early results show 5–7% reduction in shrink/waste. 50%+ of associates using at least one AI tool.
- **Why:** B-side AI win (supply chain efficiency) compounds with merchandising gains. Associate adoption shows the organization is moving with you, not against you.
- **Owner:** Chief AI Officer. Metric: shrink reduction %, associate adoption %, supply chain cost reduction.

#### Org Structure
- **Milestone:** First 180-day reviews complete. Weak performers identified and managed out (1–2 direct reports of new executives). A-players promoted or retained with clarity. New talent pool refreshed.
- **Why:** You're proving accountability works. The org sees you move on people, not just strategy.
- **Owner:** Chief People Officer. Metric: turnover (intentional vs. unintended), promotion rate, engagement scores.

#### Portfolio & Banners
- **Milestone:** One banner sold or strategic decision made public (e.g., "QFC is for sale," or "Smith's is core for 5 years"). First tranche of capital returned to shareholders or reinvested in core banners.
- **Why:** You're ruthless on capital. Wall Street sees it. You've freed up capital for next investments.
- **Owner:** CFO. Metric: deal closed or announced, capital return rate, shareholder communication.

#### Supply Chain
- **Milestone:** Supplier consolidation delivered. Top 100 suppliers locked into long-term contracts with cost efficiency targets. DC utilization up 5% across network.
- **Why:** Supplier relationships are stable. Your fulfillment partners have predictable cost structures. Supply chain cost down 100+ bps.
- **Owner:** Chief Supply Chain Officer. Metric: supplier retention, cost reduction %, DC productivity.

#### Retail Media
- **Milestone:** 200+ advertisers. $200M+ revenue run rate. First cohort of CPG-sponsored insights/offers live in Kroger app. Competitive gain vs. Walmart KMP visible.
- **Why:** Retail media is a profit lever, not a side bet. CPG partners are confident in your data and platform. Your competitive gap vs. Walmart KMP is narrowing.
- **Owner:** VP Retail Media. Metric: advertiser GMV, platform engagement, revenue per advertiser.

### **Quarter 3 (Jul–Sep FY27): Consolidation & Momentum**

**Objective:** Lock the wins. Reinvest momentum into Year 2 priorities. Communicate the inflection to Wall Street and the org.

#### Merchandising
- **Milestone:** Merchandising margin sustainable. Category leaders operating on permanent pricing/promo model, not quarter-to-quarter adjustments. Wallet share vs. Walmart stable or improved in 60%+ categories.
- **Why:** Wall Street models margin as structural, not temporary. Investor confidence in pricing power increases.
- **Owner:** Chief Merchant. Metric: YTD margin trend, market share by banner/region, merchant leadership bench strength.

#### E-Commerce & Fulfillment
- **Milestone:** 500+ stores on pickup. Instacart/DoorDash covering 80%+ of Kroger footprint. Digital penetration at 15%+ of total (up from ~6% in FY25). Fulfillment margin positive in profitable markets.
- **Why:** Scale proves the model. Digital is material to total business. Unit economics are transparent and repeatable.
- **Owner:** Chief Ecommerce Officer. Metric: digital penetration %, fulfillment margin by market, customer acquisition cost.

#### AI & Technology
- **Milestone:** Third AI initiative shipping: personalized offers + inventory visibility for customers. KTD velocity improved 20%+ (measured in shipped features, not OKRs). Associates using AI tools: 75%+ across stores.
- **Why:** AI is moving from innovation to operation. Your competitive gap to Walmart is narrowing. KTD is finally delivering.
- **Owner:** Chief AI Officer. Metric: AI-driven revenue/margin impact, KTD velocity, associate productivity improvement.

#### Org Structure
- **Milestone:** Year 1 talent refresh complete. Leadership team operating as a unit (cross-domain decisions happening in weekly rhythm, not cascading through hierarchy). Culture shift visible in engagement surveys.
- **Why:** The org is aligned. Decision-making is faster. Accountability is clear.
- **Owner:** Chief People Officer. Metric: leadership team 360 feedback, cross-functional collaboration score, retention of high-performers.

#### Portfolio & Banners
- **Milestone:** Capital allocation plan for Year 2 locked. 1–2 banners committed to transformation investment (e.g., "Harris Teeter digital refresh," "Smith's supply chain automation"). Shareholder communication clear on long-term banner strategy.
- **Why:** Investors see a multi-year capital plan, not quarter-to-quarter guessing. Employees in banner teams know the investment thesis.
- **Owner:** CFO. Metric: capital allocation transparency, shareholder confidence, banner-specific ROI targets.

#### Supply Chain
- **Milestone:** DC automation roadmap started. 5–10 DCs in pilot mode for automation/AI-driven logistics. Network is now a competitive advantage, not a legacy constraint.
- **Why:** Supply chain becomes a margin driver, not just a cost function. Your fulfillment partners benefit. You benefit.
- **Owner:** Chief Supply Chain Officer. Metric: automation ROI, logistics cost trajectory, supplier performance vs. targets.

#### Retail Media
- **Milestone:** 400+ advertisers. $300M+ revenue run rate. First media company partnerships (e.g., measurement partners, agency integrations). CPG partners benchmarking Kroger KPM against Walmart KMP publicly.
- **Why:** Retail media is a $1B business on the 3-year trajectory. You're no longer defending against Walmart; you're competing on parity or better.
- **Owner:** VP Retail Media. Metric: advertiser growth rate, revenue per advertiser, competitive positioning vs. KMP.

## Operating Cadence: Weekly to Quarterly

**Weekly Operational Reviews (Mondays, 60 min)**
- Attend: You, C-suite (7 execs), Chief of Staff
- Agenda: Blockers from each domain, cross-functional dependencies, customer/Wall Street signals
- Decision right: You move on urgent issues; others manage execution
- Output: Priority adjustments for the week; escalations locked for their owners

**Monthly Deep-Dives (third Thursday, 90 min per domain)**
- One domain per month. Full executive team + direct reports.
- Review metrics, headwinds, competitive response, capital allocation decisions
- You drill into one issue; everyone else brings context
- Output: Quarterly adjustment triggers

**Quarterly Business Reviews (end of each quarter)**
- Full-day session: financial results, market position, competitive moves, people/culture
- Wall Street communication finalized
- Year 1 strategy recalibration (if needed)
- Next quarter priorities locked

**Annual Strategic Review (November)**
- Lock Year 2 priorities by domain
- Budget and capital allocation for FY28
- Talent plan for next year

## Firmly Held Beliefs (Non-Negotiable)

1. **Pricing is a pricing decision, not a marketing fix.** If merchandising isn't hitting margin targets by Q2, raise prices. Don't run more promos.

2. **Fulfillment economics must be positive within 18 months.** If the third-party model doesn't work by end of Year 1, pivot. Sunk costs don't matter.

3. **AI is a revenue lever, not a cost-cutting tool.** If your Chief AI Officer isn't shipping customer-facing initiatives that move margin by Q3, you hired wrong.

4. **Org structure must change with strategy.** If your executive team isn't operating as a unit by end of Year 1, the structure isn't right. Rebuild.

5. **Capital allocation is ruthless.** If a banner or initiative can't defend its capital, divest or sunset it. Portfolio rationalization isn't optional.

---

## Loosely Held Opinions (Adapt as Conditions Change)

- **Supplier consolidation speed:** Targeting aggressive timelines, but if supply disruptions emerge, extend negotiations.
- **DC automation:** Pursuing pilots, but ROI may vary by region. Be ready to accelerate or slow based on results.
- **Retail media partnership models:** Starting with first-party only; third-party partnerships may emerge as opportunity.
- **Banner divestiture speed:** Targeting one decision by end of Q2, but market conditions may accelerate or delay.

## Year 1 Scorecard

By December FY27, you've won if:

1. **Wall Street calls the Kroger story "credible."** No more "will it execute?" Upgrade targets based on margin/digital trajectory.

2. **Comparable-store sales stabilize or improve.** Digital is 15%+. Margin is up 20+ bps. Fulfillment cost trend is favorable.

3. **Your leadership team operates as a unit.** Cross-domain decisions happen in one meeting, not five. People know who owns what.

4. **Employees see the change.** Engagement scores up. Turnover down for A-players. Culture surveys reflect "clarity of direction."

5. **One major capital allocation decision is executed.** Either a banner divested, a strategic investment made, or a major CFC closed and partnerships live.

6. **AI is shipping; KTD is faster.** Customer-facing personalization live. Associate adoption is material. You've narrowed the Walmart gap.
