# Job Search Targeting Script — Claude Desktop

> **How to use**: Paste the prompt below into a new Claude Desktop conversation.
> Work through it conversationally — Claude will guide you through each section.
> At the end, ask Claude to output the final targeting matrix in a clean format
> you can paste back into this project.

---

## Paste This Into Claude Desktop

```
I'm running a structured job search and I need your help building a targeting matrix — which roles, companies, and industries to pursue. I have 7 weeks to land an offer (target: June 1, 2026). Let's work through this step by step. Ask me questions one section at a time — don't dump everything at once.

## About Me (context for you)
- Brady Smallwood, 39, Bentonville AR, single dad of five
- Chicago Booth MBA (2012), CFA charterholder background
- Career arc: Freddie Mac (finance/analytics) → American Capital (PE/lending) → Walmart (Neighborhood Markets, then Pricing & Analytics) → Younique Products (VP Ops, MLM/DTC beauty) → Walmart again (Director, strategic initiatives) → Kroger (Sr Director, digital/analytics) → IVFH (COO + Board Member, food distribution startup) → Independent consulting + entrepreneurship (current, since late 2024)
- Currently: AI consulting practice (mception.ai), building Broker Co (food broker tech), advisory work
- Why searching: need stable income, benefits, and the right operating role. Entrepreneurship isn't scaling fast enough for a family of six.
- Location: strongly prefer NW Arkansas (Walmart ecosystem) or remote. Open to hybrid if compelling.
- LinkedIn network: 2,093 connections, 34% C-Suite/Founders, deep in Walmart (165), Kroger (80), food/CPG (399), tech/AI (358)

## Walk me through these sections one at a time:

### 1. Career Arc Synthesis
Help me find the through-line across my career. What story does this tell? What's the narrative a hiring manager should hear? Push back on me if I'm underselling something.

### 2. What Energizes vs. Drains
For each major role, ask me: what did I love? What drained me? What would I never do again? Use this to build a "role filter" — characteristics that a good role MUST have and MUST NOT have.

### 3. Superpowers Inventory
Based on my career and our conversation, help me name my 3-5 genuine superpowers. Not resume fluff — the things I do better than 95% of people at my level. Be specific and honest.

### 4. Role Archetype Exploration
Based on everything above, propose 4-6 role archetypes that fit me. For each one:
- Title examples
- Why it fits my superpowers
- Why it might not work
- Comp range expectation (NW Arkansas / remote adjusted)
- Example companies that hire for this

Force-rank them by fit, not by prestige or comp.

### 5. Company Filter
Help me build a company filter with these dimensions:
- Size: startup (<50) vs. growth (50-500) vs. mid-market (500-5000) vs. enterprise (5000+)
- Industry: food/CPG, retail, retail tech, AI/ML, logistics, fintech, other
- Stage: early, growth, scaling, turnaround, mature
- Geography: NW Arkansas, remote-first, hybrid (and which cities)
- Culture signals: what to look for, what to avoid

### 6. Comp & Lifestyle
Ask me about:
- Base salary floor (what I need to cover family expenses)
- Total comp expectations (base + bonus + equity)
- Equity appetite (startup equity vs. RSUs vs. cash-heavy)
- Travel tolerance (days/month)
- Schedule flexibility needs (single dad of five)
- Benefits that matter most

### 7. Deal-Breakers
Help me articulate 5-7 hard deal-breakers. Things that would make me walk away no matter how good the comp is.

### 8. Output: Targeting Matrix
After we've worked through everything, produce a clean targeting matrix:
- 3-5 role archetypes (ranked by fit)
- For each: 5-10 specific target companies
- For each company: why it's a fit, who I might know there (reference my LinkedIn concentrations), and what the likely entry point is (apply, referral, recruiter, direct outreach)
- A 1-paragraph "positioning statement" I can use across all outreach
- A prioritized list of 20 people to reach out to first (by archetype)

Format the output so I can paste it directly into my project tracking system.
```
