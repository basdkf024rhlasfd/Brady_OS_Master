# Scenario Matrix: Justin / Ashley / 1915 South

**Date:** 2026-04-21
**Context:** Brady evaluating strategic options with Justin Woods / Russell Turner / 1915 South (Ashley HomeStore franchisee, 29 stores, Southeast US). 4 scenarios scored across time, money, optionality, and fit.

---

## The 2×2

|  | **Keep mception.ai** | **Wind down mception** |
|--|--|--|
| **Full-time at 1915 South** | Scenario A | Scenario B |
| **Advisory / Consulting only** | Scenario C | Scenario D |

---

## Scenario A — Full-Time at 1915 South + Keep mception Running

**Premise:** Brady takes a COO, VP Operations, or Chief Strategy Officer role at 1915 South while mception continues with reduced attention (maybe 2-4 hrs/week).

**Time:** High demand. A full-time ops exec role at a 29-store regional retailer is not a 9-5 — it's travel, store visits, crisis response. mception will coast, not grow. Brady would need to be honest that mception is on autopilot, not active development.

**Money:** Highest near-term income scenario. A C-suite role at a top-50 US furniture retailer should land $175-225K base + performance bonus, possibly with equity consideration given Russell Turner's ownership structure. Resolves the June financial pressure entirely.

**Optionality:** Medium. Creates a strong operational track record in a specific vertical (furniture/retail). Could be a stepping stone to a larger multi-site retail operating role. mception stays alive as optionality if the role doesn't work out. Locks Brady into Thomasville, GA geography (or heavy Southeast travel).

**Fit:** High on paper. Brady's COO background, retail analytics, and AI fluency are exactly what a rapidly-scaling regional furniture franchisee needs. Justin's analytics + strategy pedigree means they won't duplicate each other. Russell Turner would see a complete executive team.

**Risk:** Russell Turner may prefer organic promotion over bringing in a COO-level outsider. Justin joined in 2025 and may want to build his own team. Brady needs to position as complement to Justin, not above him.

**Verdict:** Best financial outcome, highest execution demand, limited mception upside. Only works if Brady can stomach the geography and pace, and if Justin is the champion (not competitor) in the hire.

---

## Scenario B — Full-Time at 1915 South + Wind Down mception

**Premise:** Brady commits fully to 1915 South and puts mception into maintenance/closure. Clean career pivot.

**Time:** Cleanest split. Brady's full attention goes to 1915 South. No context switching, no client management on the side.

**Money:** Same as Scenario A on base. Loses the optionality value of mception (future consulting revenue, equity, brand). If the 1915 South role works for 3-5 years, the financial and career outcome could be significantly better than staying independent — especially if there's an ownership/equity component tied to company performance.

**Optionality:** Lowest. Brady bets entirely on 1915 South. If Russell Turner sells the company, shifts strategy, or the role doesn't work, Brady has no active consulting practice to fall back on. His network decays unless he actively maintains it.

**Fit:** Same as A on day-to-day. The question is whether Brady wants to close the entrepreneurship chapter at this stage or preserve the optionality.

**Risk:** Irreversibility. mception is harder to restart than it is to pause. Brady's AI consulting brand (especially the mception.ai surface) has real optionality value — giving it up for a regional retail role is a meaningful tradeoff.

**Verdict:** Only choose this if the 1915 South role comes with clear equity participation or a defined 3-year path to meaningful ownership/bonus structure. Otherwise, preserving mception costs almost nothing and protects against downside.

## Scenario C — Advisory / Consulting Only + Keep mception

**Premise:** Brady does not take a full-time role. Instead, engages 1915 South (and potentially Ashley Global Retail separately) as a consulting/advisory client. mception grows in parallel.

**Time:** Most flexibility. Brady controls his calendar. Ideal if he can close 1-2 more consulting clients alongside 1915 South to hit income targets.

**Money:** Near-term risk. A single advisory engagement with 1915 South might generate $2-5K/month retainer or a $25-50K project fee. That doesn't resolve the June financial pressure alone. Brady would need to close additional consulting clients simultaneously. Ceiling is high if multiple clients come together; floor is uncertain.

**Optionality:** Highest. mception grows, Brady's AI consulting brand strengthens, and if the 1915 South relationship deepens, it could convert to a part-time operating role or grow into a multi-client advisory practice in furniture/retail.

**Fit:** Justin and Russell would receive Brady's expertise without the commitment risk of a full hire. For a family-owned company that has never had a non-family president before Justin, bringing in a COO-level outsider might feel aggressive. An advisory start is a lower-friction entry.

**Risk:** Brady may not close enough consulting revenue fast enough. June pressure is real. Also: 1915 South may not have a defined consulting budget or engagement model for this type of work — Brady would need to structure it as a project (Innovation Workshop, GMROI diagnostic, AI roadmap) rather than a vague retainer.

**Verdict:** Best optionality, highest near-term income uncertainty. The right play if Brady is confident he can close 2+ engagements by June and wants to preserve mception's trajectory.

---

## Scenario D — Advisory Only + Wind Down mception

**Premise:** Brady does advisory consulting work for 1915 South / Ashley ecosystem without taking a full-time role, and simultaneously winds down mception. This is the low-commitment, low-upside path.

**Time:** Moderate. Advisory work without the back-end infrastructure of mception is lighter, but Brady loses the platform that makes him more credible as an AI consultant.

**Money:** Weakest scenario. Advisory fees without mception's positioning and AI tools are harder to command. Brady would essentially be a freelance ops consultant without the differentiated AI angle.

**Optionality:** Very low. Brady doesn't have the FTE security, doesn't have the mception brand, and doesn't have a growing consulting practice. This is the "stuck in the middle" scenario.

**Fit:** Poor. Nothing about this scenario plays to Brady's strengths.

**Verdict:** Don't do this. Eliminate from consideration.

---

## Recommended Path

**Start with Scenario C, position to convert to Scenario A.**

1. Use the Justin Woods connection to open a conversation about 1915 South's current operational and AI maturity challenges.
2. Propose a structured engagement — either an Innovation Workshop ($25-50K project) or a GMROI Diagnostic — that delivers immediate value and puts Brady in front of Russell Turner.
3. If the chemistry is right and Russell sees the gap, Brady can pitch a structured part-time operating role (Fractional COO / Chief Growth Officer) that preserves mception's flexibility while establishing Brady inside the organization.
4. If 1915 South moves aggressively toward a full-time offer with equity, evaluate Scenario B only if the equity structure is meaningful and the role has a 3-5 year horizon.

**Timeline pressure note:** Given Brady's June target, the Justin/1915 South path should be treated as a 60-90 day opportunity. If a concrete offer or engagement contract isn't in motion by May 15, Brady needs to activate parallel outreach.
