## Settled this session (2026-06-11)

1. **"Wife Rebeccah" resolved.** Tobias Bull I's 1687 will leaves his whole estate to "wife Rebecca" = **Rebecca (Hollowell)** (m. ~1675; later m. William Bunting). Confirmed in MilesFiles (S570) and Carey (Wills & Deeds 1676–1690, p. 458). → add to whitepaper.
2. **Boole branch documented + lighthouse keeper verified.** Nicholas Boole (d. 1823) → David Nottingham Boole (1821–1896, Hog Island Light keeper) confirmed in MilesFiles to the exact date.
3. **Common ancestor documented (not just inferred).** Carey roots the Northampton **Bool/Boole** branch in **Richard Bull (b. ~1686), son of Tobias Bull I** — the fork that separates Jane (via Richard) from Brady (via Tobias II). The cousin connection is now sourced to primary county records, not assumed.
4. **Tobias II/III discrepancy resolved in the whitepaper's favor.** Carey: Daniel Bull (b. ~1724) is a son of **Tobias II** and brother of Tobias III; MilesFiles had mis-filed him a generation low. Brady's line stands. Net cousin estimate: **~8th cousins (likely once removed)**.
