# The Carey "Bulls of Accomack" Report — the Documented Bridge

**Date:** 2026-06-11
**Source:** Mary Frances Carey, *Bulls of Accomack County, Virginia* (Certified Genealogist #237). Saved at `research/source/bullsofaccco-carey-report.pdf` (2.89 MB, retrieved 2026-06-11 via authenticated browser session — the file is Cloudflare-gated to non-browser clients). Companion reports also saved: `bullbagwell-carey-report.pdf` (3.37 MB), `bullsofmessongocreek-carey-report.pdf` (0.78 MB).
**Why this matters:** Carey supplies the two things the MilesFiles database did **not**: (1) a documented connection from the Northampton **Bool/Boole** branch up into the Tobias Bull trunk, and (2) a resolution of the Tobias II-vs-III generation discrepancy on Brady's own line. Both are sourced to primary Accomack/Northampton county records.

---

## 1. The common ancestor is now documented: Tobias Bull I (b. ~1648, d. 1687)

Carey establishes Tobias Bull (the immigrant) from primary records:
- Born **circa 1648**; wife **Rebecca born circa 1660**; living at **Wallops Neck**, Accomack, in the employ of Thomas Welburne of Horntown; involved in the Chincoteague Island ownership dispute (Whitelaw, p. 1377).
- **Will written 29 Apr 1687, recorded 16 Nov 1687** (Wills & Deeds 1676–1690, p. 458): *"all my worldly goods and whole estate after my just debts are paid to my Loving wife Rebecca,"* made her executrix; witnessed by John Wallop, Nathaniel Tunnell, Thomas Conaway; signed with his mark.
- Owned **no land**; widow (only ~27 at his death) likely moved to central Accomack to raise the family.
- A deed of **11 May 1686** (Wills & Deeds 1676–1690, p. 423) proves at least one son: Richard Hastings gave "to Richard Bull, son of Tobias Bull and his wife Rebecca," a brindle cow — fixing son Richard's existence by 1686.

This is the same will and wife ("Rebeccah" = Rebecca) confirmed independently in MilesFiles (see `01-milesfiles-findings-2026-06-11.md` §3). Carey supplies the primary citation.

## 2. Tobias Bull I's children — the fork that separates Brady and Jane

Carey reconstructs Tobias I + Rebecca's children (they married ~1683):

| # | Child | Fate | Line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Benjamin Bull (b. ~1684?) | Witnessed Thomas Hope's will 1732; no other record | — |
| 2 | **Richard Bull (b. ~1686)** | Died intestate **Northampton Co. by 15 Jan 1728**; widow **Esther Booll** administered estate (North. Co. Orders 1722–1728, p. 362) | **→ the Northampton BOOL/BOOLE branch → Jane Badger** |
| 3 | **Tobias Bull II (b. 1687–88)** | "See next section" — m. Frances; central Accomack | **→ the Accomack BULL branch → Brady** |

**This is the fork.** Brady descends from Tobias I through son **Tobias II**; Jane descends from Tobias I through son **Richard (b. ~1686)**. The common ancestor — **Tobias Bull I (d. 1687)** — is now documented at the top of *both* branches, not merely inferred.

## 3. Richard Bull (b. ~1686) → the Northampton Boole family

Carey, citing the estate division of **26 July 1729** (North. Co. Wills & Deeds 1725–1733, pp. 202–203), names Richard's widow **Esther Booll** and his **eight children**:

> John Bool · Richard Bool · [a daughter who married William Mills] · Dewey Bool · Tabitha Bool · **Nicholas Bool** · Rachel Bool · Sarah Bool

And states explicitly: **"The Bulls in Northampton County continued to spell their name as Bool or Boole."**

So the Boole surname-drift is documented at its origin: it begins with Richard Bull (b. ~1686), son of Tobias I, whose Northampton descendants adopted Bool/Boole. Jane's 18th-century ancestors — **Richard Bool (~1745)** and **Nicholas Boole (~1777)** — descend from this branch. (Note: the *Nicholas Bool* in the 1729 division is an earlier Nicholas, b. ~1710s — an uncle/great-uncle of Jane's Nicholas, not the same man. The middle generations between Richard b.1686's children and Jane's Richard ~1745 are the remaining gap to walk.)

A nice cross-link: Carey notes Richard's brother **Dewey Bull** died in Accomack by 1738 with his estate administered by **Rebecca Bunting** — echoing that Tobias I's widow Rebecca (Hollowell) later married a Bunting. The Bull–Bunting tie recurs.

## 4. Resolution of the Tobias II-vs-III discrepancy — whitepaper was right

`01-milesfiles-findings` flagged that MilesFiles filed **Daniel** (Brady's ancestor) under **Tobias III**, one generation lower than the whitepaper's **Tobias II → Daniel**. Carey settles it from the wills:

- **"Daniel Bull, son of Tobias Bull, II, and his wife Frances, was born circa 1724."** (Carey, "Daniel Bull — The Third Generation")
- "Daniel Bull and Catherine Bull witnessed the will of **his brother, Tobias Bull, III**, in 1760" (Wills 1757–1761, p. 308).
- The Daniel who was *son of Tobias III* is a different person, who "apparently died young, unmarried" (Arcadia Bull deeded his interest away 5 Mar 1773).

So **Daniel (b. ~1724), Brady's ancestor, is a son of Tobias II and a brother of Tobias III** — exactly the whitepaper's structure. MilesFiles conflated the two Daniels. **The whitepaper line stands; the extra-Tobias-III generation is ruled out.**
