## Headline findings

1. **The Bull = Bool = Boole equation is confirmed inside MilesFiles itself**, not just in the library's file title. The database files the variant spelling as a parenthetical suffix on the same "Bull" surname: e.g. "Richard Bull **(Bool)**," "Nicholas Bull **(Boole)**," "David Nottingham Bull **(Boole)**." Same surname object, Northampton/Accomack geography.

2. **Jane's Boole line is confirmed internally, generation by generation, and her lighthouse-keeper ancestor is verified to the day.** Nicholas (Boole) → David Nottingham (Boole) is documented with primary Northampton County records.

3. **BUT the link from the Boole branch up to Tobias Bull I/II is NOT documented in MilesFiles.** Both Boole progenitors — Richard Bull (Bool, ~1745) and Nicholas Bull (Boole, ~1777) — appear as *unparented roots* in the database (each shows "1 earliest ancestor" = himself). The authoritative Eastern Shore database does **not** connect them upward to Tobias. The cousin relationship remains a strong inference from shared surname + same small geography + the library's own "Descendants of Tobias Bull I — also spelled Bool, Boole" file title — but it is not a documented parent-child chain.

4. **A dating problem surfaced that the primer's proposed chain did not anticipate.** The primer had Richard Bull (~1737) → Nicholas Boole (1766). MilesFiles puts Richard (Bool)'s two documented marriages at **1783 and 1787** and Nicholas (Boole)'s birth at **~1777** — so this specific Richard cannot be the father of this Nicholas (Nicholas predates Richard's marriages, and is only ~32 years younger). Either there is an earlier undocumented union, or Nicholas's father is a different Richard, or the generations are mis-stacked in Jane's WikiTree-derived line. **This is the real state of the evidence and should be represented honestly to Jane.**

5. **The "wife Rebeccah" question is fully resolved with the primary source** — it is Tobias Bull **I** (d. 1687), and his wife was Rebecca Hollowell. See §3.

### Richard Bull (Bool), I72077 — the details
- Birth Abt 1745, Northampton County. **No parents in the database.**
- 1787 guardian record: *"Northampton County Order Book 30 page 677, June 2, 1787 — The court doth appoint Richard Bool to be gua[rdian]..."*
- Family 1: m. **Keziah Bull (Bool)** (I119282, ~1750), 10 Dec 1783 — "Geo. Ashby was security on the M.L.B. of Richard Bull and Keziah Bool."
- Family 2: m. **Margaret 'Peggy' James / Addison** (I71956, b. 1749, widow), 31 May 1787 — "Jonathan Stott was the security on the M.L.B. of Richard Bool and Margaret Addison." Child: Charles W. Bull (~1788).
- Sources: Marriages, Northampton Co. VA 1660/1–1854 (S622); Northampton Co. Order Book 30 (S3469).

### Nicholas Bull (Boole), I99518 — the details (Jane's line, confirmed internally)
- Birth Abt 1777, Northampton County; **death Bef 8 Sep 1823** — *"administration on the estate of Nicholas Bool was granted to Custis Kellam."* **This death matches Jane's WikiTree date (d. 8 Sep 1823) exactly.** (Her WikiTree birth of 1766 is ~11 years earlier than MilesFiles's ~1777.)
- **No parents in the database** (unparented root).
- 1804 land cause: named in *Richard Coleburn &c. vs David Nottingham &c.*, St. George Parish, Accomack — 17 Jun 1804.
- 1820 census head of household, Northampton Co.: "Nicholas Bool, age 45+ (b. bef. 1775)."
- m. **Elizabeth 'Betsy' (of Abel) Nottingham** (I99519, ~1782–Oct 1840), 18 Aug 1802 — "Edmund Bell was security on the M.L.B. of Nicholas Bool and Elizabeth Nottingham." This is where the **Nottingham** middle names enter the Boole line (David **Nottingham** Boole, George **Nottingham** Boole — named for their mother's family).
- Children: George N. Boole (~1803), Michael (~1815), Sally H. (~1817), **David Nottingham (Boole) (~1821)**.
- Sources: Bo Lewis family info (S2657); Nottingham *Accomack Land Causes 1728–1825* (S571); Hamilton *Northampton Co. Census 1820–1840* (S2510); Scott *Northampton Co. Abstracts of Wills & Administrations 1800–1854* (S1003); Northampton marriages (S622).

### David Nottingham Bull (Boole), I99516 — Jane's direct ancestor, the lighthouse keeper
- b. Abt 1821, Northampton Co.; **d. 1 Feb 1896**, Northampton Co.
- m. **Sally Ann (of Edmond) Bell** (I93881), 23 Nov 1840.
- **The 1 Feb 1896 death + marriage to Sally Bell exactly match the Chesapeake Chapter USLHS keeper bio** (David Nottingham Boole, Principal Keeper, Hog Island Lighthouse 1864–1872; m. Sally Bell 23 Nov 1840; d. 1 Feb 1896; buried Red Bank Baptist Church Cemetery, Marionville). MilesFiles and the lighthouse-service record are the same man. The brother George Nottingham Boole (1803) from Jane's email is also confirmed as a sibling here.

## 3. "Wife Rebeccah" — RESOLVED with the primary source

The primer flagged: *"MilesFiles p656.htm cites Stratton Nottingham, Accomack Wills & Administrations 1663–1800, p. 15 — will of Tobias Bull, wife Rebeccah. The whitepaper has no wife for Tobias; verify which Tobias this is and add."*

**Resolved: it is Tobias Bull I (d. 1687).** His MilesFiles page (I56790) records:
- **Will dated 29 Apr 1687**, Accomack County; proved before 16 Nov 1687.
- Will abstract: ***"To wife Rebeccah Whole estate. Wife Exec. Witt: John Wallop, Nathaniel Tunnell, Thomas Conaway."***
- Source: **Stratton Nottingham, *Accomack Co., VA, Wills & Administrations, 1663–1800*** (MilesFiles source S570) — the exact citation the primer named. Also cited: Carey, Miscellaneous Research Files (S769).
- Wife identified as **Rebecca Hollowell** (I56251, ~1660–~1740), m. ~1675. After Tobias I died (1687) she remarried **William Bunting**.
- Their children: **Rebecca Bull** (~1680, m. Henry White) and **Tobias Bull II** (~1682, m. Frances —?—). Tobias II continues Brady's line.

**Whitepaper action:** add to Tobias Bull I — *wife Rebecca Hollowell (m. ~1675); he willed her his entire estate and named her executrix, 29 Apr 1687 (Nottingham, Accomack Wills & Admin. 1663–1800); she later married William Bunting.*
