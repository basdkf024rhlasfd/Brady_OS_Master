**Almost certainly cousins — roughly 8th cousins, common ancestor Tobias Bull I (d. 1687) or Tobias Bull II (d. 1761), ~10 generations back on each side.** Her branch stayed in Northampton Co. and drifted to the Boole spelling; Brady's branch moved into Accomack (Pungoteague Creek / "Bull Run") and kept Bull. The exact generation where her Richard Bull (~1737) attaches under the Tobias structure is the one unverified link.

## Settled — Evidence Found This Session

1. **Institutional equation of the names.** The Eastern Shore of Virginia Public Library's family file for this lineage is titled *"Descendants of Tobias Bull I — Also spelled Bool, Boole, Boll, Booll."* One catalogued family, anchored to Brady's 7th great-grandfather, with Boole as a recognized variant.
2. **1861 court record legally equates Bull = Bool on Jane's own ancestor.** Northampton Order Book No. 44, 1857–1865: 516–518 (transcription by Gail M. Walczyk at easternshorestuff.com/misc/david_nottingham_bull.htm): David Nottingham **Bool** got drunk at Birdsnest Tavern, Marionville, spring 1861 and spoke against slavery; the grand jury indicted him as **"David Bull"** for "maintaining that owners have no right of property in their slaves"; convicted, 3 months jail; later freed by Union troops and given charge of the Hog Island lighthouse.
3. **Jane's line confirmed deep Eastern Shore.** Chesapeake Chapter USLHS keeper bio (cheslights.org/keeper-bio-david-nottingham-boole): David Boole b. 1821 Northampton Co.; m. Sally Bell 23 Nov 1840; Principal Keeper, Hog Island Lighthouse 1864–1872 ($400→$560/yr; wife Sallie First Assistant); d. 1 Feb 1896; buried Red Bank Baptist Church Cemetery, Marionville. Later Booles born at Exmore, Northampton Co. into the 1890s (WikiTree).
4. **Colonial-era Booll spelling in Northampton primary records.** Marshall, *Northampton Co. Abstracts of Wills & Administrations, 1632–1802*, p. 382: Nicholas Booll, wife Frances, in administration of Jonathan Smith — the family sits in Northampton will books in the mid-1700s, right where Jane's Richard and Nicholas belong.
5. **Geographic loop closes via the whitepaper.** Brady's Bull line first appears as John and Thomas Bulluck on the **1666 Northampton tithables**, then moves to Accomack. Both branches share the Northampton origin.
6. **Bonus detail for the whitepaper:** MilesFiles p656.htm cites Stratton Nottingham, *Accomack Wills & Administrations 1663–1800*, p. 15 — will of Tobias Bull, **wife Rebeccah**. The whitepaper has no wife for Tobias; verify which Tobias this is and add.

## Settled — Decisions

- Treat Boole/Bool/Booll as spellings of the Tobias Bull family (institutionally established).
- Relationship estimate: ~8th cousins via Tobias I or II.
- "Richard Bull 1937" in Jane's email = typo for 1737.
