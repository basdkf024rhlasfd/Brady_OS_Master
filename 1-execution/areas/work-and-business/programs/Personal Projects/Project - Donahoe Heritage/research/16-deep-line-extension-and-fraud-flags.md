## What's NEW (clean and verified)

### Capt William H. Parker (9SKY-DJ3, ~1717–1784) — NEW DAR PATRIOT FOUND

Susan's **6th great-grandfather** through the Coulbourne descent path (he was the husband of Frances Colburne, daughter of Robert Coulburne). Born/died Accomack Co, VA. **Revolutionary War: Lieutenant 1776, Captain 1778–1781.** This is documented on his FamilySearch profile.

**This is the strongest, cleanest DAR Patriot candidate on Susan's line.** Stronger than Elijah Bull (whose parents aren't even linked in the tree) and arguably stronger than Seth Dean (Connecticut, military-age but no documented service confirmed in this walk). DAR application path runs:

> Capt William H. Parker → Frances Colburne (his wife) → Ruth Parker (their daughter) → Francis Boggs III → Eliza Evins Boggs → William Frances Rodgers → Mary Eliza Rogers → Fannye Bull → Mary Ellen Donahoe → Susan Caylor Dean / Paulette Dean.

### Anthony Fisher Sr & Anthony Fisher Jr — Great Migration Pilgrim ancestry CONFIRMED, but on the Fisher line, NOT the Dean line

**Both Anthony Fisher Sr (1591–1671) and Anthony Fisher Jr (~1623–1670) arrived at Boston on the ship *Rose* from Yarmouth, 26 June 1637.** Documented Great Migration immigrants. Sr signed the Dedham Covenant (#36); both took the Freeman's Oath. NEHGR 151:171–183 cited (Hyde & Plummer, 1997, "The English Ancestry of New England Settlers Joshua and Anthony Fisher").

The Fisher line goes back to **John Fisher of Wingfield, Suffolk (1465–1523)** with full documentation, including a 1522/3 will and the 1524 lay subsidy of Syleham. Cleanest deep European chain in the entire walk.

**Important correction:** The Pilgrim-era / Great Migration ancestry on Brady's tree is on the **FISHER** line (Susan's 7th-great-grandmother Johanna "Hannah" Fisher's parents and ancestors), NOT on the Dean line. Files 12 and 15 implied Dean Pilgrim-stock — that was wrong. **Nathaniel Dean (1693) has NO parents linked in the FamilySearch tree.** The Dean line dead-ends at a Connecticut-born individual. There is no Mayflower / Plymouth / Massachusetts Bay 1620–1640 connection on the Dean surname itself.

### John Floyd I "of Hog Island" (LRNC-CKK, ~1640–1687) — Welsh-origin Northampton VA immigrant

Susan's 7th great-grandfather (Berry Floyd's grandfather → Matthew Floyd Sr's father). **Arrived Northampton Co 30 July 1660 as one of 20 headrights transported by John Wilcocks.** Patented 100 acres (1668) and 2,200 acres (1681) — "Hogg Island alias Shooting Beach." Appointed constable of Northampton Co 1669. **Profile explicitly states: "He was presumably born in Wales, although nothing of his parentage or early life is known."** A Hog Island deed records his surname as "Lloyd" — Welsh original.

### Coulbourne well-documented at Pomfret

Col William Coulbourne (1630) and his father Charles Coulbourne (1603) are both well-documented; both born Westminster, London (St Martin-in-the-Fields parish). Charles immigrated to Somerset Co, MD. Sources: Torrence's *Old Somerset on the Eastern Shore of Maryland*; Maryland Historical Trust property file for Pomfret Plantation.

### Kellam English origin

Richard Kellam II (LQR6-LDT) confirmed as Aslackby, Lincolnshire-origin, with his father Richard Kellam (~1581–1636), born Aslackby, married Katherine Ederidge 20 March 1616 at St Paul, Lincoln. Both well-sourced. Richard Sr's father Edmund Kellam (1555) is the last clean ancestor — above Edmund the tree conflates Kellams with Drapers (clear data-integrity issue).

### Parker / Mattapony immigrant

**John Parker Sr "of Mattapony" (LY2W-JFR, 1634–1695)** — Susan's 9th great-grandfather through the Parker line — born/christened Southampton, Hampshire, England; immigrated to Virginia by March 1655 (transported by his brother George Parker); patented 600 acres "Mattapany" in 1660. **Eastern Shore VA founding-generation immigrant.** Well-documented in Whitelaw's *Virginia's Eastern Shore* and MILESFILES.

His father George Parker "of Southampton" (1596–1639) and grandfather John Parker (1550–1612) are both documented in Southampton parish records, with George's will dated 24 October 1638 transcribed in full.

## What's RETRACTED or DOWNGRADED to "uncertain"

### Sir John Henry Maynard 3rd Baronet — RETRACTED

The English-nobility claim from files 12 and 15 is **explicitly fraud-flagged on FamilySearch itself.**

The profile carries two researcher notes (Steve Maynard, 20 June 2018) that say:
1. "**Parents of Henry Maynard not confirmed (as of 20 Jun 2018)**"
2. "More research for proven documentation of Henry's parentage is needed."
3. "The real 3rd Baronet's will doesn't mention a wife Sarah or any Maryland children."

Plus internal contradictions: TWO separate "3rd Baronet" sons listed under Sir William Maynard 1st Baronet ("William Maynard 2nd Baronet" MM2B-X68 AND "Sir Henry Maynard 3rd Baronet" G144-WRC AND "Sir John Henry Maynard 3rd Baronet" KDBG-GPF). Duplicate "3rd Baronet" assignment is diagnostic of fraudulent merge.

**The Henry Maynard who is Brady's actual 7th great-grandfather is real**, but the "3rd Baronet of Walthamstow" identification is an unverified aspirational graft. The whitepaper should call him "Henry Maynard (1681–1738), of Walthamstow, Essex (parentage and any baronet connection unverified)" — not "Sir John Henry Maynard, 3rd Baronet."

### Lady Rachel Sarah Hopkins — RETRACTED

Her FamilySearch profile carries an explicit note: "This Sarah's **maiden name is 'unknown'** as are her birth date and place, parentage, and when she was married to Robert Hopkins." The "Lady Rachel Sarah" name and the "Sir Robert Hopkins / Lady Sarah Elizabeth DeVille" parents are aspirational fanfic on a real woman whose actual maiden name is genuinely unknown. **Treat her as "Sarah, m. ~ Robert Hopkins, maiden name unknown."**

### Floyd line above Nathaniel Floyd — RETRACTED

The "Sir John Theophelus Floyd" + "Lady Mary Veriar of Queen Elizabeth's Court" parents grafted onto Nathaniel Floyd (1601) are flagged as **bogus on the profile itself**. **Nathaniel Floyd himself (1601, Wales → *Bona Nova* arrival in Virginia 1624/5, 850-acre Isle of Wight patent 1637) is a real, documented ancestor.** The Welsh origin and Bona Nova arrival stand. Above him is fraud territory; do not walk past.

### Coulbourne visitation pedigree (Wythehill → John → Thomas → William of Braton) — DOWNGRADED to "tradition"

The 1623 Visitation of Somerset (Harleian Society Pub. Vol. XI, p. 26) reproduces a Coulbourne pedigree. **The FamilySearch profile itself notes this chain is "not unlikely" but explicitly NOT proven for the Maryland immigrant.** Cite as English-tradition pedigree, not verified ancestry.

### Donaghe Ulster pre-1635 chain — DOWNGRADED to "0-source"

No documented Plantation record, no Scottish origin, no parish data. **The whitepaper can claim Ulster origin for the Donaghe surname as plausible but flag that pre-1635 documentation is absent.**

### Curry cross-link to the Clark/Monacan project — RETRACTED

Elizabeth Currie / Curry (G7H6-SRJ, 1637–1687), James Donaghe's wife, has 0 sources on her parents, and her mother is listed as "Gordana Gordia" — a name with no genealogical record anywhere outside FamilySearch. **The Curry surname coincidence with Peter Curry in the Clark/Monacan project's Sorrells case is just that — a surname coincidence.** No deep common-ancestor link is supportable.

### Kellam tree above Edmund Kellam (1555) — RETRACTED

Above Edmund Kellam, the tree confuses Kellams with Drapers. Thomas Kellam's father is recorded as "Robert Kellam ALS Draper" and Thomas's wife is also a Draper; sibling list mixes Kellams and Drapers. Cross-family conflation. **The Kellam line is solid back to Edmund Kellam (1555); above him, do not cite.**

### Bull line above Elijah Bull (1748) — UNAVAILABLE

Elijah Bull has NO parents linked in the FamilySearch tree. No colonial Accomack patentee Bull ancestor is available. **The Bull-Kellam land continuity story for the whitepaper rests on the Kellam side (Richard Kellam II 1635 immigrant), not on a deep Bull-side patentee.
