## How the line was resolved (the test of each candidate)

The FamilySearch tree gives Nathaniel Dean as b. 9 January 1693 (Connecticut) and married to Joanna Fisher 17 May 1716 (MA Bay Colony). At least four candidate Nathaniel Deans of approximately the right vintage exist in early-1690s New England records, so the test is whether any candidate fits both anchors AND has a wife named Joanna/Johanna Fisher.

### Candidate A: Nathaniel Dean of Taunton MA, b. 25 Apr 1685 (NOT 1693), son of Isaac Deane + Hannah Leonard

This is the candidate that several genealogy sites (WikiTree Deane-129, FamilySearch LC87-VKY) link to "Joanna Fisher" via secondary trees. **It does not match Brady's tree:**

- Wrong birth year — 1685 vs 1693. Eight-year discrepancy is too large for a transcription error of a known birth record.
- Wrong birthplace — Taunton, Bristol County, Massachusetts vs Connecticut.
- The Isaac Deane→Hannah Leonard line is well-documented in *Vital Records of Taunton*, but their son Nathaniel's later life is poorly documented; some trees graft Joanna Fisher onto him because of WikiTree merges, not primary record evidence.

**Verdict: REJECTED.** This Nathaniel is a different person. He may have lived and died in Taunton or Norton MA. Some FamilySearch trees have erroneously merged Candidate A with Candidate B (below) — that is the source of the "1685 vs 1693" confusion that file 16 flagged.

### Candidate B: Nathaniel Dean baptized 2 Apr 1693 Stonington CT, son of James Deane

**This is the match.**

- **Stonington Cong. baptism register, Rev. James Noyes:** "Nathaniel Dean, son of James Dean, baptized April 16, 1693" (one secondary source gives 2 April; the small discrepancy is a transcription divergence between two published versions of the same register; both point to early April 1693).
- **Birth-year match** — 1693 vs Brady's tree's 1693. ✓
- **Geography match** — Connecticut, exactly per Brady's tree. ✓
- **Hudson-Mohawk Genealogical and Family Memoirs (Cuyler Reynolds, ed., 1911), Deane entry:** "Nathaniel (baptized April 2, 1693) — married Joanna Fisher." ✓ The published genealogy independently confirms the wife.
- **Geographic logic:** James Deane removed from Stonington to Plainfield CT in 1698, and was a large landowner in Voluntown CT. The next generation's Seth Dean is born in Voluntown CT in 1722 (per Brady's existing tree). Nathaniel of Stonington/Plainfield is exactly the right person in the right place at the right time to father Seth Dean in Voluntown.

**Verdict: ACCEPTED.** Nathaniel Dean (P76R-69B) is the baptized-1693 son of James Deane of Stonington/Plainfield CT.

### Candidate C: Nathaniel "Hall" Dean of Taunton, b. 18 May 1695, m. Mable Winchell 9 Dec 1716

The 1695 Taunton Nathaniel — described in some Suffield CT records as a "cordwainer" — is REJECTED because (a) wrong year (b) wrong wife (Mable Winchell, not Joanna Fisher) (c) wrong children (he had nine children all named Hall, suggesting a different surname conflation in the original record).

### Candidate D: Nathaniel, son of John Deane and Sarah Edson (the brother of Isaac of Taunton)

Possible secondary candidate — John Deane (b. 1639 Taunton, son of John the immigrant and Alice) had a son who could potentially be a 1693 Nathaniel. **No primary record found for any 1693 Nathaniel in this branch**, and no spouse Joanna Fisher in the descendant trees of John Deane Jr. **REJECTED for lack of any evidence**, but kept on file as a secondary fallback if Candidate B is ever overturned.

## The Deane chain, with primary-source citations

### Generation 7 — Nathaniel Dean (Brady's 6th great-grandfather)

- **Baptized:** 2 April 1693 (some transcripts: 16 April 1693), Stonington Congregational Church, Stonington, New London County, Connecticut Colony, by Rev. James Noyes.
- **Father:** James Deane (Stonington blacksmith, Plainfield town clerk).
- **Mother:** Sarah Tisdale (b. ~1648 Duxbury MA, d. 26 April 1726 Plainfield CT — daughter of John Tisdale of Duxbury MA, a separate Pilgrim-stock line worth chasing in v2 Stream A4 work).
- **Marriage:** 17 May 1716, Boston, MA Bay Colony, to **Joanna (or Johanna) Fisher**, b. 13 Sep 1686 Dedham MA, daughter of Josiah Fisher (1654-1736) and his second wife Mary (Eliot) Fisher. Joanna's grandfather was Anthony Fisher Jr (the *Rose* 1637 Pilgrim).
- **Death:** Brady's tree gives 1727 with a question mark. This is consistent with the absence of any later Stonington/Plainfield/Voluntown record for him; he likely died young (about age 34) leaving Joanna a young widow. **Confirming this requires checking Stonington/Norwich/Voluntown probate 1727-1735 — open action item.**
- **Children documented:** Brady's tree shows son Seth Dean the elder (K82V-GRY, b. 1722 Voluntown CT, d. 1781 New Marlborough MA). The Voluntown birth confirms the Plainfield/Voluntown geographic footprint inherited from his father James (large landowner Voluntown).

### Generation 8 — James Deane Sr. (Brady's 7th great-grandfather)

- **Birth:** ~1648, Taunton, Plymouth Colony, Massachusetts (now Bristol County).
- **Father:** Walter Deane of Taunton (immigrant 1637).
- **Mother:** Eleanor Cogan (also rendered "Strong" in older sources, but the more current scholarship per the Deane Society gives Cogan, daughter of Thomas/William Coggan of South Chard, Somerset; some older sources say Eleanor Strong, daughter of Richard Strong of Taunton-in-England — ambiguity remains, but it's known the family pattern was tightly tied to Chard/South Chard, Somerset, where multiple Deanes intermarried with Strongs and Cogans).
- **Trade:** Blacksmith and iron worker.
- **Stonington land grant:** 26 February 1676, Stonington town vote granted James Deane "a home lot of twenty-four acres of land" plus 100 acres of farmland to induce him to settle there as the town blacksmith. (*History of New London County, Connecticut*, full text on cga.ct.gov; same record in *Hudson-Mohawk*.)
- **Removal to Plainfield CT:** 1698.
- **Plainfield town clerk:** Elected first town clerk of Plainfield in 1699; held office "many years."
- **Voluntown landholder:** "A large land owner in Plainfield and in Voluntown" — this directly explains why Seth Dean (Brady's 5th gg) was b. 1722 Voluntown.
- **Marriage:** Sarah Tisdale, ~1674.
- **Death:** 29 May 1725 Plainfield CT. Wife Sarah died 26 April 1726 Plainfield.
- **Children documented in Hudson-Mohawk:**
  - John (b. 15 May 1678, m. Lydia Thatcher 10 Jun 1708)
  - **Nathaniel (bp. 2 April 1693, m. Joanna Fisher) — Brady's line**
  - Jonathan (bp. 22 April 1695, m. Sarah Douglas)

### Generation 9 — Walter Deane (Brady's 8th great-grandfather, GREAT MIGRATION IMMIGRANT)

- **Born:** ~1612, baptized 13 May 1612 at St. Mary's Church, Chard, Somerset, England.
- **Parents:** William Deane of South Chard (d. 1634) and his wife (mother's name not yet identified in primary sources).
- **Immigration:** Sailed on the ship *Speedwell* from Weymouth, Dorset, England, 22 April 1637, with his elder brother John Deane. Master: John Driver. Each brother brought six servants.
- **Cited in:** Anderson, *The Great Migration Directory*, p. 91 — confirming Speedwell 1637 arrival at Boston.
- **Settlement:** Initially Dorchester MA, then settled at Cohannet (renamed Taunton) in Plymouth Colony as one of the original purchasers of the town. Trade: tanner.
- **Freeman's oath:** 4 December 1638.
- **Civic offices:** Deputy to Plymouth General Court 1640; Selectman of Taunton 1666-1686 (some sources 1679-1686).
- **Marriage:** Eleanor Cogan/Strong, in Taunton 1636/1637. (See note above on disputed maiden name.)
- **Death:** After 25 August 1693 Taunton MA.
- **The "Joseph A. Deane" lineage in the 1893 Plainfield biography traces this exact line:** "His father, Abijah, whose father Nathaniel, was a son of Ebenezer, a son of John, whose father John, was a son of John Dean, who came to this country in 1637 from England" — a different branch (through Walter's brother John, not Walter himself), but it confirms the immigrant John+Walter pair as the foundational fact for ALL CT-MA Deans of this line.

### Generation 10 — William Deane of South Chard (Brady's 9th great-grandfather)

- **Born:** ~1568 Chard, Somerset, England (one transcript: 8 January 1570).
- **Died:** July 1634, South Chard, Somerset.
- **Will:** Dated 22 July 1634, names children including John, Walter, Margerie (first wife of Elder John Strong, a NEW England Pilgrim founder of Northampton MA), William (eldest son), Thomas, Isaacke, Susan, Eleanor, and Joane.
- **Parents:** Walter Deane (the elder, ~1545-1591) and Joan Walsele (per WikiTree Deane-10/Deane-758 secondary).

**Note the deep structural finding:** William Deane's daughter Margerie (Brady's 9th great-aunt) was the first wife of **Elder John Strong** — the Pilgrim-stock founder of Northampton, Massachusetts, and ancestor of multiple New England political families including (probably) the Strongs of Connecticut who appear in Hartford and New London. The Deane family of South Chard was a tight, intermarried Pilgrim-recruiting cluster. This is why some older sources erroneously give Walter Deane's wife Eleanor as Strong rather than Cogan — the families were intermarried, and the Eleanor Cogan/Eleanor Strong confusion runs through the published genealogies for over a century.

This Deane-Strong intermarriage in South Chard, Somerset, is the structural reason the Deane and Strong surnames appear together in early Taunton + Northampton MA records.

### Generation 11 — Walter Deane the elder (~1545-1591)

Documented in WikiTree Deane-758. His wife was Joan Walsele (some sources Walsele/Wasley). Born and died Chard, Somerset. **Considered secondary-source documented; primary records (parish registers) need verification at the Somerset Record Office.**

Above this point the line is open and not yet primary-source-resolved.

- 1568 Chard Somerset (William Deane) → 1637 Taunton MA (Walter, *Speedwell*) → 1676 Stonington CT (James, blacksmith) → 1698 Plainfield CT (James, town clerk) → 1722 Voluntown CT (Seth Sr) → 1746 Voluntown/New London CT (Seth Jr) → 1785 Jaffrey NH (Amasa) → 1814 Vermont (William F.) → mid-1800s Brighton MI (William F./Francis Pearce) → 1886 South Lyon MI (Roy Franklin) → 1930 South Lyon MI (Del) → 1960s Danville VA → today.

- **Nine generations of documented New England residency before the Michigan move** — making the Dean surname Brady's most deeply New-England-rooted surname.

### CROSS-LINE STRUCTURAL FINDING: Deane-Strong intermarriage

Margerie Deane (b. ~1620 Chard Somerset, d. ~1635 Dorchester MA) was the first wife of Elder John Strong. Elder John Strong is one of the founders of Northampton, Massachusetts, and a major Pilgrim-stock patriarch. The Deane-Strong pairing happened in South Chard, Somerset, not in America — these were already intermarried families in Somerset before the Great Migration.

This means **Brady has Strong-family heritage by AFFINAL connection** (Deane married into Strong before the migration), which intersects with the Hopkins/Maynard tail still under fraud-flag review and may surface other ancestral surface area in v2.

### THE DEANE-FISHER LINK IS GREAT-MIGRATION-PILGRIM ON BOTH SIDES

The marriage of Nathaniel Dean to Joanna Fisher in 1716 Boston brought together TWO Great Migration *Speedwell*/*Rose* 1637 immigrant family lines:
- Nathaniel Dean's grandfather Walter — *Speedwell* 1637.
- Joanna Fisher's grandfather Anthony Fisher Jr — *Rose* 1637.

Both ships arrived at Boston within two months of each other (April-June 1637). Both families were among the earliest English settlers of the new Pilgrim/Puritan settlements (Walter at Taunton, Anthony at Dedham). The Dean-Fisher marriage 79 years later in Boston was effectively the merger of two parallel founder lines.

This is a **clean Pilgrim-stock convergence** that v2 should foreground.
