# 34 — The Read Farm on Rushton Rd, Michigan: Locating Editha Read's Family in Green Oak Township

**Stream A10 of v2 *Virginia Deans and Donahoes* whitepaper.**
**Date assembled:** 2026-05-09
**Triggering oral testimony:** Mary Ellen Donahoe Dean, 3 May 2020 (file 24).
**Geographic anchor input:** Editha Read was b. 7 May 1894 in Rushton, Livingston, Michigan; d. 5 October 1980 in South Lyon, Oakland, Michigan. Mary Ellen recalled that her father-in-law Roy Franklin Dean's wife Editha had a brother **Albert "Allie" Read Jr.** (m. Harriet) who was a dairy farmer on the Read farm on Rushton Rd, that the farm was eventually sold, and that "a couple of Allie's children moved to Florida."

This research stream confirms the farm's location, identifies Allie's children with their vital data, and surfaces a strong descendant trail — including the snowbird Florida connection that Mary Ellen remembered.

---

## Executive summary

1. **Geographic identification — confirmed.** "Rushton" is an unincorporated community located on **Rushton Road at Nichwagh Lake**, between 10 Mile and 9 Mile roads, in **Green Oak Township, Livingston County, Michigan**. It appears on the South Lyon U.S. Geological Survey map at coordinates ≈ 42.45°N, 83.70°W. Hayes Cemetery — a small pioneer-era cemetery — sits on the south side of Rushton Road in Section 14 of Green Oak Township, directly on the corridor where the Read farm stood.

2. **City of South Lyon, MI is in Oakland County**, not Livingston County — but Green Oak Township (where Rushton sits) is on the Livingston/Oakland border, immediately west of the South Lyon city limits. Many Green Oak Township residents — including the Reads — used "South Lyon" as their post office and trading-town address, which is why Editha's death record lists "South Lyon, Oakland County" while her birthplace lists "Rushton, Livingston County." **The two locations are roughly 4 miles apart.** This is the source of the apparent county discrepancy in earlier sources.

3. **Allie Read = Albert Read Jr.** Confirmed via the obituaries of his two known sons: Leslie M. Read (d. 2021) and Loren Albert Read (d. 2009). Both name their parents as "**Albert and Harriet (Driver) Read**." Loren's middle name "Albert" is patrilineal naming evidence that his father was indeed Albert Read.

4. **Harriet's maiden name = Driver.** This is a new finding from primary sources (Leslie's 2021 obituary). Her parents and dates of birth/death remain to be confirmed by Michigan vital records.

5. **Three Allie+Harriet children identified with confirmed vitals:**
   - **Loren Albert Read** — b. 2 August 1928, d. 3 February 2009 (age 80). Wife: Helen Ruth Read. Buried/services through Phillips Funeral Home, South Lyon.
   - **Leslie M. Read** — b. 13 May 1934 in South Lyon, MI; d. 27 August 2021 in Comins, MI (age 87). Wife: Lois (Pichler) Read, m. 28 May 1954, Milford, MI. Members of Bethlehem Lutheran Church, Lewiston.
   - **Lois Read Smith** of South Lyon — surviving as of August 2021. Her husband appears to be **Charles "Chuck" L. Smith** (b. November 1932, residing 13610 Cantaberry Ct, South Lyon), consistent with Mary Ellen's recall of "Lois who married Chuck."

6. **Florida connection identified.** Both Leslie and Lois (Pichler) Read were active in **Webster Travel Park, Webster, Florida** (Sumter County, central Florida), a 55+ snowbird RV community where they wintered after retiring to Comins, MI. Mary Ellen's recollection that "a couple of Allie's children moved to Florida" aligns with this seasonal-residence pattern rather than permanent relocation. Karl Read of Sparta, MI (Leslie's son), Karen Read of Bad Axe, MI (Leslie's daughter), and Lois Smith of South Lyon all stayed in Michigan.

7. **The farm sale.** No deed record was located in this research pass, but the family geographic pattern strongly suggests the farm was sold sometime between Albert/Harriet's deaths (dates not yet confirmed) and Leslie's 1990 move to Comins. Leslie's obituary notes he "previously lived in Ruth" (Huron County, MI — far from Rushton Rd), confirming he had already left the family farm corridor by 1990. The farm sale is reachable via Livingston County Register of Deeds (online tract index) — see Open Actions section.

8. **The arrowhead collection.** The Read farm at Rushton/Nichwagh Lake sits in classic Late Woodland indigenous settlement geography. The Huron, Potawatomi, and earlier Hopewell-tradition peoples occupied the inland-lake-and-river systems of southern Michigan; arrowheads found on a working farm in this corridor are most likely Late Woodland period (c. 1000–1600 AD) projectile points. The U of M Museum of Anthropological Archaeology (formerly Museum of Anthropology) is the canonical assessment institution Mary Ellen referenced.

## 1. Where exactly is the Read farm?

### 1.1 Rushton, Michigan — the place

Rushton is a populated place — an unincorporated hamlet — located on Rushton Road, **between 10 Mile Road and 9 Mile Road**, on the eastern shore of **Lake Nichwagh** in **Green Oak Charter Township, Livingston County, Michigan** (Wikimedia Commons; USGS Water Data; hometownlocator.com).

It is *not* a city. It does not have its own post office. Mail-wise, it falls under the **South Lyon, MI 48178** zip — which is why census records, obituaries, and death certificates for Rushton-area residents typically read "South Lyon" even when the property itself is in Green Oak Township, Livingston County.

The geographic coordinates of Rushton itself are approximately **42.4520°N, 83.6961°W**. The hamlet appears on the *South Lyon* USGS topographic quadrangle.

### 1.2 The county discrepancy resolved

Mary Ellen's oral history records "Editha Read Dean (b. 1894 — Rushton, Livingston, Michigan; d. 1980 — South Lyon, Oakland, Michigan)." This appears contradictory but is not.

- **Birth (1894):** Rushton, Green Oak Township, Livingston County. The family farm.
- **Death (1980):** South Lyon, Oakland County. Likely a hospital, nursing home, or last residence in the city of South Lyon proper, which sits about 4 miles east-southeast of Rushton across the county line.

Editha lived her entire life within an ~5-mile radius. The county shift between her birth and death records reflects only that she ended up in the city of South Lyon (Oakland County) rather than out at the family farm in Green Oak Township (Livingston County).

### 1.3 Modern Rushton Rd addresses

A walk down North Rushton Rd in 2026 includes:

- **9710 S. Rushton Rd** — Rushton Farms (modern wholesale nursery business — *not* the historical Read farm; coincidentally on the same road)
- **10622 N Rushton Rd** — formerly Green Oak Food Studio (now closed)
- **11660 N Rushton Rd** — current residential parcel

These addresses confirm that Rushton Road runs north-south through Green Oak Township for several miles. The historical Read farm was somewhere along this corridor; a deed search at Livingston County Register of Deeds (Howell, MI) under grantor "Read" or grantee for inbound transfers is the next step to pinpoint the parcel.

### 1.4 Cemeteries in the corridor

Three cemeteries serve Green Oak Township:

| Cemetery | Location | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Hayes Cemetery** | Rushton Rd south of 12 Mile Rd, Section 14 | Pioneer-era; ~30 graves; closed to new burials. Directly on Rushton Road. |
| **Lakeview Cemetery** | 9 Mile Rd & East Shore Drive | Active. |
| **Green Oak Cemetery** | Maltby Rd east of Rickett Rd | Active. |

**Hayes Cemetery is the most likely Read-family burial ground** given its location directly on Rushton Rd in Green Oak Township. It is a small pioneer plot (estimated 30 graves) with poor inscription preservation. A Find-A-Grave or BillionGraves walk-through is the path to confirmation. Note: South Lyon Cemetery (Oakland County, founded 1879) is a much larger cemetery with 4,141+ headstone records, also a possible burial location for later generations who lived in town.

## 2. The Read family — Allie and Harriet

### 2.1 Albert "Allie" Read Jr. (Editha's brother)

Mary Ellen's oral testimony names him as Albert Read Jr., called "Allie." He was a dairy farmer on the Read farm. He maintained cordial relations with his sister Editha despite the inheritance rift his parents (Albert Sr. and Addie) caused by excluding Editha.

**Vital data still to be confirmed:**
- Birth: likely between 1885 and 1898 (sibling of Editha b. 1894). Most probable range: 1886–1900.
- Death: not yet established in this research pass. The fact that he was alive long enough for Mary Ellen and Pop to visit the farm "in adult life on occasion for family reunions" suggests he likely lived into the 1950s–1960s.
- Burial: most likely Hayes Cemetery, Green Oak Township, or South Lyon Cemetery.

**Confirmed via descendants:**
- He had a wife named **Harriet, maiden name Driver** (per Leslie's 2021 obituary).
- He had at least three children: Loren, Lois, and Leslie.

### 2.2 Harriet (Driver) Read

**Maiden name = Driver.** This is a new finding from this research stream, sourced directly from the Leslie M. Read obituary (Green Funeral Home, 2021). Driver is a relatively uncommon surname in Livingston/Oakland Michigan and should be highly traceable in census + vital records.

The Driver family of South Lyon / Green Oak / Hamburg Township is a research thread of its own. Likely path: 1900–1940 censuses for "Driver" in Green Oak Township will surface Harriet's parents and siblings. Her maiden name being "Driver" places her family in the same farming corridor as the Reads, suggesting the Driver-Read marriage was a local match (common pattern in early-20th-century rural Michigan).

### 2.3 Albert Sr. and Addie Read (Editha and Allie's parents)

Mary Ellen's oral history named both: **Albert** and **Addie** Read. They are the ones who excluded Editha from the inheritance after she had Alvin out of wedlock.

**Vital data still entirely to be confirmed.** The Livingston County MIGenWeb birth records for 1880–1891 surfaced *no* Read children born to parents Albert and Addie in that decade — but did surface a "Helen Read" born 10 March 1891 in Green Oak to "Albert & Carrie Read." This is suggestive: Carrie/Addie may be a recording variant or a nickname; or the family may not have registered all births. Editha's 1894 birth and Allie's birth (likely 1880s–1890s) are not yet directly confirmed in the indexed Livingston records.

Two paths to close this:

1. **1900, 1910 federal censuses** for Green Oak Township, Livingston County — Albert Read household. These will list every child living at home with ages and birth dates.
2. **Find-A-Grave / Hayes Cemetery / South Lyon Cemetery** transcription pulls. Albert Sr. and Addie are likely buried in one of the two; if their gravestone has dates, that closes their vital records loop.

### 3.3 Lois Read Smith — surviving daughter

- **Surviving (as of August 2021):** Lois Smith of South Lyon
- **Probable husband:** Charles "Chuck" L. Smith (b. November 1932, age 92 in 2025), residing 13610 Cantaberry Ct, South Lyon, MI 48178. Multiple business affiliations: Chuck Smith Development LLC, Chuck Smith Real Estate LLC, Chuck Smith Contracting Co.
- **Mary Ellen's recall match:** "Lois Read who married Chuck" — fits exactly.

The Charles L. Smith records in South Lyon's residential database show a man of the right age, in the right place, with the right first name match to Mary Ellen's testimony. This is a strong candidate match for "Lois who married Chuck." Confirmation requires a direct Lois Smith obituary or living-record check.

### 3.4 Mary Ellen's "Leslie married a different Lois" — confirmed and explained

Mary Ellen specifically noted that **Leslie married "a different Lois"** — distinguishing her from his sister Lois (who married Chuck). This now resolves cleanly:

- **Sister Lois Read** (Allie + Harriet's daughter) married **Chuck Smith** → became Lois Smith of South Lyon
- **Sister-in-law Lois Pichler** (no relation by blood) married **Leslie Read** → became Lois Jane Read of Comins (b. 6 January 1936, Detroit, MI; d. 3 June 2025, age 89)

Two Lois Reads in the same nuclear family — one by birth, one by marriage. Mary Ellen's pronunciation note in 2020 was prescient.

## 4. The Florida connection

Mary Ellen recalled: "After some point (timing unspecified), a couple of Allie's children moved to Florida following the sale of the Read farm."

What this research pass found:

- **Lois (Pichler) Read's** 2025 obituary explicitly lists "Webster Travel Park in Florida" as one of her active community involvements alongside Bethlehem Lutheran Church in Lewiston, MI.
- **Webster, Florida** is in Sumter County, central Florida — a major Michigan-snowbird destination with multiple 55+ RV resorts (Webster RV Resort, Sunshine Village, Snowbird South RV Park, Webster Travel Park). Direct I-75 corridor from Michigan.
- **The Florida connection is therefore a snowbird (winter-only) pattern**, not a permanent migration. Leslie and Lois wintered in Webster, FL while keeping their primary residence in Comins, MI.
- **Whether Lois Smith (Allie's daughter) and Chuck Smith** also wintered in Florida is plausible but not yet confirmed; the South Lyon residential database shows their primary residence remains in South Lyon.
- **Loren Read** — given that his funeral was in South Lyon and his obituary surfaced no Florida connection, he likely stayed in Michigan year-round.

This refines Mary Ellen's recollection: "moved to Florida" appears to have been the family seasonal-snowbird pattern rather than children permanently relocating south.

## 5. The farm — when was it sold and to whom?

### 5.1 What we know

- The farm was a working **dairy farm** (Mary Ellen's testimony).
- It was on Rushton Road (Mary Ellen's testimony).
- Allie and Harriet ran it.
- At least one of Allie's children (Loren) likely worked the farm at some point given his Phillips Funeral Home / South Lyon connection.
- Leslie had moved to Ruth, MI (Huron County, ~140 miles northeast of South Lyon) by 1990 at the latest. He was not running the farm.
- Lois (Smith) married Chuck Smith — who is in real estate / development / contracting, not dairy farming. Chuck likely did not take over the farm operationally.

### 5.2 Probable sale timeframe

The most likely window is **1960s–1980s**. Three signals:

1. **Editha Read Dean's 1980 death.** If Allie pre-deceased Editha, the farm probably passed to his children well before that. If Allie post-deceased Editha, the sale could be in the 1980s.
2. **Decline of small Michigan dairy farms in the 1960s–1980s.** This is the canonical period when family dairy operations in southeastern Michigan were sold to developers as Detroit suburbs pushed outward. Green Oak Township sits on the M-23 / I-96 corridor — directly in the path of suburban expansion.
3. **The Rushton Rd corridor today.** Modern aerial views show the corridor as semi-rural with scattered residential parcels and one wholesale nursery operation (Rushton Farms) — consistent with a former farm subdivided in the late-20th century.

### 5.3 How to close this

**Livingston County Register of Deeds** (200 E. Grand River, Howell, MI 48843) maintains an online tract index searchable by name. A search under grantor "Read" or "Read, Albert" in Green Oak Township will surface every property transaction the Read family was party to. The Register of Deeds online portal is the canonical primary source.

Likely deed history pattern:
1. Original land patent / acquisition by an earlier Read (Allie's grandfather or earlier — possibly mid-19th-century settlement).
2. Inheritance to Albert Sr.
3. Inheritance to Allie / Albert Jr.
4. Sale by Allie or his heirs (Loren, Lois, Leslie) to a third party — likely a developer or successor farmer.
