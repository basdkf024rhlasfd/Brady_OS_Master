## 2. Why the prior files were wrong

I was reading FamilySearch app **relationship-calculator screenshots** as if they showed direct biological descent. They don't — FamilySearch's "9th great-grandfather" label includes cousin-relationships, not just direct ancestry. The label "9TH GREAT-GRANDFATHER" can mean either lineal ancestor OR shared-ancestor cousin. I should have run "View Relationship" on each candidate before treating them as direct ancestors. I did not. **Files 11, 12, 13 were built on that mistaken assumption and overstated the FFV ancestor cluster.**

The genealogy-research skill's convention #1 is "Folklore is a hypothesis, not a fact." That convention is hereby extended: **FamilySearch screenshots are a hypothesis, not a fact, until verified through the relationship-calculator descent path.** Updating the engine skill's conventions accordingly.
