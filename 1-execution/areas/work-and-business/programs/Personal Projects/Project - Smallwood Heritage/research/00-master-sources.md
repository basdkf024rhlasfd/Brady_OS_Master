## 2. Free public sources

### Federal
- **FamilySearch.org** — ARK pattern: `https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/...`. Brady has accounts; tree is synced. Free for census, vital records.
- **Library of Congress** — Smallwood family files; Northern Neck VA papers; American Memory digital collections
- **National Archives (NARA)** — Revolutionary War pension files (James Smallwood b. 1755 would have been age 21 in 1776 — check); Civil War service records (Henry James Smallwood b. 1832, Hamilton Osborn Smallwood b. 1864 was a postwar child but check father)
- **Find a Grave** — Augusta and Rockingham county cemeteries: Riverview (Waynesboro), Pleasant Valley Brethren (Weyers Cave), Middle River Brethren (New Hope), Summit Brethren (Centerville), Oak Lawn (Bridgewater)

### Virginia
- **Library of Virginia (LVA), Richmond** — same archive as Monacan project. Cross-engagement trip planning makes sense (Augusta + Rockbridge + Stafford + King George + Spotsylvania county materials all under one roof). Accession indices to pull:
  - Stafford County deed books, will books, court order books 1750–1800
  - King George County records 1780–1800 (James Smallwood d. 15 JAN 1792 King George — probate likely)
  - Spotsylvania County records 1780–1820 (Catherine Clark d. 1820 Spotsylvania)
  - Rockingham County deed and chancery records 1830–1900
  - Augusta County deed and chancery records 1860–1950
- **Virginia Historical Society / Virginia Museum of History and Culture, Richmond** — family papers; Carter of Corotoman papers (relevant if H3 holds)
- **Library of Virginia chancery causes online database** — full-text search: search "Smallwood" in Stafford, King George, Spotsylvania, Rockingham, Augusta
- **Virginia Memory** — LVA's digital collections; Northern Neck Land Office grants (James Smallwood land in Stafford or King George?)

### County
- **Augusta County Historical Society, Staunton** — Joseph Waddell *Annals of Augusta County* (free at Internet Archive); cemetery transcriptions; Brethren community records
- **Rockingham County Historical Society, Dayton** — Nancy B. Hess, *The Heartland: Rockingham County* (1976); Brethren church records; Mount Crawford / Weyers Cave / Mount Sidney township histories
- **Stafford County Historical Society** — colonial-era records; Aquia Episcopal Church records
- **King George County clerk's office / online land records** — James Smallwood probate

### Brethren community archives
- **Brethren Historical Library and Archives, Elgin IL** — denominational HQ; church minutes; biographical files. Will lookup by mail. Email reference desk first.
- **Bridgewater College Alexander Mack Memorial Library, Bridgewater VA** — Brethren in Virginia archive; Mack collection; congregational histories. Bridgewater is in Rockingham — directly relevant.
- **Eastern Mennonite University Menno Simons Historical Library, Harrisonburg VA** — Anabaptist / Brethren overlap; Shenandoah Valley German records
- Three specific congregations to query for membership/baptism/cemetery records:
  - Pleasant Valley Church of the Brethren, Weyers Cave VA
  - Middle River Church of the Brethren, New Hope VA
  - Summit Church of the Brethren, Centerville (near Stuarts Draft) VA

### German-American
- **Palatines to America** society — German immigration; surname Knicely / Kneisel
- **Pennsylvania German Society** — many Shenandoah Valley Brethren came south from PA (Lancaster, Berks counties) in 1740s–1770s; PA records may hold the immigrant generation

### Genealogy databases (free tier)
- **WikiTree** — `Smallwood-` profile cluster; check for collaborative tree on James Smallwood (1755 Stafford)
- **Find a Grave** — already mentioned; also check virtual cemetery feature for compiled "Smallwood of Stafford VA" lists
- **USGenWeb** — county-level volunteer genealogy sites for Stafford, King George, Spotsylvania, Rockingham, Augusta
- **Internet Archive** — out-of-print county histories, Brethren historical journals (*Brethren Life and Thought*, *Pennsylvania Folklife*)

## 6. Hypotheses to verify or kill

(Mirror of PROJECT.md, restated for Phase 2 agents.)

1. **H1 (load-bearing): Stafford/King George VA Smallwoods descend from Col. James Smallwood (1639) of Charles Co MD.** Owner: bridge-specialist agent (file `02-md-to-va-migration-bridge.md`). Resolution = Stafford/King George VA records 1750–1800 cross-ref'd with Charles Co MD records same period.
2. **H2: James Smallwood (1755) is a separately-arrived immigrant or a different colony's line.** Same agent, opposite finding.
3. **H3: Catherine Clark (1756 Stafford) connects to FFV Carter-Corotoman gentry network.** Owner: identity reconciler agent (file `05-family-identity.md`). Resolution = Stafford/King George Clark family records, marriage bonds, Carter family papers at Virginia Historical Society.
4. **H4: Endogamous cousin marriage of Elijah Gold Knicely + Cora Jane Knicely is documented in Brethren church records.** Owner: religious/cultural agent (file `04-religious-cultural-context.md`). Resolution = Middle River + Summit + Pleasant Valley church records.
5. **H5: Knicely surname is anglicized German Kneisel/Kneisle/Kneisly, Palatinate origin via PA migration.** Same agent. Resolution = German-American surname references; PA Mennonite/Brethren immigration records.
6. **H6: Smallwood / Knicely / Hess / Johnson families took the pacifist Brethren stance during the Civil War.** Owner: erasure specialist agent (file `03-political-legal-context.md`). Resolution = Brethren church minutes; Confederate conscription exemption records (LVA).
7. **H7: The line was caught up in Plecker-era racial classification scrutiny (Augusta/Rockingham was in Plecker's zone).** Same erasure-specialist agent. Resolution = Plecker correspondence indices same as Monacan project.
8. **H8: Royalty descent claims in Jean Carter Smallwood book apply to Brady.** Downstream of H1 — answer that one first. If H1 holds, then individual claims need independent verification (per genealogy-research convention #11 — compiled trees are hypothesis, not fact).

## 7. Out of scope (intentional)

- **The Carter line.** Jean's book is half Carter; Brady's project is Smallwood. The Catherine Clark → possible Carter-Corotoman thread is a Phase 2 footnote, not a separate engagement.
- **The Loudoun Co. VA Smallwoods.** Jean's book documents these (Bayne / Beane / George / John Wesley → George Washington → Elmer Eugene). They're Brady's distant cousins at most; not his direct ancestors.
- **General William Smallwood (1732, Maryland governor).** Famous, well-documented, and a grand-nephew of Col. James — but only relevant to Brady's project if H1 holds AND the specific bridge generation can be tied to Col. James's particular grandchildren. Don't get distracted.
- **Royalty appendices in Jean's book** until H1 resolves. Pre-spending research time on Charlemagne descent for a line that may not even connect to Col. James is the genealogy-research convention #11 trap.
