# 07 — Sources and Original Rolls

Trip-ready accession list. Organized by archive. Designed to make the **already-planned Library of Virginia (LVA) Monacan trip** multi-purpose by adding all Smallwood-relevant pulls, and to identify which other archives need separate trips, mail-in lookups, or interlibrary loan.

**Conventions in this file:**
- "VERIFY at desk" = catalog reference is from LVA's published collection guides and finding aids, but the specific microfilm reel or call number must be confirmed at the reading-room reference desk before pulling. LVA's catalog is at `https://www.lva.virginia.gov/` (main) and `https://lva.primo.exlibrisgroup.com/` (Primo discovery).
- "ARK" = FamilySearch Archival Resource Key, a permanent URL of pattern `https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/...`
- Cost estimates are 2026 baseline; confirm at point of order.
- Online-first work is broken out in §9 — do that BEFORE the trip so you arrive at LVA with sharper questions.

### 1A. Stafford County VA records, 1750–1800

**CRITICAL CONTEXT:** Stafford County Courthouse burned **April 1862** during the Civil War. Most pre-1865 deed books, will books, and order books were destroyed. What survives is fragmentary: copies returned from the Virginia State Land Office, copies pulled into chancery suits later, vestry book copies held by the Episcopal diocese, and a small number of "rescued" original loose papers. **Plan accordingly — Stafford research is salvage work, not exhaustive search.**

### 1F. Northern Neck VA Land Office grants, 1745–1795

The Northern Neck Proprietary (Lord Fairfax's grant) covered Stafford and King George counties; land grants from 1690 to 1862 were issued by the Proprietor's Office, not the colony's General Land Office. These are separately indexed.

| Northern Neck Surveys (loose) | LVA — Northern Neck Surveys, indexed by surveyor's name and county | Loose papers | Surveyor's notes may name parties present at chain-pulling; sometimes names neighbors and creek/run boundaries that locate the family in modern terms |
