## Correction 2 — Charles Richard Smallwood burial location

**Prior claim** (in PROJECT.md and GEDCOM):
- Buried: Piedmont, Augusta VA (1977)

**Corrected claim:**
- Buried: **Middle River Church of the Brethren Cemetery, New Hope, Augusta VA**

**Source:** Find a Grave + Brethren community records via `10-verbatim-extracts-familysearch-findagrave.md`. Charles Richard moved to New Hope in 1941. After Ethel May Knicely died 1949, he remarried Ellen Bunch Pittman on 11 Mar 1955 and lived out his life in the Brethren community until his 1977 death.

**Why this matters:** Middle River Brethren is the same cemetery where Ethel May Knicely (his first wife) is buried (1949), Elijah Gold Knicely (his father-in-law) is buried (1955), and other Knicely-line ancestors. **Charles Richard chose burial in his wife's Brethren community, not in his patrilineal Piedmont family plot.** That is itself a religious-identity finding — by 1977 Charles Richard had identified more strongly with the Knicely-Brethren community than the Smallwood-Methodist patriline. Add to PROJECT.md and consider for whitepaper.

## Correction 3 — Hamilton Osborn Smallwood Sr. religious affiliation

**Prior claim** (implicit in PROJECT.md and 04-religious-cultural-context.md):
- Smallwood patriline likely Methodist Episcopal South / Anglican; Brethren came in via Charles Richard's 1922 marriage to Ethel May Knicely.

**Corrected claim:**
- **Hamilton Osborn Smallwood Sr (b. 1864) was a Brethren CONVERT** — joined the Church of the Brethren "in early manhood" per his obituary.
- Hamilton + Ida transferred their Brethren membership from Middle River (New Hope) to Pleasant Valley (Weyers Cave) in 1923 when they moved to Weyers Cave.
- Hamilton was BLIND for the last ~10 years of his life (~1928–1938), per obituary.

**Source:** Hamilton Osborn Smallwood obituary captured by Phase 3 agent in `10-verbatim-extracts-familysearch-findagrave.md`. 

**Why this matters:** This pushes the Smallwood-side Brethren conversion back ONE generation — from Charles Richard's 1922 marriage to **Hamilton's adult conversion in the 1880s**. The Brethren community was already the Smallwood family's religious home before Charles Richard married into the Knicely line. The 1922 Hagerstown marriage was a community-internal Brethren marriage, not a confessional bridge. File 04 (religious/cultural) should be amended; the whitepaper should reflect this.

The blindness detail is biographical color worth preserving in the whitepaper.

## Correction 4 — Catherine Clark's parents and origin

**Prior hypothesis (H3) in 00-master-sources.md:**
- Catherine Clark may trace to FFV Carter-Corotoman gentry network.

**Corrected finding:**
- Catherine Clark's parents are **Charles Clark + Phebe Rebecca (Goldsmith) Clark** of **Caroline County VA** (NOT Westmoreland or Lancaster, the Carter-Corotoman counties).
- This is a yeoman / middling-planter Clark line, NOT FFV gentry.
- Charles Clark was a documented landholder in Spotsylvania who SOLD ~332 acres to James Smallwood (1755) — the same land James held at his 1792 death.

**Source:** Find a Grave memorial for Catherine Clark + Spotsylvania Deed Book chain documented in `10-verbatim-extracts-familysearch-findagrave.md` and `12-lva-chancery-and-county-histories.md`.

**Why this matters:**
- H3 (Carter-Corotoman) is **refuted**. The Carter family in Brady's tree is NOT via Catherine Clark.
- H3 is replaced by a stronger finding: **the Smallwood-Clark connection was an intentional alliance**, not chance. Charles Clark sold land to his future son-in-law James Smallwood, witnessed his will, and served as executor along with Catherine. This was a tight Clark-Smallwood family compact in colonial Spotsylvania. The whitepaper should treat this as a documented alliance, not a footnote.

## Correction 6 — Cora Jane's maiden name

**Prior claim** (PROJECT.md, 00-master-sources.md):
- Cora Jane (b. 1877 Rockingham, d. 1956 Baltimore) — maiden name assumed to be **Knicely** based on GEDCOM linking her parents to F63 (Adam W. Kniceley + Martha Loker), which would have made Elijah Gold Knicely + Cora Jane Knicely a cousin marriage (H4).

**Corrected status:**
- Cora Jane's maiden name is **STILL UNCONFIRMED** by primary sources. Find a Grave does not name her parents. The GEDCOM linkage may be incorrect — there is no independent verification that her parents were Adam Kniceley + Martha Loker.
- Elijah Gold Knicely's parents are confirmed (via his obituary) as **George Robert Knicely + Sarah Amanda Wiseman** — NOT Adam Kniceley + Martha Loker.

**What this means for H4:**
- The "Elijah + Cora Jane = cousin marriage" hypothesis depended on Cora Jane being a Kniceley. With her surname unconfirmed and Elijah's parents now distinct from Adam Kniceley, **the cousin-marriage hypothesis is not supported by primary evidence**.
- It's plausible they were second cousins (both lines descend from the same earlier Kneisly immigrant generation), but this requires a Brethren-community archive pull at Mack Library / Brethren Historical Library to confirm.

**Source:** `10-verbatim-extracts-familysearch-findagrave.md` + `04-religious-cultural-context.md`.

**Resolution path:** Pull Cora Jane's birth record or 1880 Rockingham census household to confirm her parents. Single census lookup resolves it.

## Correction 8 — Migration pattern revealed (Spotsylvania → Preston Co. WV → Rockingham VA)

**Prior framing:**
- Migration trajectory was: Stafford/King George VA (Northern Neck) → Spotsylvania VA → Rockingham VA → Augusta VA. Implicit: continuous overland migration.

**Corrected finding:**
- Three of four children of James Smallwood (1792 Spotsylvania) were **born in Preston County, WV** (then VA, separated 1863).
- Henry James (1831/32) was born back in Virginia (likely Rockingham or returned from Preston).
- The line did a **Spotsylvania → Preston Co. WV → reverse migration to Rockingham VA** loop.

**Source:** `12-lva-chancery-and-county-histories.md`.

**Why this matters:** The Preston Co. WV detour is a significant geographic and cultural waystation. Preston Co. (then in Virginia, on the Pennsylvania border) was heavily German Brethren. **This is plausibly where the Smallwood patriline first joined the Brethren community** — one or two generations before Hamilton Osborn's documented "joined in early manhood" conversion. Whitepaper should investigate.
