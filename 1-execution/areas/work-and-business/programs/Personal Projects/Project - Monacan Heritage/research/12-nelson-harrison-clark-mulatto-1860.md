## Why this matters

Until this finding, the documented Native-classification evidence in Brady's biological line was on the maternal Cash/Sorrells/Cooper side (William Wesley Cooper b. 1805, Mulatto in 1850 census). The paternal Clark line had no direct census-level race classification confirmed — only Joseph Anderson Clark's 1906 marriage on the joepayne.org informal Monacan marriage list, and the Plecker-era 1943 surname inclusion.

**This 1860 census entry confirms Brady's direct paternal Clark line was officially classified as Mulatto in federal records 64 years before Plecker.** That is documentary evidence of mixed-race / Native ancestry in the actual paternal Clark surname line, not just adjacent or by marriage.

## The Ware/Wane name correction

The 1819 marriage record (FamilySearch ARK 1:1:668H-KVWQ) records Nelson Harrison Clark's second wife as **"Edy Ware"**, not "Edith Wane" as the GEDCOM/RootsMagic shows.

> "Virginia, County Marriage Records, 1771-1989", FamilySearch (https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:668H-KVWQ : Thu Mar 07 17:12:21 UTC 2024), Entry for Nelson H. Clark and Edy Ware, 29 Jun 1819.

So the actual primary-source surname is **WARE**, not Wane (or Wayne). The GEDCOM transcription drift went Ware → Wane in the FamilySearch tree. There's a contested merge note on WikiTree (Ware-4329 vs Wane-37) reflecting community disagreement.
