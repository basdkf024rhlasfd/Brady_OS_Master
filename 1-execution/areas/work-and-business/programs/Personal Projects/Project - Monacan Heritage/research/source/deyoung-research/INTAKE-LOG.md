## Summary

Marcene's research is a solid European-ancestry pedigree out to ~14 generations, anchored in FamilySearch primary sources. Where it overlaps with our existing research, it confirms the core line back to Joseph Carter Clark Sr. (1797). Where it diverges, the differences are minor and reconcilable. Where it is silent, the silences are on the Plecker-era documentary record — primary records that exist and that have been pulled separately into our research.

The file does **not** explicitly claim the family has no Native American heritage. It simply omits the documents that point in that direction.

## LACKING — primary records that exist and that Deyoung's file is silent on

These are not in Marcene's research and should be cross-referenced separately when the Deyoung material is integrated. Full treatment of each lives in the file noted.

- **1876 *James Clark v. J. P. Moore*** — Rockbridge County mandamus case, Library of Virginia accession **1140710**. The earliest documented legal challenge by this Clark line to Virginia's racial-classification regime. See `02-plecker-era.md`, `08-primary-source-extracts.md`, and `deliverables/earlier-generations-deep-dive.md` §2.
- **1924–25 *Sorrells v. Shields*** — Rockbridge County Circuit Court, Library of Virginia accession **1140744 / 1164679**. Atha Sorrells (1904–1979), a Clark cousin descended from Joseph Carter Clark Sr. (1797 — the same patriarch in Marcene's spreadsheet), sued the county clerk under the Racial Integrity Act, with Walter Plecker testifying for the defense. Judge Holt ruled for Sorrells. Plecker, in a letter to Shields dated 9 May 1925 (Library of Virginia accession **1160754**), advised against appeal because if the Virginia Supreme Court upheld the ruling, it would "embolden the Rockbridge tribe." See `02-plecker-era.md` and `deliverables/earlier-generations-deep-dive.md` §4.
- **1860 federal census, Amherst County, VA** — Nelson Harrison Clark (Brady's 6x-great-grandfather) classified Mulatto. Full household enumeration in `12-nelson-harrison-clark-mulatto-1860.md`.
- **1850 federal census, Rockbridge County, District 51, household 975** — William Wesley Cooper household classified Mulatto. See `06-dna-analysis.md` and `11-gedcom-corrected-paternal-line.md`.
- **1906 Amherst County marriage register** — Joseph Anderson Clark + Mary Susan Clark classified colored. Source: Olson *Crossroads* (2004), William S. Tanner letter to Eugene Larsen 1 May 1951. See `03-mormon-conversion.md` and `08-primary-source-extracts.md`.
- **1911 LDS baptism of Joseph Anderson Clark** on Irish Creek, part of the missionary circuit that began with Peter Mason's 1883 baptism on Pedlar Creek. See `03-mormon-conversion.md` and `deliverables/earlier-generations-deep-dive.md` §3.
- **1943 Plecker reclassification list** — "List of Mixed Negroid Virginia Families Striving to Pass as 'Indian' or White," Bureau of Vital Statistics letterhead, distributed to all Virginia county clerks. Rockbridge County entry includes the Clark surname. Held at the Library of Virginia. See `02-plecker-era.md`.
- **1951 LDS priesthood-ordination case** for Claude Clark, decided in favor of ordination on the basis of a genealogical brief assembled by Eugene Larsen (Branch President, Buena Vista LDS Branch), William S. Tanner (LDS Church Genealogical Society), and two unnamed Utah genealogical missionaries. See `03-mormon-conversion.md` and `deliverables/earlier-generations-deep-dive.md` §5.
- **2018 federal recognition of the Monacan Indian Nation** — P.L. 115-121, Thomasina E. Jordan Indian Tribes of Virginia Federal Recognition Act, signed January 30, 2018. See `01-history-and-current.md`.
