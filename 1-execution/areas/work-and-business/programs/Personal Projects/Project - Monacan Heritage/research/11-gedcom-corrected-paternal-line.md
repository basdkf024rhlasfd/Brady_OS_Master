## Other key paternal-line corrections from the GEDCOM

### Mary Holivey "Mondula" was born in SCOTLAND, not a Native Virginia surname

The "Mondula" surname mystery is now resolved. **Mary Holivey Mondula (@I528) was born 6 FEB 1820 in Scotland**, died 2 APR 1908 in Nelson, Virginia. Her parents:

- **William Menduel** (@I939) — born ~1790, North Carolina
- **Polly Stokesbury** (@I938) — born ~1790, North Carolina

So Mondula → Menduel — a transcription drift. The surname is Scottish/Northern English in origin (possibly French or Cornish), not Native. **The earlier speculation that Mondula might be a garbled Native surname is wrong.** This is European ancestry on Mary Martha Robertson's mother's side.

Polly Stokesbury's father (@I1473) is documented as having married in 1778 in the **Diocese of Ossory, Leinster, Ireland** (Family F600) — so there's confirmed Irish ancestry one generation back from Polly Stokesbury.

This also confirms Brady's earlier "Cherokee and Irish" family folklore — at least the *Irish* half. The Mondula-Menduel-Stokesbury line is Scottish via Ireland via North Carolina.
