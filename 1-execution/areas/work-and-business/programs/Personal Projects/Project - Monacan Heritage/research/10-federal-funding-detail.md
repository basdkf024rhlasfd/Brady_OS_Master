## Gap 1 — Treasury CARES Coronavirus Relief Fund + ARPA SLFRF allocations

**Finding (CARES CRF, FY 2020):** *Not in indexed public record.* Treasury published the April 30, 2021 tribal allocation table (`Allocations-to-Tribal-Governments-April-30-2021.pdf`) but the file did not render in any fetch attempt and no third-party source quoted Monacan's line. MIN was newly federally recognized in Jan 2018, so it would have been eligible for both Title I ($1B equal share, ~$1.78M per eligible tribe) and Title V (the larger $7B+ population/employment-weighted share). MIN is included in Treasury's master tribal recipient lists (BIA list of 574 federally recognized tribes), but per-tribe dollar lines were not retrieved.

**Finding (ARPA SLFRF, 2021–2022):** *Not in indexed public record at the per-tribe level.* Treasury structured the $20B tribal SLFRF as: $1B equal allocation among eligible tribes (~$1.74M per tribe), $12.35B enrollment-based, $6.65B employment-based with a $1M employment minimum for tribes that submitted 2019 employment data. With ~2,000 enrolled citizens, MIN's enrollment slice would have been small; the $1M employment minimum likely floored their take. **No published source confirms the exact MIN line.** Source: [Treasury Tribal Government Allocation Methodology](https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/136/Tribal-Government-Allocation-Methodology.pdf) [cred 5].

**Pandemic Oversight (PRAC) dashboard:** PRAC's tribal SLFRF recipient dashboard only includes tribes with awards >$30M. **MIN does not appear** — confirms MIN's SLFRF allocation was below $30M (consistent with a small, newly-recognized tribe). Source: [PRAC interactive dashboards](https://pandemicoversight.gov/data-interactive-tools/data-stories/how-tribes-are-spending-slfrf-money) [cred 5].

**Litigation context:** A 2022 federal lawsuit (dismissed 2023, Werkheiser et al. v. Branham et al., USDC W.D. Va. Lynchburg division) alleged 9 plaintiffs were "wrongfully denied more than $55,000 collectively" in benefits including CARES Act money and ~"$91,600 in federal funds" — these are individual-distribution figures, **not** the tribal allocation. Sources: [WSET ABC13](https://wset.com/news/abc13-investigates/monacan-indian-nation-federal-lawsuit-dispute-fake-elections-allegations-cares-act-federal-funding-mismanagement-bobby-thompson-jr-chief-kenneth-branham-greg-werkheiser-amherst-county-virginia-july-2022) [cred 4], [News Advance](https://newsadvance.com/news/local/judge-dismisses-federal-lawsuit-against-monacan-indian-nation-as-part-of-inner-tribal-dispute/article_cc4bf418-0ac2-11ee-8079-47ee2906dfc8.html) [cred 4].

**What this means:** MIN received both CARES CRF and ARPA SLFRF as a federally recognized tribe, but the per-tribe lines remain in unindexed Treasury PDFs. Best public floor estimates: ~$1.78M (CARES Title I share) + ~$1.74M (ARPA equal share) + ~$1M (ARPA employment minimum) = ~**$4.5M floor**, with actual likely modestly higher. **Use these only as floor estimates labeled "not officially published."**

## Gap 2 — EPA CPRG Phase 1 (Planning) grant

**Finding:** MIN was selected as a CPRG Phase 1 planning grantee, EPA Region 3, with status **"PENDING"** as of EPA's August 16, 2023 grant-status table — meaning selected but award not yet executed at that snapshot. Award subsequently executed under EPA assistance agreement number **95320301** (referenced in MIN's Priority Climate Action Plan, April 2024). **The exact dollar amount is not in indexed public record at the per-tribe level**, but the EPA program guidance set the per-single-tribe ceiling at **up to $500,000** (groups of 2+ tribes up to $1M).

**Best public-record number:** Up to **$500,000** ceiling per individual tribe; actual MIN line not officially published. Sources: [EPA CPRG Planning Grant Award Status, Aug 16 2023 (PDF)](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2023-08/CPRG_Planning_Grant_Award_Status_16Aug2023.pdf) [cred 5]; [Monacan Indian Nation PCAP (April 2024)](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2024-04/monacan-indian-nation-pcap.pdf) [cred 5]; [EPA CPRG Planning Grants Program Guidance for Tribes (March 2023)](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2023-02/EPA%20CPRG%20Planning%20Grants%20Program%20Guidance%20for%20Tribes-Tribal%20Consortia-Territories%2003-01-2023.pdf) [cred 5].

**What this means:** MIN built and published a full Priority Climate Action Plan in April 2024 — credible technical lift for a 2,000-person tribe with a 25-mile service area. Funded under IRA Sec. 60114.

## Gap 6 — USDA LFPA — Walut Pí:se ("Food is Good")

**Finding (HARD NUMBER):** USDA Agricultural Marketing Service cooperative agreement **AM23LFPA0000C005** — **$783,063** — to Monacan Indian Nation. Period of performance: **Nov 1, 2022 – Oct 31, 2024** (24 months). 100% federal funding, $0 non-federal match. CFDA 10.182. **Status: Complete (100% as of July 3, 2025).** Source: [HigherGov: Grant AM23LFPA0000C005](https://www.highergov.com/grant/AM23LFPA0000C005/) [cred 3, sourced from USAspending].

**Project scope:** Purchase and distribute local food targeting socially disadvantaged farmers/ranchers; partnerships with 19 local food distributors; ~425 families served; ~1,300 nutritious meals/month. Sources: [USDA AMS press release](https://www.ams.usda.gov/press-release/usda-takes-steps-support-food-sovereignty-monacan-indian-nation) [cred 5]; [First Nations Development Institute disruption report](https://www.firstnations.org/news/the-face-of-disruption-impacts-of-usda-funding-cuts-on-tribal-food-systems/) [cred 4].

**Disruption note (2025):** First Nations Development Institute reported MIN absorbed an unreimbursed **$19,000** when USDA cancelled the LFPA renewal contract in early 2025; food bank director Sally Latimer projected near-term funding gap of **~$30,000**. LFPA had covered 60% of MIN's local food purchases.

**What this means:** LFPA was MIN's largest discretionary federal program win — completed full cycle, but the LFPA Plus follow-on was cut by USDA in 2025 and MIN now operates with a 60% revenue hole in the Walut Pí:se program.

## Executive summary (5 bullets, paste-ready)

- **HUD IHBG is the durable backbone.** MIN's FY 2020 IHBG formula allocation was **$329,194** with **$1,439,194** total available against $1.11M of carry-over; FY 2020 actual spend was just **$190K** (COVID-driven under-execution). This is a recurring formula stream of roughly $300–500K/year, the most predictable federal income MIN has.
- **USDA's Walut Pí:se ("Food is Good") was MIN's biggest competitive win and just got cut.** $783,063 cooperative agreement (AM23LFPA0000C005) ran Nov 2022 – Oct 2024 — fully expended, served 425 families and 1,300 meals/month via 19 local food partners. The LFPA Plus follow-on was cancelled by USDA in early 2025, leaving MIN with a $19K unreimbursed bill and a ~$30K near-term gap; LFPA had covered 60% of program food purchases.
- **EPA gave MIN technical credibility but the implementation door closed.** MIN was selected for CPRG Phase 1 (≤$500K ceiling, exact amount unpublished) and built a full Priority Climate Action Plan (April 2024). MIN was **not** among the 33 tribes selected for the September 2024 CPRG Phase 2 ($300M implementation pool). EPA also awarded MIN ~$110K in GAP funding (Nov 2020) as part of a $660K seven-VA-tribes batch.
- **HHS is the second-largest stream, running through ACF, ACL, and IHS.** TAGGS shows **$2,356,005 across FY 2024–FY 2026** — heavily weighted to ACF child care/LIHEAP ($1.54M), then ACL elder care ($259K), then IHS Special Diabetes Program for Indians ($154K). Several hundred thousand dollars per year of formula-style support.
- **Treasury COVID dollars (CARES + ARPA SLFRF) are the missing data point.** MIN was eligible for both, but Treasury's per-tribe allocation tables are not text-indexed and no third-party publication quotes MIN's line. PRAC dashboard confirms MIN's SLFRF was below $30M (the dashboard's inclusion threshold), consistent with their 2,000-citizen size. Realistic floor estimate from Treasury's published methodology — $1.78M (CARES Title I) + $1.74M (ARPA equal) + $1M (ARPA employment minimum) ≈ **~$4.5M floor**, but **none of this is officially published** at the per-tribe level. Treat as estimate, not citation.
