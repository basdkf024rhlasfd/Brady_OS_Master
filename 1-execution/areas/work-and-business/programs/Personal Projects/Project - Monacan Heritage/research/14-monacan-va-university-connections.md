# 14 — Monacan / Virginia University Connections (Named Individuals Only)

**Date:** 2026-05-07
**Scope:** Direct, named-individual Monacan connections to Virginia universities.
**Out of scope:** Non-Monacan academics who study the tribe; generic "Native programs"; land acknowledgments without a Monacan-named partner.
**Standard:** Public records only. Cite URLs. Credibility 5 = official institutional source / 4 = mainstream press / 3 = secondary aggregation.

## University of Virginia (UVA) — Charlottesville

**The single deepest Monacan-by-name connection in Virginia higher ed. UVA is built on Monacan ancestral land, and the institution has invested in named Monacan legacy infrastructure no other VA university has matched.**

### Karenne Wood (1960–2019) — alumna / faculty / namesake of UVA's most visible Monacan honors

- **Identity:** Enrolled member, Monacan Indian Nation. Daughter of Robert Edward Wood; UVA-trained linguistic anthropologist and poet. (Cred 5)
- **Degree:** PhD in Anthropology, UVA Graduate School of Arts and Sciences, **2016** — first Monacan ever to earn a doctorate at UVA. Dissertation: *"The Language Ghost: Linguistic Heritage and Collective Identity Among the Monacan Indians of Central Virginia."* Advisors: Eve Danziger, Jeffrey Hantman, Lise Dobrin, Christian McMillen. (Cred 5 — UVA LibraETD record)
- **UVA roles:** Taught in UVA Department of Anthropology; directed Virginia Indian Programs at Virginia Humanities (a UVA-affiliated entity) for 11 years.
- **Memorial portrait:** Hangs in **Shannon Library** as part of UVA's Fine and Decorative Arts Collection. Painted by Esther Candari (who also created the Monacan mural in Amherst). Depicts Wood in regalia in front of Bear Mountain. Unveiled by President Jim Ryan. (Cred 5)
- **Memorial bench:** Outside **New Cabell Hall**, part of the UVA Memorial Benches Initiative honoring underrepresented contributors. (Cred 5)
- **Published archives:** Two poetry collections (*Markings on Earth*, 2001; *Weaving the Boundary*, 2016). Much of her work preserved in Virginia Humanities' Discovery Virginia archive and the Virginia Indian Archive (which she produced). No dedicated processed paper collection located at Albert and Shirley Small Special Collections Library as of this scan — possible drift to investigate.

### Teresa Pollak — Monacan tribal member

- Documented public participant at the Karenne Wood portrait unveiling ceremony at UVA, identified in UVA Today as a member of the Monacan Indian Nation. (Cred 5)
- Role beyond ceremony participation not surfaced in this scan.

### UVA Tribal Liaison position

- **Incumbent:** **Kody Grant** (hired June 2023, UVA's first-ever tribal liaison).
- **Tribal affiliation:** Pueblo of Isleta and Eastern Band of Cherokee Indians — **NOT Monacan**. He works with Monacan and other VA tribes but is not Monacan-enrolled. (Cred 5 — UVA Center for Community Partnerships profile)
- **Honest read:** The position itself is a Monacan-relevant institutional asset (developed in partnership with Virginia's federally recognized tribes, including Monacan), but the named individual holding it is not a Monacan tribal connection.

### NIRC (Native and Indigenous Relations Community)

- UVA-wide affinity body of faculty/staff/students. Karenne Wood is its most prominent Monacan-affiliated legacy figure. No current incumbent named-Monacan leader surfaced in this scan.

**Credibility:** UVA = strongest, deepest Monacan named connection of any VA university, anchored by Karenne Wood's complete arc (alumna → faculty → memorial).

## Virginia Tech (VT) — Blacksburg

**Second-strongest. Active Monacan staff member with named institutional role.**

### Victoria Ferguson — Monacan elder / Virginia Tech program staff

- **Identity:** Enrolled member, Monacan Indian Nation. Marshall University graduate. Self-trained historian on Yesáh (Eastern Siouan) lifeways; 25+ years of seed-keeping research. (Cred 5)
- **VT roles (current):**
  - **Program Director of Solitude** (oldest building on Virginia Tech's campus)
  - **Presidential Ambassador to Native Nations at Virginia Tech**
  - One of two **founding elders** of the **VT Indigenous Community Garden** (established 2014); leads seed-saving practices
- **Public-facing:** "Who's Hollerin'?" Speaker Series, April 2025 (VT Alumni Relations); Taubman Museum Member Hour talks; VPM/PBS feature on heirloom seed-keeping. (Cred 5)
- **This is the single most operationally embedded Monacan-by-name role at any VA university — recurring program leadership, not a one-off ceremony.**

### Tribal Match Scholarship origin

- **Origin story:** Came from a direct conversation between then-Monacan Chief **Kenneth Branham** and VT President **Tim Sands** about higher-ed financial burden on tribal members. Provides up to $2,500/year matching tribal scholarship funding 1:1. (Cred 5)
- Specific Monacan-by-name 2024–2026 recipients not publicly disclosed in this scan (scholarships generally don't publish individual names).

### AIICC director

- **Incumbent:** **Melissa Faircloth** (since 2019). Tribal affiliation: **Coharie Tribe (NC) / Eastern Band of Cherokee** per VT public materials — **NOT Monacan**. (Cred 5)

## University of Lynchburg — Lynchburg

**Active partnership signed by named Monacan chief; named scholarship recipients; geographic proximity to Bear Mountain makes this a high-leverage relationship.**

### Land acknowledgment + scholarship partnership

- **Signed February 21, 2022** by Monacan Chief **Kenneth Branham** and U Lynchburg President Alison Morrison-Shetlar. (Cred 5 — News & Advance, WDBJ7, WSET)
- Monacan Indian Nation Scholarship — renewable four years; among the university's highest monetary awards.
- **First two recipients:** Two students from **Amherst County High School** (the high school that primarily serves the Monacan community on Bear Mountain). Names of recipients not disclosed in available press; this is the closest VA university has come to publicly named Monacan undergrads on scholarship.

### Winona R. Gear — University of Lynchburg student

- **Affiliation:** Tribal affiliation **NOT publicly stated**; cannot be confirmed Monacan-enrolled from public sources. **Label: AMBIGUOUS.**
- **Work:** Authored 2023 Student Scholar Showcase oral presentation *"Reclaiming an Identity: The Impacts of The Racial Integrity Act of 1924 on The Identity Construction of Present-Day Monacan Indians in Amherst County, Virginia."* Faculty mentor Dr. Paul McClure (Anthropology). (Cred 5 — Lynchburg Digital Showcase)
- Most substantive Monacan-focused undergraduate research output produced at any VA university this decade based on this scan.

## Sweet Briar College — Amherst County

**Active Monacan partnership with named tribal leadership; first scholarship recipient enrolled 2024.**

### Land acknowledgment plaque (September 26, 2025) + scholarship

- Plaque dedication remarks delivered by:
  - **Diane Shields** — Monacan Tribal Chief (elected June 17, 2023). Spoke at plaque ceremony AND at Founders' Day Convocation. Background includes prior records work at **Central Virginia Community College** — the only known Monacan chief with documented community-college employment history. (Cred 5 — Sweet Briar College + Monacan Nation)
  - **Edith Lou Branham Parrish** — Assistant Tribal Chief. Public remarks at plaque ceremony. (Cred 5)
- **Scholarship:** Sweet Briar–Monacan partnership scholarship; first recipient enrolled in **2024**. Recipient name not publicly released in this scan.

## Executive Summary — the 3–5 named-individual Monacan-university connections that belong in the whitepaper

1. **Karenne Wood + UVA = the anchor story.** First Monacan PhD (2016), UVA anthropology faculty, posthumous portrait in Shannon Library and memorial bench at New Cabell Hall, dissertation on Monacan linguistic heritage. No other Monacan has a comparable university legacy. UVA owns this story whether or not it actively curates it.
2. **Victoria Ferguson + Virginia Tech = the only embedded operational role.** Program Director of Solitude, Presidential Ambassador to Native Nations, co-founder of the Indigenous Community Garden. This is a recurring, paid, named institutional position held by an enrolled Monacan elder — the single strongest "Monacan in residence" relationship at any VA university today.
3. **Kenneth Branham's two-front partnership: U Lynchburg (2022 land acknowledgment + scholarship) and Virginia Tech (Tribal Match Scholarship origin via direct conversation with President Sands).** A single Monacan chief is the named architect of two of the three most consequential financial partnerships between the tribe and VA universities.
4. **Diane Shields + Edith Lou Branham Parrish at Sweet Briar (Sept 26, 2025).** Current Chief and Assistant Chief publicly co-fronting the most recent, geographically closest (Amherst County) university partnership. Active and live as of last quarter.
5. **The negative finding matters.** With ~2,000+ enrolled Monacans and Bear Mountain's proximity to multiple VA universities, the named-individual graduate / faculty / staff footprint is **roughly five people**: Wood (deceased), Ferguson, Shields, Branham Parrish, and Lou Branham — and only Ferguson holds a paid, ongoing university role. The pipeline is thinner than the ceremonial activity suggests, and that gap is itself a story the whitepaper should name plainly.
