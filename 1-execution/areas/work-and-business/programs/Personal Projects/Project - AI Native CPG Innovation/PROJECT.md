# AI Native CPG Innovation

Personal R&D project exploring AI-driven product innovation across CPG, foodservice, functional foods, and industrial categories.

## Status
Active — ongoing idea generation and pipeline management.

## What This Is
A repeatable innovation engine that generates, scores, visualizes, and tracks product ideas across multiple categories. Uses the Innovation Workshop skill to produce 20 researched concepts per run, with Canva visuals, competitive analysis, and operational complexity assessments. All ideas feed into a persistent pipeline with stage gates from trend identification through scaled business.

## Pipeline Summary
- **85 ideas** across 5 runs/sources
- **6 categories**: Premium CPG, Performance Bakery, National Pipe / B2B, Global Snacks, Fermented Snacks, Functional Beverage
- **Stage gate system**: 0 (trend) through 10 (scaled business)
- **Tracking**: Notion database + interactive HTML viewer + mception.ai portal app
