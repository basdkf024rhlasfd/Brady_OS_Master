## Sibling Surname Note (Genealogical Flag)

Gene's obituary lists siblings with surnames Weyerman, Simmons (×2), Pugh, and Lee — none are Smallwood. "Simmons" appears twice (Keith Simmons, Don Simmons). ## Assessment

Brady and his siblings were almost certainly **out-structured, not defrauded outright.** Gene moved the lion's share of his wealth into joint survivorship instruments (home, brokerage accounts) that legally bypassed the probate estate — and thus bypassed any divorce decree claim — entirely. What remained in probate was concentrated in a leveraged real estate company that Debi controlled as President, with $335K in bank debt. Whether that debt consumed the equity legitimately or through mismanagement/self-dealing is the unresolved question. The 9-year window in which Affordable Properties continued operating under Debi with no final accounting to heirs is the most suspicious element. The 2023-2024 coordinated wind-down (house sale + corporate termination) suggests everything was finally unwound, leaving nothing for anyone to examine.

**The most actionable path:** A Virginia estate attorney can file a motion to compel a final accounting if the estate was never formally closed. Whether any assets remain to recover is a different question — but the accounting itself would answer what happened.
