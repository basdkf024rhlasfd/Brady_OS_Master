## VII. Mecklenburg County Court — Access Limitations

The Virginia CJIS online system does **not** index probate/fiduciary cases. The "CWF" prefix (Commonwealth Fiduciary) falls outside the searchable case types. To access estate file CWF1500000011:

**Mecklenburg County Circuit Court Clerk**  
393 Washington Street, Boydton, VA 23917  
Phone: **(434) 738-6191**  
Hours: 8:30 AM – 5:00 PM, Mon–Fri  
Ask for: the fiduciary ledger entry for CWF1500000011, including all inventories, accountings, and any final order

Property records (deeds, trust deeds, foreclosures) are partially online at [mecklenburgcovarecords.org](https://www.mecklenburgcovarecords.org/) but post-1971 instruments require either a paid SRA subscription or in-person access.

## XI. Assessment Update

With this additional research, the picture sharpens considerably:

**Debi received at minimum $858K in non-probate transfers plus operated Gene's businesses for 9 years.** The probate estate ($451K gross) was burdened by $335K in corporate debt and real estate that apparently couldn't sell at 2015 appraised values. The children's claim sat in the probate estate; everything of real liquid value had already transferred to Debi by joint title.

**The 2023-2024 wind-down is the final chapter no one told Brady and Mike.** Affordable Properties of VA Inc. ran for 9 years after Gene died with Debi as President, handling its properties and any proceeds with no court-compelled accounting to the heirs. By the time it terminated in October 2023, whatever had been in those parcels — sales proceeds, rental income, appreciation — was effectively unaccountable to Gene's children.

**One live thread:** If the Commissioner of Accounts in Mecklenburg never approved a final accounting, the estate is technically still open. Virginia law gives the Commissioner authority to compel executors. An attorney filing a motion to compel a final accounting could still surface what happened — though the practical recovery may be minimal at this point.
