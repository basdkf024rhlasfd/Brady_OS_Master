# Work & Business

All professional identity, income generation, and value creation outside the home.

## What Winning Looks Like

A portfolio career that generates $200K+ revenue annually through a combination of board work, consulting, and entrepreneurial ventures — operated with clear governance, not hustle.

## Boundary

This ring covers work and income. It does not cover:
- Personal finance, investments, or retirement (separate ring)
- Learning for its own sake (separate ring)
- Family obligations that happen to involve money (separate ring)

## Operating Principles

1. Revenue is the scoreboard, not effort
2. No more than 2 active venture projects at any time (WIP limit)
3. Consulting and ventures are distinct franchises — services vs. products/equity
4. Board obligations are maintenance, not growth — minimize active project count
5. Career transition has a sunset — when it resolves, the Program ends
