## Sport
Venture building — identifying, validating, and building income-generating products and businesses outside of employment.

## Team
Product thinking, technical execution, market validation, go-to-market capability.

## Coach
To be assigned at Cycle review.

## Strategic Outcome
Build and operate ventures that generate revenue through products, equity, or recurring business models.

## Operating Principles
1. Ideation and execution are phases of the same pipeline, not separate activities
2. Every idea must pass a kill gate before becoming a Project (time, money, energy budget)
3. Maximum 2 active venture Projects at any time — WIP limit is non-negotiable
4. Ventures that don't show traction within a defined timeframe get killed or paused
5. If a venture becomes services work, migrate it to the Consulting Program

## Key Metrics
- Ideas evaluated per cycle
- Active venture projects (must stay at or below 2)
- Time-to-first-revenue for each venture
- Total venture revenue

## Milestones
Defined per venture project, not at Program level. Each venture Project sets its own phase-gates at creation.

## Pipeline

```
Ideate → Validate → Build → Operate → Scale or Sunset
              ↑
         Kill Gate
    (Does this pass the time/money/energy test?)
```
