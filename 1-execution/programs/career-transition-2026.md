## Sport
Career repositioning — transitioning from full-time COO to a portfolio career model.

## Team
Self-positioning, networking, opportunity evaluation, personal brand.

## Coach
To be assigned at Cycle review.

## Strategic Outcome
Land in a professional arrangement (or set of arrangements) that matches post-COO identity, desired lifestyle, and income requirements.

## Operating Principles
1. This Program has a **sunset** — when the transition is complete, the Program ends
2. "Complete" means: the portfolio model is operating and generating target income
3. Networking is the primary activity — conversations, not applications
4. Every opportunity is evaluated against the portfolio model, not against traditional employment
5. Residual activity after transition completion moves to whichever Program inherits it (Consulting, Ventures, or a new Program created at ARC level)

## Key Metrics
- Conversations with target contacts per cycle
- Opportunities evaluated
- Offers or arrangements received
- Progress toward income target

## Milestones
- Positioning defined (who am I now, to whom)
- Active in market (conversations happening regularly)
- First arrangement secured
- Portfolio model operational (sunset trigger)
