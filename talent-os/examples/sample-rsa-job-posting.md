# Retail Sales Associate

**Company:** [Furniture Retailer]
**Location:** Store location
**Job Type:** Full-time

## Job Summary

We are seeking a Retail Sales Associate to join our growing team. The ideal candidate will be
responsible for assisting customers on the sales floor, maintaining product knowledge, and helping
drive store sales. This is a great opportunity for a motivated individual to grow with an
established company.

## Responsibilities

- Greet customers and assist them on the sales floor
- Maintain knowledge of current products and promotions
- Process sales transactions accurately
- Keep the showroom clean and organized
- Meet individual and team sales goals
- Other duties as assigned

## Requirements

- High school diploma or equivalent required; associate's degree preferred
- 1-2 years of prior furniture or retail sales experience required
- Strong communication and interpersonal skills
- Ability to work in a fast-paced environment
- Must be a team player with a positive attitude
- Availability to work a flexible schedule including some weekends

## Compensation

Competitive pay based on experience. Commission opportunities available.

## Benefits

- Health insurance
- Paid time off
- Employee discount

We are an Equal Opportunity Employer.
