# Pricing Methodology

Frameworks for pricing Orlando listings — absorption rate analysis, probability-based pricing, comp methodology, seller motivation signals, showing velocity benchmarks, and price adjustment rules.

---

## Absorption Rate Analysis

The absorption rate tells you how quickly homes are selling in a micro-market. It is the single most important metric for setting list price.

### Formula

**Months of Supply = Active Listings ÷ Closed Sales per Month**

Pull from Stellar MLS or Redfin for a specific ZIP code, subdivision, or price bracket. Use the most recent 3-month rolling average for closed sales to smooth out noise.

### Thresholds

| Months of Supply | Market Type | Pricing Implication |
|-----------------|-------------|-------------------|
| Under 3 months | **Seller's market** | Price at or slightly above recent comps. Multiple offers likely. |
| 3–6 months | **Balanced market** | Price at comp average. Negotiate normally. |
| 6–9 months | **Buyer's market** | Price at or below comp average. Be prepared to negotiate. |
| 9+ months | **Distressed market** | Price aggressively below comps. Expect long DOM and concessions. |

### Micro-Market Adjustment

When pulling comps, narrow the absorption rate to:
1. **Same subdivision** (if 10+ sales in last 6 months) — most accurate
2. **Same ZIP + price bracket (±10%)** — if subdivision is too small
3. **Adjacent ZIPs + price bracket** — only if ZIP has fewer than 5 sales/month

## Probability-Based Pricing

Map list price to estimated days on market (DOM) and probability of selling within 60 days. This replaces the static CMA "suggested price" with a range showing trade-offs.

### Price-to-DOM Mapping Framework

| Price Position (vs. Comp Average) | Estimated DOM | 60-Day Sell Probability | Best When |
|-----------------------------------|--------------|------------------------|-----------|
| 5–8% below comps | 7–14 days | 90–95% | Must sell fast — relocation, divorce, estate |
| 2–5% below comps | 14–25 days | 80–90% | Want speed with fair value, balanced market |
| At comp average | 21–35 days | 65–80% | Standard pricing in seller's or balanced market |
| 2–5% above comps | 35–55 days | 40–60% | Testing the market, willing to wait, unique features |
| 5–10% above comps | 55–90 days | 15–35% | Aspirational pricing, expect price reduction |
| 10%+ above comps | 90+ days | Under 15% | Likely to go stale — not recommended |

### Adjustments by Market Type

| Factor | Seller's Market (< 3 mo) | Balanced (3–6 mo) | Buyer's Market (6+ mo) |
|--------|--------------------------|-------------------|----------------------|
| DOM multiplier | 0.7× (faster) | 1.0× (baseline) | 1.4× (slower) |
| Price ceiling | 5–8% above comps possible | At or slightly above comps | At or below comps |
| Multiple offer likelihood | High at comp average | Moderate below comps | Rare |
| Days before first reduction | 21–28 | 14–21 | 10–14 |

### Comp Spread Impact

| Comp Spread (High – Low ÷ Median) | Pricing Confidence | Strategy |
|------------------------------------|--------------------|----------|
| Under 5% | **High** — tight comps | Price confidently at or above median |
| 5–10% | **Moderate** — some variation | Price at median, let condition differentiate |
| 10–15% | **Low** — wide variation | Condition and updates matter more than comps |
| 15%+ | **Very low** — comps unreliable | Use per-sqft analysis, consider pre-appraisal |

## Stale Risk Monitoring

A listing becomes "stale" when DOM exceeds buyer expectations for the price bracket. Stale listings attract lowball offers and appraisal risk. Monitor these triggers weekly.

### DOM Thresholds by Price Bracket (Orlando Metro)

| Price Bracket | Average DOM (Healthy) | Stale Warning | Stale (Action Required) | Critical |
|--------------|-----------------------|---------------|------------------------|----------|
| Under $300K | 18 days | 25 days | 35 days | 50+ days |
| $300K–$450K | 25 days | 35 days | 50 days | 70+ days |
| $450K–$650K | 32 days | 45 days | 65 days | 90+ days |
| $650K–$1M | 45 days | 60 days | 85 days | 120+ days |
| $1M+ | 65 days | 90 days | 120 days | 180+ days |

### Showing Velocity Triggers

Combine DOM with showing trends for more accurate stale risk detection:

| Signal | Interpretation | Action |
|--------|---------------|--------|
| Showings trending up + no offers | Property is interesting but overpriced | Reduce 1–2% |
| Showings flat (3–5/week) + no offers | Priced close but something's off | Review feedback themes, consider 2–3% reduction |
| Showings declining + DOM > stale warning | Going stale — buyers are skipping it | Immediate 3–5% reduction or re-list strategy |
| Zero showings for 7+ days | Listing is invisible or severely overpriced | Emergency review: price, photos, description, agent remarks |
| Showing requests spike after reduction | Reduction was right, momentum building | Hold new price for 7–10 days before further action |

### Re-List Strategy

When DOM exceeds "Critical" threshold, consider withdrawing and re-listing rather than reducing further:
- Withdraw from MLS (must be off for **minimum 30 days** in Stellar MLS to reset DOM)
- Update photos, description, and pricing
- Re-list as "new" — resets DOM counter and triggers new-listing alerts
- **Warning**: Experienced buyer agents will check listing history. A re-list doesn't fool savvy buyers, but it does trigger automated alerts again.

## Quick Comp Methodology

A rapid pricing intelligence method for buyer agents preparing for showings. Takes 5–10 minutes per property using free tools.

### 3-Source Price Check

| Source | What to Pull | Where to Find |
|--------|-------------|---------------|
| **Zillow Zestimate** | Automated valuation | zillow.com → property page |
| **Redfin Estimate** | Automated valuation (different model) | redfin.com → property page |
| **List Price** | What the seller is asking | MLS / Zillow / Redfin listing |

### Delta Analysis

| Metric | Calculation | Flag |
|--------|------------|------|
| Zestimate vs List | (List – Zestimate) ÷ Zestimate | Flag if delta > ±5% |
| Redfin vs List | (List – Redfin) ÷ Redfin | Flag if delta > ±5% |
| AVM Average vs List | Average both AVMs, compare to list | Primary pricing signal |
| AVM Agreement | |Zestimate – Redfin| ÷ Average | If > 8%, AVMs unreliable — need manual comps |

### Interpretation

| AVM Average vs List Price | Signal | Buyer Action |
|---------------------------|--------|-------------|
| List is 5%+ below AVMs | **Potential opportunity** — underpriced or condition issues | Verify condition, offer quickly if good |
| List within ±5% of AVMs | **Fairly priced** — standard negotiation applies | Negotiate based on DOM and motivation |
| List is 5–10% above AVMs | **Overpriced** — expect negotiation room | Offer at or below AVM, cite data |
| List is 10%+ above AVMs | **Significantly overpriced** — may not appraise | Consider avoiding, or offer at AVM with appraisal contingency |

**Limitations**: AVMs (Automated Valuation Models) do not account for renovations, condition, lot premium, or view. Use as a directional signal, not a definitive price. Always compare to 3–5 recent closed comps for accuracy.

## Seller Motivation Signals

Public MLS and listing data reveal seller urgency. Scoring these signals helps buyer agents identify negotiation opportunities.

### Signal Scoring

| Signal | Points | How to Check |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| DOM > area average | +2 | MLS listing date vs area avg DOM |
| DOM > stale threshold | +4 | See Stale Risk table above |
| One price reduction | +2 | MLS price history |
| Two+ price reductions | +4 | MLS price history |
| Total reductions > 5% of original | +3 | Calculate from original to current list |
| Previously expired/withdrawn | +3 | MLS listing history — prior MLS# |
| Relisted (DOM reset) | +2 | Check listing history, tax records for prior MLS# |
| Vacant property | +3 | Agent remarks, showing instructions ("vacant, lockbox") |
| Estate / probate sale | +3 | Agent remarks, ownership records |
| Relocating seller | +2 | Agent remarks ("motivated," "relocated") |
| Divorce / court-ordered | +3 | Public records, agent remarks |
| Builder/investor seller (not owner-occupied) | +1 | Tax records — mailing address ≠ property address |
| Recent rent-back or lease-back request | +2 | Agent remarks or showing notes |
| "Bring all offers" in agent remarks | +2 | MLS agent-only remarks |

### Motivation Score Interpretation

| Total Score | Seller Motivation | Negotiation Approach |
|------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| 0–3 | **Low** — patient seller | Expect close to list price negotiations |
| 4–7 | **Moderate** — willing to deal | Offer 3–5% below list, reasonable terms |
| 8–12 | **High** — motivated to sell | Offer 5–10% below list, ask for concessions |
| 13+ | **Very high** — distressed or desperate | Aggressive offer warranted, 10–15% below list possible |

### Red Flags (Seller May Be Difficult)

- Multiple relists with same or higher price (unrealistic expectations)
- Agent remarks "seller will not negotiate" or "firm on price"
- Freshly listed at above-AVM price in buyer's market
- Listing agent is also the owner

## Showing Velocity Benchmarks

Expected showings per week by price bracket. Use to evaluate whether a listing is getting adequate exposure.

### Showings per Week (First 14 Days on Market)

| Price Bracket | Hot (Seller's Mkt) | Normal (Balanced) | Slow (Buyer's Mkt) | Concern Threshold |
|--------------|--------------------|--------------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Under $300K | 10–15+ | 6–10 | 3–6 | Under 3 |
| $300K–$450K | 8–12 | 5–8 | 2–5 | Under 2 |
| $450K–$650K | 5–8 | 3–5 | 1–3 | Under 1 |
| $650K–$1M | 3–5 | 2–3 | 1–2 | Under 1 |
| $1M+ | 2–3 | 1–2 | 0.5–1 | None in 14 days |

### Week-Over-Week Trend Analysis

| Pattern | Signal | Action |
|---------|--------|--------|
| Week 1 strong, Week 2 declining | Normal — initial interest spike, settling | Hold price if above concern threshold |
| Consistent 2+ weeks | Good pacing | Maintain and wait for offers |
| Declining 3+ weeks in a row | Losing momentum | Review price, photos, feedback |
| Spike after price reduction | Reduction working, renewed interest | Hold new price for 7–10 days |
| Zero showings, Week 1 | Serious problem | Verify MLS syndication, check pricing vs search filters |

### Search Filter Trap

Buyers filter by price on portals (Zillow, Redfin, Realtor.com). Common filter breakpoints:

| Filter Ceiling | If Priced at | Problem |
|---------------|-------------|---------|
| $300K | $305K | Invisible to "$200K–$300K" searchers |
| $350K | $355K | Invisible to "$300K–$350K" searchers |
| $400K | $410K | Invisible to "$350K–$400K" searchers |
| $500K | $510K | Invisible to "$400K–$500K" searchers |

**Rule**: If list price is within 3% of a common filter ceiling, price at or just below the ceiling. A $405K listing should be $399K to capture the $300K–$400K filter.

## Price Adjustment Rules

Structured guidelines for when and how much to reduce price. Removes emotion from the decision.

### Decision Framework

| Condition | Days on Market | Showings/Week | Recommended Adjustment |
|-----------|---------------|---------------|----------------------|
| Good showings, no offers | Any | Above concern threshold | Reduce 1–2% — price is close but slightly high |
| Declining showings | 14+ days | Below normal range | Reduce 2–3% — price is discouraging viewings |
| Few showings from start | 7+ days | Below concern threshold | Reduce 3–5% — significantly overpriced or poor photos |
| Stale warning reached | See table | Any | Reduce 3–5% immediately |
| Stale (action required) | See table | Any | Reduce 5–8% or consider withdraw/re-list |
| Critical DOM reached | See table | Any | Withdraw and re-list at market price |

### Reduction Rules by Price Bracket

| Price Bracket | Minimum Meaningful Reduction | Notes |
|--------------|-----------------------------|-------|
| Under $300K | $5,000 (1.5–2%) | Small reductions look desperate. Go meaningful or hold. |
| $300K–$450K | $7,500 (1.5–2.5%) | Must cross a search filter boundary to have impact |
| $450K–$650K | $10,000 (1.5–2%) | Consider rounding to filter-friendly number |
| $650K–$1M | $15,000–$25,000 (2–3%) | Luxury buyers less price-sensitive, more value-driven |
| $1M+ | $25,000–$50,000 (2–5%) | Fewer buyers at each increment, bigger moves needed |

### Timing Rules

| Situation | Recommended Timing |
|-----------|-------------------|
| First reduction | After 14–21 days with below-average showings |
| Second reduction | 14 days after first reduction if no improvement |
| Third reduction | Time to withdraw and re-list instead |
| Holiday/seasonal pause | Avoid reductions during Thanksgiving–New Year (low showing volume skews data) |
| After feedback pattern | Within 7 days of identifying consistent "overpriced" feedback |

### What NOT to Do

- **Don't reduce by $1,000–$2,000** on a $400K+ listing — it signals desperation without changing buyer behavior
- **Don't reduce every week** — looks like a falling knife. Space reductions 14+ days apart
- **Don't chase the market down** in small increments — one meaningful reduction beats three small ones
- **Don't skip the conversation** — present data (showings, feedback, comps, DOM) to seller before recommending reduction
