# Schools & Quality of Life

Education landscape and lifestyle factors that drive real estate decisions.

---

## School Districts

### Orange County Public Schools (OCPS)
- **Size**: ~210,000 students — 8th largest district in the US
- **Schools**: 200+ schools
- **Graduation rate**: ~90%
- **Overall grade**: B+ (Niche)
- **Notable**: Massive district with wide variation in school quality by zone
- **School choice**: Magnet programs, controlled open enrollment options

### Seminole County Public Schools
- **Size**: ~69,000 students
- **Overall grade**: A- (Niche) — consistently one of Florida's top districts
- **Notable**: Higher property values partially reflect school quality
- **Top schools**: Lake Mary High, Oviedo High, Hagerty High

### Osceola County Public Schools
- **Size**: ~75,000 students
- **Overall grade**: B (Niche)
- **Notable**: Rapidly growing, many new schools under construction
- **Challenge**: Growth outpacing school construction in some areas

### Lake County Schools
- **Size**: ~48,000 students
- **Overall grade**: B+ (Niche)
- **Notable**: Smaller, community-oriented, new schools in Clermont growth areas

## Climate & Weather

### Typical Weather
- **Summer (Jun-Sep)**: Hot and humid. Highs 90-95°F. Daily afternoon thunderstorms. Humidity 70-90%.
- **Fall (Oct-Nov)**: Warm and pleasant. Highs 75-85°F. Lower humidity. Hurricane season ends Nov 30.
- **Winter (Dec-Feb)**: Mild. Highs 60-75°F. Occasional cold fronts to 40s. Rare freeze events.
- **Spring (Mar-May)**: Warm and dry. Highs 75-90°F. Lowest humidity. Best weather of the year.
- **Annual rainfall**: ~52 inches (mostly summer thunderstorms)
- **Sunshine**: 233 sunny days per year (above national average)

### Hurricane Season
- **Season**: June 1 - November 30 (peak Aug-Oct)
- Orlando is **~40 miles inland** — significantly reduces storm surge risk
- Direct hits are rare (3 since 1974: Charley 2004, Frances 2004, Irma 2017)
- Main risks: wind damage, heavy rain flooding, tornadoes, power outages
- Most Orlando homes are built to Florida Building Code (post-Andrew standards)
- **Insurance** is the primary hurricane-related cost concern, not direct damage

### Climate Change Considerations
- Rising insurance costs tied to climate risk perception
- Flood zone maps being updated — some areas reclassified
- Sea-level rise doesn't directly affect inland Orlando but impacts state insurance market
- Increased intensity of storms could affect premiums long-term
- Orlando's inland position is an advantage vs. coastal Florida

## Outdoor Recreation

### Lakes
- Orlando has **100+ named lakes** within city limits
- Many neighborhoods are lakefront
- Popular activities: fishing, kayaking, paddleboarding, wakeboarding
- **Butler Chain of Lakes** (Windermere): Most prestigious lake chain
- **Lake Eola** (Downtown): Iconic fountain, swan boats, farmers market
- **Lake Toho** (Kissimmee): World-class bass fishing

### Parks & Trails
- **West Orange Trail**: 22-mile paved trail from Apopka to downtown Winter Garden
- **Cross Seminole Trail**: Connects Oviedo, Winter Springs, Lake Mary
- **Wekiva Springs State Park**: Natural springs, canoeing, hiking
- **Blue Spring State Park**: Manatee viewing, springs (45 min from Orlando)
- **Tibet-Butler Nature Preserve**: Windermere, hiking, wildlife
- **Shingle Creek Regional Trail**: South Orlando, headwaters of the Everglades

### Springs (Within Day-Trip Distance)
- Wekiwa Springs (20 min)
- Blue Spring (45 min)
- Rock Springs / Kelly Park (30 min)
- De Leon Springs (45 min)
- Rainbow Springs (90 min)
- Three Sisters Springs / Crystal River (90 min — manatees)

### Beaches (All Within ~1 Hour)

- New Smyrna Beach (East, ~60 min)
- Daytona Beach (Northeast, ~60 min)
- Clearwater Beach (West, ~90 min via I-4)

## Crime & Safety

### General Overview
- Orlando's crime rate varies dramatically by neighborhood
- Overall metro crime rate is near the national average
- Tourist areas have higher property crime rates (car break-ins)
- Violent crime concentrated in specific neighborhoods

### Safest Areas
- Winter Park, Windermere, Lake Nona, Baldwin Park, Celebration
- Lake Mary, Oviedo, Winter Springs
- Horizon West, Winter Garden
- Most master-planned communities have low crime

### Areas to Research Carefully
- Parts of Pine Hills (historically higher crime)
- Some blocks in Parramore, downtown fringe areas
- Parts of Colonial Drive corridor
- Some areas of Kissimmee
- Always check crime maps for specific addresses (CrimeMapping.com, SpotCrime)

### Gated Communities
- Very common in Orlando — especially new construction
- Gate doesn't guarantee safety but provides perception and access control
- Gated communities often command 5-10% price premium
