# Orlando Neighborhoods & Submarkets

Comprehensive profiles of every major neighborhood and submarket in the Orlando metro area.

---

## ORLANDO PROPER (City of Orlando)

### Downtown Orlando / Central Business District
- **Median price**: $250K-$450K (mostly condos/lofts)
- **Vibe**: Urban core, high-rises, nightlife, restaurants, arts district
- **Who lives here**: Young professionals, singles, DINKs (dual income no kids)
- **Notable**: Lake Eola Park, Amway Center (NBA Magic), Dr. Phillips Performing Arts Center
- **Investment**: Strong rental demand from young professionals. Condo HOA fees can be high ($400-$800/mo)
- **Walkability**: Highest in Orlando metro — 70+ Walk Score
- **Red flags**: Parking can be expensive. Some blocks still transitioning. Homeless population near certain corridors.
- **Schools**: Limited — this is not a family-oriented area

### Thornton Park
- **Median price**: $450K-$700K
- **Vibe**: Orlando's most charming walkable neighborhood. Brick streets, boutiques, cafes, wine bars
- **Who lives here**: Affluent young professionals, couples, creatives
- **Notable**: Adjacent to Lake Eola, one of Orlando's most desirable addresses
- **Walkability**: Excellent — one of the few truly walkable Orlando neighborhoods
- **Investment**: Strong appreciation, limited inventory keeps prices high
- **Red flags**: Small area, very limited supply. Some flood risk near lake.

### College Park / Edgewater
- **Median price**: $400K-$650K
- **Vibe**: Tree-lined streets, bungalows, local shops on Edgewater Drive. "Old Orlando" charm
- **Who lives here**: Families, professionals, long-time residents, artists
- **Notable**: Voted one of Orlando's best neighborhoods consistently. Strong community identity.
- **Schools**: Good elementary options. Near several magnet programs.
- **Growth**: 4-6% annual appreciation. Gentrifying steadily.
- **Red flags**: Older homes may need updates (1940s-1960s construction). Some flooding in low areas.

### Mills 50 / Mills Park
- **Median price**: $350K-$550K
- **Vibe**: Orlando's "Little Saigon" — Vietnamese restaurants, craft breweries, indie shops, arts
- **Who lives here**: Diverse mix — Vietnamese community, hipsters, young families, artists
- **Notable**: One of Orlando's most culturally vibrant corridors. Rapidly gentrifying.
- **Investment**: Strong appreciation potential as area transforms
- **Red flags**: Mixed quality block-to-block. Some areas still rough around edges.

### Audubon Park
- **Median price**: $450K-$650K
- **Vibe**: Charming Garden District. Local boutiques, community garden, farmers market
- **Who lives here**: Families, professionals, educators
- **Notable**: Adjacent to Audubon Park Garden District commercial area
- **Schools**: Audubon Park Elementary is highly rated
- **Red flags**: Older housing stock. Limited inventory.

### Baldwin Park
- **Median price**: $575,000 (6% annual growth)
- **Vibe**: New urbanist community built on former Naval Training Center. Walkable, planned, family-friendly.
- **Who lives here**: Families, professionals, military retirees
- **Notable**: Town center with shops/restaurants, multiple parks, community pools
- **Schools**: Baldwin Park Elementary is sought-after
- **Walkability**: Very good for Orlando — designed for it
- **Investment**: Strong and consistent appreciation
- **Red flags**: HOA required. Can feel "planned" vs. organic neighborhoods.

### Lake Nona
- **Median price**: $780,000 (8% YoY growth)
- **Vibe**: Master-planned tech/medical hub. Modern, amenity-rich, forward-looking.
- **Who lives here**: Medical professionals, tech workers, families, executives
- **Notable**: Medical City (VA Hospital, UCF Medical, Orlando Health), USTA campus, Town Center
- **Range**: Townhomes from mid-$300Ks, single-family $400K-$600K, luxury estates $1M+
- **Schools**: Lake Nona High School ranked 82nd in Florida. Strong new schools.
- **Growth corridor**: Massive ongoing development. Expected to add 30,000 jobs over next decade.
- **Investment**: Premium prices but strong fundamentals. $7.6B projected economic impact.
- **Red flags**: CDD fees. Long commute to downtown/west side without toll roads. Still building out — construction everywhere.

### Delaney Park
- **Median price**: $500K-$800K
- **Vibe**: Historic, quiet, residential. One of Orlando's oldest neighborhoods.
- **Who lives here**: Established families, professionals
- **Notable**: Near downtown but feels suburban. Beautiful homes.
- **Red flags**: Older homes may need significant updates.

### SODO (South of Downtown)
- **Median price**: $300K-$500K
- **Vibe**: Transitional area south of downtown. Rapidly developing.
- **Who lives here**: Young professionals, investors
- **Notable**: New SoDo shopping district, SunRail station
- **Investment**: High upside potential as area develops
- **Red flags**: Still gentrifying. Quality varies block to block.

### Parramore
- **Median price**: $200K-$350K
- **Vibe**: Historic Black neighborhood. Major revitalization underway with Creative Village.
- **Who lives here**: Long-time residents, students (UCF downtown campus)
- **Notable**: Creative Village mixed-use development, Amway Center adjacent
- **Investment**: Highest appreciation potential in Orlando proper if revitalization continues
- **Red flags**: Higher crime rates in some blocks. Rapid gentrification raises displacement concerns.

### Ivanhoe Village
- **Median price**: $400K-$650K
- **Vibe**: Antique Row, boutiques, restaurants along Orange Avenue. Lakefront.
- **Who lives here**: Creatives, professionals, couples
- **Notable**: Lake Ivanhoe, walkable commercial district
- **Red flags**: Small area, limited inventory. Traffic on Orange Ave.

### Colonialtown
- **Median price**: $400K-$600K
- **Vibe**: Quiet residential streets minutes from downtown. Mix of historic and renovated homes.
- **Who lives here**: Young families, professionals
- **Notable**: Split into North and South by Colonial Drive
- **Red flags**: Colonial Drive itself is busy/commercial. North vs South have different characters.

### Milk District
- **Median price**: $300K-$450K
- **Vibe**: Up-and-coming arts district. Breweries, murals, local shops.
- **Who lives here**: Artists, young professionals, investors
- **Notable**: Named after the old T.G. Lee dairy. Monthly Milk District Arts Market.
- **Investment**: Strong upside — still early in gentrification curve
- **Red flags**: Some areas still transitioning. Quality varies.

## ORANGE COUNTY SUBURBS

### Winter Park (32789, 32792)
- **Median price**: $654,500-$742,000 (3% annual growth)
- **Vibe**: Orlando's most prestigious suburb. Historic charm, brick streets, world-class culture.
- **Who lives here**: Affluent families, retirees, executives, old Florida money
- **Notable**: Park Avenue shopping, Rollins College, multiple museums, chain of lakes
- **Schools**: Top-rated. Winter Park High School is among best in state.
- **Walkability**: Park Avenue area is very walkable
- **Range**: Condos from $250K, average homes $500K-$900K, estates $1M-$10M+
- **Red flags**: Premium prices. Older homes in historic district can have expensive maintenance. Property taxes reflect high values.

### Maitland
- **Median price**: $400K-$600K
- **Vibe**: Quiet, lakefront suburban town between Orlando and Winter Park
- **Who lives here**: Families, professionals
- **Notable**: Maitland Art Center, multiple lakes, RDV Sportsplex (now AdventHealth)
- **Schools**: Solid. Feeds into Winter Park or Maitland Middle.
- **Investment**: Steady appreciation, good location

### Windermere (34786)
- **Median price**: $782,500
- **Vibe**: Luxury lakefront living. Privacy, space, prestige.
- **Who lives here**: Athletes (NBA/NFL players), executives, celebrities, wealthy families
- **Notable**: Isleworth Golf & Country Club (Tiger Woods' former home), Butler Chain of Lakes
- **Range**: Entry at ~$500K, typical $800K-$2M, ultra-luxury $3M-$20M+
- **Schools**: Windermere High School ranked 65th in FL. Top-rated elementaries.
- **Red flags**: Very spread out, car-dependent. Some areas have long commutes. HOA + CDD fees in newer communities.

### Dr. Phillips
- **Median price**: $500K-$800K
- **Vibe**: Upscale suburb with Restaurant Row. International food scene.
- **Who lives here**: Affluent families, international residents (large Brazilian, Latin American community)
- **Notable**: Restaurant Row on Sand Lake Road, close to Universal/I-Drive
- **Schools**: Dr. Phillips High School is diverse and well-regarded
- **Investment**: Stable. Mature community with little new development.

### Bay Hill
- **Median price**: $600K-$1.5M
- **Vibe**: Golf community, Arnold Palmer's legacy. Country club living.
- **Who lives here**: Golfers, retirees, executives
- **Notable**: Arnold Palmer's Bay Hill Club, PGA Tour event

### Belle Isle
- **Median price**: $350K-$550K
- **Vibe**: Small town feel, surrounded by lakes, near Orlando airport
- **Who lives here**: Families, working professionals
- **Notable**: Conway Chain of Lakes. Technically an independent city.
- **Red flags**: Some flood risk near lakes. Flight path noise in some areas.

### Hunters Creek
- **Median price**: $380K-$500K
- **Vibe**: Large master-planned community from the 1990s. Family-oriented.
- **Who lives here**: Diverse families, Hispanic community, working professionals
- **Notable**: Large HOA community with pools, parks, commercial areas
- **Schools**: Hunters Creek area schools are decent, diverse
- **Investment**: Stable, affordable entry to south Orange County

### Horizon West
- **Median price**: $550,000 (7% annual growth)
- **Vibe**: Orlando's fastest-growing submarket. New everything — homes, schools, shopping.
- **Who lives here**: Young families, move-up buyers, remote workers
- **Villages**: Hamlin (town center), Lakeside, Bridgewater, Waterleigh, Summerlake
- **Range**: Townhomes from $350K, single-family $450K-$800K
- **Schools**: Brand new schools being built. Mixed ratings as they establish.
- **Growth**: Massive ongoing buildout — will be 50,000+ homes at completion
- **Red flags**: Traffic is brutal and worsening. Infrastructure lagging behind rooftops. CDD fees $1,500-$3,000/year. Everything is new — no mature trees or established community feel yet.

### Winter Garden (34787)
- **Median price**: $570,000 (up 2.3% YoY)
- **Vibe**: Historic downtown with modern suburban growth. Garden District meets master-planned.
- **Who lives here**: Families, professionals, retirees
- **Notable**: Historic Plant Street (shops, restaurants, Garden Theatre), West Orange Trail
- **Schools**: Strong and improving. New schools opening.
- **Investment**: Solid growth area. Benefits from Horizon West spillover.

### Oakland
- **Median price**: $500K-$700K
- **Vibe**: Tiny historic town with new luxury development (Oakland Park)
- **Who lives here**: Families seeking community, nature lovers
- **Notable**: Oakland Nature Preserve, West Orange Trail, Oakland Park new urbanist community

### Ocoee
- **Median price**: $350K-$450K
- **Vibe**: Affordable suburb west of Orlando. Mix of older and newer developments.
- **Who lives here**: Families, working professionals, first-time buyers
- **Notable**: Good value relative to Winter Garden/Windermere
- **Schools**: Average. Some strong individual schools.

### Apopka
- **Median price**: $350K-$450K
- **Vibe**: Formerly agricultural, rapidly suburbanizing. "Indoor Foliage Capital of the World"
- **Who lives here**: Families, workers commuting to Orlando/Altamonte
- **Notable**: Wekiwa Springs State Park, rapid growth on west side
- **Growth**: Significant new construction pushing into former farmland
- **Red flags**: Some areas lack infrastructure. Commute to downtown can be long.

### MetroWest
- **Median price**: $280K-$400K
- **Vibe**: Large 1980s-90s planned community. Diverse, accessible, affordable.
- **Who lives here**: Diverse families, service workers, first-time buyers, international residents
- **Notable**: MetroWest Golf Club, near Universal, multiple shopping centers
- **Investment**: Good rental yields due to lower entry price and proximity to employment
- **Red flags**: Aging infrastructure. Some HOAs are poorly managed. Can feel dated.

## SEMINOLE COUNTY

### Altamonte Springs
- **Median price**: $350K-$450K
- **Vibe**: Suburban, central, accessible. SunRail connected.
- **Who lives here**: Families, young professionals, service workers
- **Notable**: Altamonte Mall, Cranes Roost Park, SunRail station
- **Investment**: Good value. Benefits from SunRail connectivity.
- **Schools**: Average to good.

### Lake Mary
- **Median price**: $450K-$650K
- **Vibe**: Upscale Seminole County suburb. Corporate offices, good schools.
- **Who lives here**: Corporate professionals, families
- **Notable**: AAA headquarters, multiple corporate campuses, Colonial Town Park
- **Schools**: Among the best in Seminole County
- **Investment**: Stable, desirable. Limited new inventory.

### Sanford
- **Median price**: $330K-$420K
- **Vibe**: Historic downtown on Lake Monroe. Revitalizing with craft breweries, restaurants.
- **Who lives here**: Mix of long-time residents and younger gentrifiers
- **Notable**: Historic downtown, Riverwalk, SunRail terminus, Sanford Airport (Allegiant hub)
- **Investment**: Strong upside. Most affordable Seminole County city with most character.
- **Red flags**: Higher crime in some areas. St. Johns River flooding in extreme events.

### Longwood
- **Median price**: $380K-$500K
- **Vibe**: Quiet, family-oriented suburb. Tree-lined, established.
- **Who lives here**: Families, professionals
- **Notable**: Historic district, Wekiwa Springs nearby, SunRail station

### Oviedo
- **Median price**: $430K-$550K
- **Vibe**: Family-oriented, good schools, "chickens crossing the road" town character
- **Who lives here**: Families, UCF employees, professionals
- **Notable**: UCF nearby, Oviedo Mall, multiple parks, known for semi-rural feel
- **Schools**: Strong. Oviedo High School well-regarded.
- **Red flags**: Far from most things. Limited nightlife/entertainment. Traffic on Red Bug Lake Rd.

### Winter Springs
- **Median price**: $400K-$520K
- **Vibe**: Suburban, family-friendly, quieter than Oviedo
- **Who lives here**: Families, professionals
- **Notable**: Central Winds Park, Cross Seminole Trail
- **Schools**: Good. Part of Seminole County school system.

### Casselberry
- **Median price**: $320K-$420K
- **Vibe**: Affordable, accessible, diverse suburb
- **Who lives here**: Diverse families, working professionals
- **Notable**: Secret Lake Park, Red Bug Lake. Good central location.
- **Investment**: Good rental yields, affordable entry point in Seminole County

## OSCEOLA COUNTY

### Kissimmee
- **Median price**: $337K-$342K (up 1.5% YoY)
- **Vibe**: Tourist corridor meets residential. Extremely diverse — large Puerto Rican and Latin American community.
- **Who lives here**: Hispanic families, service industry workers, investors
- **Notable**: US-192 tourist corridor, Old Town, proximity to Disney
- **Range**: Condos/townhomes from $200K, single-family $280K-$450K
- **Investment**: Strong for short-term rentals (where allowed) and long-term rentals
- **Red flags**: Tourist traffic. Some areas feel transient. Quality varies dramatically by neighborhood. Insurance costs can be high.

### St. Cloud
- **Median price**: $350K-$420K
- **Vibe**: Small-town Florida. Lakefront downtown, growing suburbs.
- **Who lives here**: Families, military (near former base), retirees
- **Notable**: Downtown lakefront, growing rapidly on east side toward Sunbridge
- **Growth**: Major expansion underway with Sunbridge and Tohoqua communities
- **Schools**: Improving. New schools opening with growth.
- **Red flags**: Far from everything except Lake Nona. Limited dining/entertainment.

### Celebration
- **Median price**: $595K-$635K (down 10% YoY in 2025)
- **Vibe**: Disney-created town. Immaculate, Truman Show-esque, community-oriented.
- **Who lives here**: Families, retirees, Disney employees, people who love planned community living
- **Notable**: Built by Disney in 1996. Town center, golf course, events, pristine aesthetics.
- **Schools**: Celebration School (K-8) is part of Osceola County district
- **Red flags**: Declining prices recently. High HOA fees. Disney sold to Lexin Capital in 2004 — ongoing management questions. CDD fees. Can feel artificial. HOA is very strict.

### Poinciana
- **Median price**: $280K-$340K
- **Vibe**: Affordable, sprawling residential community. Very diverse.
- **Who lives here**: Working-class families, first-time buyers, investors
- **Notable**: One of Florida's largest CDPs (Census-Designated Places). SunRail extension planned.
- **Investment**: Highest rental yields in metro due to low entry price. Cap rates 7-9%.
- **Red flags**: Long commute to anywhere. Limited amenities and commercial development. Higher crime in some areas. Remote feel.

### Reunion / ChampionsGate
- **Median price**: $400K-$800K (resort homes)
- **Vibe**: Resort/vacation home community near Disney. Golf courses, water parks.
- **Who lives here**: Vacation home owners, short-term rental investors, international buyers
- **Notable**: Reunion Resort, ChampionsGate by OMNI. Popular Airbnb area.
- **Investment**: Designed for short-term rentals. Occupancy rates 60-75%.
- **Red flags**: Not ideal for primary residence. High HOA/resort fees. Management company quality varies. STR regulations tightening.

## LAKE COUNTY

### Clermont
- **Median price**: $420K-$500K
- **Vibe**: Rolling hills (rare in Florida), lakes, outdoor recreation. Rapidly growing suburb.
- **Who lives here**: Families, outdoor enthusiasts, commuters priced out of Orange County
- **Notable**: Florida's hill country, numerous lakes, Waterfront Park, National Training Center (triathlon)
- **Growth**: One of fastest-growing cities in Florida. Major new construction.
- **Schools**: Improving. Several new schools.
- **Red flags**: 45-60 min commute to downtown Orlando. Limited employment locally. Growth outpacing infrastructure.

### Groveland / Minneola
- **Median price**: $350K-$430K
- **Vibe**: Former small towns being swallowed by suburban growth. Affordable, new construction.
- **Who lives here**: Families seeking affordability, commuters, first-time buyers
- **Notable**: Hills of Minneola (large new development), south Lake County growth
- **Investment**: Good for new construction rental properties
- **Red flags**: Very far from Orlando core. Infrastructure still catching up.

### Mount Dora
- **Median price**: $380K-$500K
- **Vibe**: Charming small town. Antiques, art galleries, festivals, lakefront.
- **Who lives here**: Retirees, artists, families seeking small-town life
- **Notable**: Historic downtown, Lake Dora, craft beer scene, festivals
- **Investment**: Unique tourism appeal. Vacation rental potential.
- **Red flags**: Far from Orlando employment centers. Aging housing stock.

### Leesburg / Tavares / Eustis
- **Median price**: $280K-$380K
- **Vibe**: Traditional small Florida towns. Lakes, fishing, quieter pace.
- **Who lives here**: Retirees, long-time residents, families seeking affordability
- **Notable**: Lake Harris, Dead River area, Harris Chain of Lakes
- **Investment**: Affordable entry to Lake County. Growing slowly.
- **Red flags**: Far from metro amenities. Limited job market locally.

## WEST ORANGE / POLK COUNTY FRINGE

### Davenport
- **Median price**: $330K-$400K
- **Vibe**: Vacation home capital. Endless cookie-cutter resort communities.
- **Who lives here**: Short-term rental investors, vacation home owners, families seeking affordability
- **Notable**: Massive vacation home communities (Solterra, Providence, Windsor Hills, Storey Lake)
- **Investment**: Built for STR. 6-8% gross yields. Must check STR zoning.
- **Red flags**: Oversupplied with vacation homes. Property management quality varies wildly. Far from Orlando employment. Some communities feel soulless.

### Haines City
- **Median price**: $300K-$370K
- **Vibe**: Working class, growing, diverse. Between Orlando and Lakeland.
- **Who lives here**: Working families, commuters, investors
- **Notable**: Southern Ridge area growing fast
- **Red flags**: Limited amenities. Long commute. Higher crime in some areas.

### Four Corners
- **Median price**: $320K-$400K
- **Vibe**: Where Orange, Osceola, Polk, and Lake counties meet. Mix of residential and resort.
- **Who lives here**: Families, service workers, vacation home owners
- **Notable**: Rapid growth area, multiple new communities
- **Red flags**: Unincorporated. Service delivery can be inconsistent. Traffic on US-27 is brutal.
