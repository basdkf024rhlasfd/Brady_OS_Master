# Data Sources & Research Links

Structured reference of free and public data sources for Orlando real estate research. Includes URLs and what each source provides.

---

## Property Prices & Market Data

| Source | URL | What It Provides |
|--------|-----|-----------------|
| Zillow Research | zillow.com/research/data/ | Downloadable CSV: ZHVI by ZIP, metro, county. Monthly updates. |
| Redfin Data Center | redfin.com/news/data-center/ | Median price, days on market, inventory by metro/ZIP. Weekly updates. |
| FRED Orlando HPI | fred.stlouisfed.org (search "ATNHPIUS36740A052A") | S&P/Case-Shiller Orlando Home Price Index. Quarterly, indexed to 2000. |
| ORRA Market Reports | orlandorealtors.org/market-reports | Orlando Regional Realtor Association monthly reports. Median price, closed sales, inventory, months of supply. |
| Realtor.com Research | realtor.com/research/ | Active listings, median list price, days on market by metro. |

## STR Zoning & Licensing

| Source | URL | What It Provides |
|--------|-----|-----------------|
| Osceola County GIS | gis.osceola.org | Overlay district maps — verify if a parcel is in STR-eligible zone. |
| Orange County Zoning Atlas | ocfl.net/PlanningDevelopment | Zoning designations by parcel. Verify STR eligibility. |
| DBPR License Search | myfloridalicense.com/wl11.asp | FL Dept of Business and Professional Regulation — search active vacation rental licenses by address. |
| City of Orlando STR Registry | orlando.gov (search "vacation rental") | Registration requirements, fees ($275 first year), compliance info. |
