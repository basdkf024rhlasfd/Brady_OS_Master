# Agent Transaction Guide

Transaction mechanics, commission landscape, MLS details, and compliance for real estate agents working the Orlando market.

---

## Post-NAR Settlement Landscape

### What Changed (August 2024)
- **Seller offers of compensation** can no longer be displayed on the MLS
- **Buyer broker agreements** required before showing property
- **Compensation is negotiable** — no "standard" commission anymore
- **Buyer agents** must clearly disclose how they're compensated

### How Orlando Adapted
- Most sellers still offering buyer agent compensation (communicated off-MLS: listing remarks, agent-to-agent, broker websites)
- Typical buyer-side compensation: **2-2.5%** (down from 2.5-3% pre-settlement)
- Some listings offer 0% buyer-side — buyer pays their agent directly or negotiates seller concession
- Buyer broker agreements are now standard practice — no more informal representation
- Listing agents updating practices: compensation discussions happen during listing presentation

### Buyer Broker Agreement Best Practices
- Clearly state compensation amount (flat fee or percentage)
- Specify duration (90 days max recommended for initial agreement)
- Define scope (geographic area, property types)
- Include termination clause
- Explain how compensation works if seller offers less than agreed amount
- Have the conversation early — don't spring it at the first showing

## Stellar MLS

### Overview
- **Coverage**: Orange, Seminole, Osceola, Lake, Polk, Brevard, Volusia, and surrounding counties
- **Members**: 60,000+ agents/brokers
- **System**: Matrix MLS platform
- **Data**: Most comprehensive source for Orlando metro real estate
- **IDX/RETS**: Available for member websites and apps

### Key Fields for Orlando Agents
- **Tax amount**: Verify actual taxes vs. homestead-reduced amount
- **CDD**: Community Development District fees (separate from HOA)
- **HOA transfer fees**: Who pays, how much — negotiate in contract
- **Flood zone**: Listed but always verify independently at FEMA
- **Roof year**: Critical for insurance — verify independently
- **STR eligible**: Note zoning restrictions for investment buyers

### MLS Compliance
- **Photos**: Minimum standards, no "Coming Soon" without proper compliance
- **Status changes**: Update within required timeframes
- **Compensation**: Cannot advertise buyer agent compensation in MLS fields (post-settlement)
- **Dual agency**: Disclosed and documented per Florida statute
- **Off-MLS listings**: Must comply with "Clear Cooperation Policy" — list within 1 business day of marketing

### Market Statistics Access
- Monthly market reports from Stellar MLS
- Orlando Regional REALTOR Association (ORRA) monthly stats
- Useful for CMAs, listing presentations, and buyer counseling

## Transaction Timeline

### Pre-Contract
| Task | Timeline | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| Buyer broker agreement signed | Before showing | Required post-NAR settlement |
| Pre-approval obtained | Before showing | Verify with lender directly |
| Property tours | Ongoing | Document feedback per showing |
| CMA/market analysis | Before offer | Comp analysis, days on market, price trends |

### Contract to Closing (30-45 Day Typical)
| Day | Milestone | Agent Action |
|-----|-----------|--------------|
| 0 | Offer submitted | Verify terms, EMD amount, contingencies |
| 0-3 | Negotiation/acceptance | Counter-offers, final execution |
| 1-3 | EMD deposited | Verify receipt, confirm with escrow |
| 1-5 | Inspections scheduled | Coordinate access, attend if possible |
| 10-15 | Inspection period ends | Negotiate repairs/credits or cancel |
| 5-10 | Appraisal ordered | Provide comps to appraiser proactively |
| 14-21 | Appraisal received | Review, negotiate if low |
| 7-14 | Insurance bound | Verify coverage, confirm with lender |
| 21-30 | Loan conditions cleared | Follow up with lender weekly |
| 28-35 | Clear-to-close | Confirm wire instructions, schedule closing |
| 30-45 | Final walkthrough | 24-48 hrs before closing |
| 30-45 | Closing | Attend or confirm smooth completion |

### Post-Closing Agent Tasks
- Verify recording of deed
- Remind buyer to file homestead exemption (before March 1)
- Provide list of recommended vendors (AC, pest, pool, lawn)
- Schedule 30-day check-in
- Request review/referral at appropriate time

## Commission & Compensation

### Current Orlando Commission Structure
| Scenario | Listing Side | Buyer Side | Notes |
|----------|-------------|------------|-------|
| Traditional resale | 2.5-3% | 2-2.5% | Total 4.5-5.5% |
| Luxury ($1M+) | 2-2.5% | 2-2.5% | May be negotiated lower |
| New construction | N/A (builder pays) | 2-3% | Some builders offer bonuses |
| FSBO conversion | 3-4% (if listing agent engaged) | 2-2.5% | Or flat fee variations |
| Flat fee brokerages | $500-$3,000 | 2-2.5% | Growing segment |

### Commission Negotiation Realities
- Listing agents under pressure to reduce fees — average total commission declining
- Buyer agents must justify their value proposition (post-settlement world)
- Discount brokerages (Redfin, Ideal Agent, Clever) competing on price
- Full-service agents differentiating on: local expertise, transaction management, negotiation skill, vendor network

## Florida Contract Essentials

### FAR/BAR Contract Forms
- **"As Is" Residential Contract**: Most common in Orlando — buyer inspects, seller doesn't repair
  - Seller discloses known defects
  - Buyer has inspection period to cancel for any reason
  - No repair negotiation (but price/credit renegotiation happens in practice)
- **Standard Residential Contract**: Less common — includes repair provisions
  - Seller obligated to make repairs up to a specified cost cap
  - If repairs exceed cap, either party can cancel
- **Both forms**: FAR (Florida Association of Realtors) and Bar (Florida Bar) jointly approved

### Critical Contract Provisions
- **Effective date**: When last party signs — starts all timelines
- **Inspection period**: Negotiate carefully — 10-15 days standard, 7 days minimum recommended
- **Financing contingency**: Specify loan type, amount, rate, and deadline
- **Appraisal contingency**: Protects buyer if value comes in low
- **Title evidence deadline**: When title commitment must be provided (usually 15-30 days)
- **Closing date**: Firm date — extensions require mutual agreement (addendum)
- **EMD default**: If buyer defaults, seller keeps EMD (subject to escrow dispute process)

### Disclosure Requirements
- **Seller's disclosure**: Not legally required in FL but standard practice (FAR form available)
- **Lead paint**: Required for homes built before 1978 (federal requirement)
- **HOA/condo disclosure summary**: Required by Florida statute — buyer has 3-day rescission right after receipt
- **Energy efficiency**: Required disclosure brochure
- **Radon**: Required radon gas disclosure statement
- **Flood zone**: Material fact — must disclose if known
- **Sinkhole**: Past sinkhole activity must be disclosed
- **Chinese drywall**: Must disclose if known
- **Property tax disclosure**: Buyer acknowledges taxes may increase after purchase (loss of homestead/SOH cap)

## Working with Orlando's Buyer Segments

### Relocating Buyers (Largest Segment)
- Often remote — expect virtual tours, Facetime walkthroughs
- Need education on Florida-specific costs (insurance, CDD, no income tax benefit)
- Coming from states with different practices (attorney states, different contract customs)
- **Key value-add**: Local knowledge they can't get from Zillow

### International Buyers
- **FIRPTA**: 15% of gross sale price withheld at closing for foreign sellers — buyers should understand implications for resale
- **ITIN**: Needed for those without SSN
- **Foreign nationals**: Can get mortgages but limited lender options (typically 30-40% down)
- **EB-5 investors**: Some using real estate as part of visa pathway
- **Cash purchases**: Common for international buyers — faster closings
- **Title considerations**: Foreign entity ownership, tax treaties, estate planning

### Investors
- **1-4 unit**: Conventional investment property loans (20-25% down, 0.5-0.75% rate premium)
- **DSCR loans**: Based on property income, not borrower income — popular for portfolio investors
- **Cap rates**: 5-7% for long-term rentals in Orlando metro
- **STR analysis**: Provide AirDNA data, occupancy rates, regulatory status by jurisdiction
- **1031 exchange**: Coordinate with qualified intermediary — strict 45-day identification, 180-day closing deadlines
- **Entity structure**: Many investors buy through LLC — title insurance considerations

### First-Time Buyers
- Need more hand-holding through process (this is okay — they're your future referral network)
- Connect to DPA programs early (City of Orlando, Orange County, FHFC)
- Homebuyer education course required for most DPA programs
- Explain homestead exemption and SOH cap — significant long-term savings
- Budget education critical — many underestimate insurance, CDD, maintenance costs

## Compliance & Risk Management

### License Requirements
- Florida real estate license (FREC)
- 14-hour continuing education every 2-year cycle
- Post-licensing education: 45 hours within first renewal period
- Broker associate or sales associate under a licensed broker

### Common Compliance Pitfalls
- **Unauthorized practice of law**: Don't draft legal documents, modify contracts beyond fill-in-the-blank forms, or give legal/tax advice
- **Dual agency**: Must be disclosed and consented to in writing (Florida allows but requires disclosure)
- **Advertising**: All ads must include brokerage name; team names must include brokerage
- **Trust accounts**: EMD must be deposited within specified timeframe; mishandling is a serious violation
- **Fair housing**: Federal + Florida Fair Housing Act — protected classes include familial status, disability, age
- **Wire fraud**: Educate clients on wire fraud prevention — never send wire instructions via email alone

### Transaction File Checklist
- [ ] Buyer broker agreement (signed before showing)
- [ ] Pre-approval letter (verified with lender)
- [ ] MLS listing printout
- [ ] Offer/counter-offer documentation
- [ ] Executed contract (all pages, all signatures)
- [ ] EMD receipt/deposit confirmation
- [ ] Inspection reports
- [ ] Repair addendum or amendment (if any)
- [ ] Appraisal report
- [ ] Insurance binder
- [ ] Title commitment
- [ ] Survey
- [ ] Closing disclosure (signed)
- [ ] Settlement statement (HUD-1 or equivalent)
- [ ] Commission disbursement authorization
- [ ] All addenda, amendments, and extensions

### Record Retention
- Florida requires brokers to retain transaction files for **5 years**
- Digital storage acceptable
- Includes all communications material to the transaction
- Failed transactions: retain file showing why deal fell through
