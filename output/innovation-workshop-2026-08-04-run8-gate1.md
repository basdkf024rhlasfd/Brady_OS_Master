## Stage 0 — Cluster (Readiness Level 2, sharpness gate PASSED first try)

**Cluster name:** Hospitality-Grade, Low-Labor Home Retail

- **Behavioral shift:** Consumers avoid the commissioned-salesperson gauntlet in furniture/mattress stores; they linger and spend where hospitality exists. bartaco removed order-takers entirely (90% QR ordering), replaced servers with fewer salaried zone "service leaders" (6-8 tables each vs 3-4), pooled tips house-wide — labor down 5-6 pts, ~50% fewer FOH staff, average wage UP to $23-24/hr, guest sentiment ~4.7/5, 30% store-level EBITDA (sources: Restaurant Dive 2023-01; FSR Feb 2021; Back of House Mar 2023; Opus Training podcast w/ CEO Anthony Valletta). Furniture/mattress retail still runs the 1990s commissioned floor at ~2-3% conversion.
- **Why now (2026):** ISPA 2026 report: 2025 mattress units -13.2%, dollars -6.5%, but ASP +5% and adjustable bases growing (premium trade-up). Sleep Number in Ch. 11 (sale to Sleep Country Canada; 600 shrinking stores); Mattress Firm closed ~700 stores in its 2018 bankruptcy with ~700 more closures announced since (verification-pass correction from the earlier "~900" figure); FRG collapse vacated ~880 Southeast storefronts = cheap second-gen boxes. Bed-in-a-box imploded (online mattress/bedding returns 21-22% vs ~9% in-store; CAC >30% of revenue); physical "slowrooms" winning again (Dreams, Bensons). Furniture × hospitality proven at luxury tier — RH Hospitality in 25 galleries ($5-6M/restaurant, drives furniture attach; NY gallery $100M+/yr), Poliform Café Milan (May 2026), Fritz Hansen Singapore showroom-restaurant (Mar 2026) — but NOBODY has built the mid-market, low-labor version. Enabling tech mature: QR ordering, AI sales coach (Ashley's "Theo" free to licensees), sleep tracking (Eight Sleep $100M Series D, retail 2026, mobile "House of Sleep" pod). Sleep/longevity culture peaking (SLEEPLAB Metro Paragon 10K sqft, sleep tourism).
- **JTBD:** "Let me furnish my home / fix my sleep the way I'd enjoy a night out — linger, touch, sip, decide on my own terms, without a commission-hungry stranger hovering." Secondary: "Prove the mattress works on MY body before I spend $2-5K."
- **Margin location:** Premium mid-market physical retail + services/membership attach. F&B is the dwell mechanic, not the profit center. Labor savings fund the experience.
- **NOT-to-generate:** No RH-clone mega-capex luxury; nothing that adds net labor; no pure e-comm; no liquor-license dependency without fallback; no repeats of prior 1915 South workshop ideas (Sleep Lab Specialty Stores, 15-Minute Showroom, Concierge Appointment Showroom, etc.) unless explicitly parented.
- **Operator context:** Executable by a multi-unit mid-market operator (29-store Southeast Ashley franchisee scale, owned DCs + fleet); retrofit capex $100K-$2M.

## Cross-cutting themes the panel surfaced

1. **The mattress side scored systematically higher than the furniture side** — 6 of the top 8 are sleep concepts. The "future of mattress stores" thread has more white space than furniture × F&B.
2. **The bartaco transfer that survives scrutiny is the SERVICE-LEADER + QR primitive, not the food.** Every food-forward concept took ops damage (permits, soil risk, staffing); every self-serve-ritual concept (menus, flights, suites, fit reports) survived.
3. **Paid, credited trial rituals ($25-49 that convert to purchase credit) appear independently in 4 surviving ideas** — the panel's strongest convergent signal. Ticketed retail filters intent and funds the labor model.
4. **Owned DC + fleet is the recurring moat** (delivery-date guarantees, whole-room single-date delivery) — the one asset neither DTC brands nor marketplaces can copy.
