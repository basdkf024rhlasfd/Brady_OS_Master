## Top 10 Priority Questions

1. **What is Panda's baseline crew turnover rate by role cohort, and how does it correlate with order accuracy/customer satisfaction?** (DR-01, DR-05, DR-09)
   - _Why it matters_: Turnover is the friction point in all ops improvements. Without baseline data, can't measure impact of labor productivity initiatives.

2. **What are Panda's current unit economics (sales per labor hour, food cost %, throughput by channel) by format, and what's the cash-on-cash ROI threshold James will accept for innovation investments?** (DR-01, DR-05, DR-07, DR-08)
   - _Why it matters_: Every pilot recommendation (automation, drive-thru, kitchen tech) requires P&L justification. Without these numbers, innovation scoring is theoretical.

3. **Does Panda have a centralized technology roadmap, or is digital infrastructure fragmented across franchisees? What is the current KDS vendor/deployment status and performance?** (DR-03, DR-04, DR-05, DR-13)
   - _Why it matters_: Kitchen automation, delivery integration, and labor management all hinge on backend infrastructure. Fragmentation kills scale.

4. **What is the Panda Home (virtual-only) unit economics, and is this format strategically viable or a pilot dead-end?** (DR-03, DR-08, DR-09)
   - _Why it matters_: Determines whether to invest in non-traditional formats or focus purely on drive-thru / delivery.

5. **What is the current board risk tolerance for ops innovation pilots, and has there been prior failed innovation mandates or centralization attempts that shaped current constraints?** (DR-01, DR-03, DR-13)
   - _Why it matters_: Understanding political/governance friction prevents designing pilots that will be killed mid-execution.

6. **What is the staffing model for the permanent Ops Innovation role (embedded in VP Ops seat vs. standalone function), and is there appetite for a fractional bridge (6-12 month consultant → permanent hire)?** (DR-14)
   - _Why it matters_: Role architecture determines what the permanent leader can actually move.

7. **How much do franchisees currently know about their own unit economics (labor cost detail, supply chain margin, digital attachment %), and what is their appetite for operational changes that require training or process redesign?** (DR-01, DR-02, DR-05, DR-13)
   - _Why it matters_: If franchisees can't see the ROI or don't trust data, scaling any innovation falters at franchise adoption.

8. **What are Panda's capital constraints (annual CapEx for tech, equipment replacement cycle, franchisee capex appetite), and how does this limit scope for kitchen automation, drive-thru lane dualing, or GridPoint smart building upgrades?** (DR-06, DR-07, DR-08, DR-10)
   - _Why it matters_: Scarcity is real. Need to know what's possible within guardrails.

9. **What is the SG&A expense breakdown by function (tech, real estate, supply chain, franchise support), and are there corporate efficiency opportunities that could fund innovation investments?** (DR-07, DR-13)
   - _Why it matters_: If SG&A is inflated, that's innovation budget. If it's lean, trade-offs get harder.

10. **How is order accuracy currently measured (by channel, by location, by menu item), and is there executive visibility into the gap between digital order placement and actual delivery/dine-in accuracy?** (DR-04)
    - _Why it matters_: Digital expansion drives growth; if accuracy isn't measured, quality debt accrues silently.

## Questions Panda Likely Won't Answer (Accept as Constraints)

1. **Exact franchisee-owned unit % and franchisee financial health** — This is confidential. Work around it by asking about "franchisee investment appetite" or "typical franchisee ROI threshold" instead.

2. **Specific labor cost for wok chefs or delivery drivers** — Panda likely won't share granular labor detail. Ask for ratios (labor cost % of sales, sales per labor hour) instead.

3. **Prior CEO's strategic failures or board misalignment** — Politics. Don't push; infer from "what has changed in the last 12 months?" and "what is the new CEO's priority?"

4. **Exact GridPoint contract economics** — If this is an undisclosed partnership, Panda may be defensive. Ask about "energy cost trends" and "franchisee visibility into savings" instead.

5. **Why Panda Home didn't scale or was deprioritized** — Likely a sensitive topic. Reframe as "What did we learn from Panda Home, and how does that inform the next format test?"

## Questions That Would Change the Recommendation

If answers shift the below, the entire ops innovation roadmap changes:

1. **Is franchisee adoption mandatory, or can Panda innovate at corporate-owned units only?**
   - _Impact_: If corporate-only, scope is smaller (300+ units) but faster. If mandatory, scale is harder but network effect is stronger.

2. **Is Panda targeting aggressive automation (robotics, lights-out prep) or incremental improvement (labor scheduling, order accuracy)?**
   - _Impact_: Robots require 18-24 month lead time; incremental improvements can show ROI in 6-9 months.

3. **Will Panda divest/close underperforming units, or commit to turnaround at all locations?**
   - _Impact_: If willing to close, innovation can target high-potential units. If not, innovation must work at all maturity levels.

4. **Is the goal 1-2 flagship format innovations, or a portfolio strategy (drive-thru, ghost kitchen, Panda Home variants)?**
   - _Impact_: Flagship = concentrated resources; portfolio = distributed learning (slower per-format progress, broader resilience).

5. **How much capital can Panda deploy in 2026-2027 for innovation (CapEx, consulting, pilot operations)?**
   - _Impact_: $5M/year vs. $20M/year changes pilot scope by 4x.
