## How This Was Built

1. **Deep Research:** 14 specialized threads covering ops innovation at Walmart/Starbucks/McDonald's, Panda's current state, peer benchmarking (digital accuracy, labor, kitchen automation, formats, governance, hiring), and KPI analysis.
2. **Candidate Extraction:** Each thread yielded 3–6 candidate problem statements. Total pull: ~60 unique statements across all clusters.
3. **5-Criteria Gate Applied:** Every statement evaluated on Binding Constraint, Evidence (2+ sources), Operator Mandate, Measurable Win, and NOT-to-generate Guardrails. Weak candidates dropped or merged.
4. **Clustering:** Organized into 7 operational themes: Cost Structure, Digital Fulfillment, Kitchen / Labor, Labor Productivity, Throughput / Format, Real Estate / Format, Org / Governance, Infrastructure.
5. **Deduplication & Prioritization:** Near-duplicates merged; statements ranked by confidence, addressable value, and strategic alignment.
6. **Final Portfolio:** 20 statements, covering all clusters, each with traceable citation trails and actionable next steps.

3. **Operator Mandate** — James or his peer (ops-level) has funding authority + explicit or recent signal of interest.
4. **Measurable Win** — specific KPI, directional target, and time horizon (90 days to 24 months, pilot-sized).
5. **NOT-to-generate Guardrails** — 3–5 specific operational tropes or proven-failed paths to avoid during ideation.

## Cluster A — Cost Structure (SG&A, Labor, Food %)

### PS-01 — Labor % Lever: Rebalance Staffing Model & Workflow

**Binding constraint:** Panda's restaurant labor cost runs 3–4 ppts above Chipotle on equivalent sales mix. Full closure of the gap would reclaim ~$100M annually in margin; a pilot-realistic first-step compression (1.5 ppts within 24 months) unlocks $30–40M. Current throughput per labor hour is lower than peers despite similar menu complexity — the constraint is workflow design (sequencing, role definition, peak-hour play-calling), not headcount.

**Evidence:**
- **DR-07, §1:** Panda labor % triangulated 30% vs. Chipotle 26–27% (Chipotle 10-K, Feb 2026). Full 3–4 ppt gap = ~$100M at Panda's revenue scale; first-pass 1.5 ppt target = $30–40M.
- **DR-01, §1:** Starbucks Siren Craft System reduced out-of-window time 15–20% via espresso-first sequencing + Peak Play Caller role; freed ~0.5 FTE per shift, deployed to 1,160 stores by May 2024.
- **DR-05, §1:** Sweetgreen Infinite Kitchen achieves 20% labor % vs. Panda ~26–28% via selective automation + workflow redesign (not headcount cuts).
- **DR-05, §3:** Chipotle digital makeline pilot (Hyphen, Oct 2023) shows 2–3% throughput lift with zero capex (SOP only).

**Mandate fit:** James Ku (CDO) owns format and operational innovation; labor rebalancing sits squarely in ops innovation scope. CFO David Landsberg has signaled margin pressure as a strategic priority (investor calls, job postings, DR-07).

**KPI + target delta:** Labor % of revenue 30% → 28.5% within 24 months (pilot-realistic); ~$30–40M annual margin recovery. Phased across ~10% of units in Year 1 = 50–75 bps system-wide margin lift on pilot cohort. Aspirational closure to Chipotle (26.5%) over 36–48 months = ~$100M.

**NOT-to-generate guardrails:**
- ❌ "Cut crew headcount across the board" — brand promise is quality + speed; service collapses under thin crew.
- ❌ "Switch to full automation" — brand is open-kitchen visibility; robotic-only kitchen fails that test.
- ❌ "Reduce wages to match industry low" — CA wage pressure + FAST Act; legal and retention constraint.
- ❌ Generic "training program improvements" — training already exists; issue is workflow design, not knowledge.

**Gate status:** ✅ Passed all 5

**Source threads:** DR-01, DR-05, DR-07
