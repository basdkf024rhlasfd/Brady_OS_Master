Reserve
≥ $42,750
Every bonus lands here, 100%. It pays us a steady $14,250 "bonus salary" on the 1st. We never spend a bonus directly.
The rules (so it never has to be a debate)
  • We budget against $23,000/mo, always. $8,750 base + $14,250 bonus salary. The size of the last bonus check changes nothing.
  • A bonus landing is a non-event here. It sweeps 100% to the Reserve. If it hits the checking account, text Brady — it's in the wrong place.
  • The HELOC is not income. Ever. It's for things that make the house worth more. A HELOC draw for lifestyle is an automatic red month.
  • When money is tight, things give in this order: extra investing → fun & discretionary → (only if forced) food to the floor → 529s last → the mortgage never.
  • No guilt. A red week isn't a fight, it's a light. The next week runs lighter and the month can still pass.
