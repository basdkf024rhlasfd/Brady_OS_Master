1915 South Engagement · CFO + Applied AI

CFO + Applied AI Workspace

Role design, scope of accountability, AI decision layer above existing data infrastructure, GMROI improvement levers, and finance modernization.

Status active Phase Problem Frame Last update 2026-04-26

This workspace covers the role-design lane of the engagement: a CFO-shaped role with technology and applied AI in scope, sitting above the existing data infrastructure rather than displacing it. The aim is a small set of crisp accountability statements, an applied-AI roadmap that compounds on existing investments, and a finance-modernization plan that liberates leadership from operating-cadence firefighting.

Role anchors

Scope of accountability

Accounting and controllership (incl. inventory). Deal support and modeling. Entity design and structure. Management and operating framework. Applied AI roadmap. Technology and IT.

AI decision layer

Existing data foundation (Zapsight) is implementation. The decision layer that turns data into operating choices and margin actions is the seat the role owns.

Manufacturer context

Ashley corporate (Theo / FrontlineIQ for floor coaching, AshleyDirect for ordering and assets) is operating at the floor and pipes layer. The 29-store decision layer remains an open seat.

GMROI directive

Ashley's Dec 2024 Excellence Framework is a positioning document with commercial enforcement (product access, co-op eligibility) — not a contractual numeric target.

The three operating layers

A useful way to read the AI landscape inside an Ashley licensee operation: floor, pipes, and decisions.

Floor layer · already shipping

Sales-rep coaching at the showroom (e.g., Theo / FrontlineIQ via Ashley AI Advantage). Lands inside store sessions, free at the licensee level. Useful and underutilized — but bounded.

Pipes layer · in build

Data foundation, ingestion, model and dashboard infrastructure. Largely owned by the existing data vendor. Fast progress recently; agentic capabilities being built on top.

Decision layer · open seat

The translation of data into operating choices that move GMROI, gross margin, sell-through, and labor productivity. Connects pipes to specific weekly and quarterly merchandising, pricing, inventory, and ops decisions. This is where the CFO + Applied AI role sits.

Year-1 outline

  • Inventory and GMROI lever map. 80/20 SKU audit, order frequency / depth tuning (Ashley's MOQ-of-1 enables this), structured promotional clearance design.
  • Pricing and assortment cadence. Weekly pricing rhythm with explicit decision rights and a single source of margin truth tied back to the data foundation.
  • Last-mile economics. Independent assessment of the in-house delivery operation against 3PL alternatives at current and post-acquisition footprint scale, focused on capital and complexity not just unit cost.
  • Finance modernization. Faster close, cleaner inventory accounting, and a forecasting cadence that holds up post-AGR integration.
  • Applied-AI roadmap. Sequenced capabilities (call-center triage, demand forecasting layer above corporate, agentic decision support for merchants, etc.) with explicit coordination with the existing data vendor.

Why this role exists now

  • Two-side mandate from Ashley corporate. Both the AI commitment and the GMROI directive create a multi-year window where modernization and franchise economics are explicitly on Wanek's stage.
  • Roll-up scale. A path to ~48 stores in 18 months changes the finance, IT, and decision-cadence requirements faster than incremental hiring can absorb. The role exists to absorb that complexity.
  • Vendor seam. The pipes layer is well underway; the decision layer is not yet built. The role exists to turn pipes into outcomes, not to rebuild pipes.
