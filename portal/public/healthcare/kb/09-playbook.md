# "What Do I Do When…" Playbook

The fast answers. Hand this to anyone.

**…we need to see a NEW doctor or specialist?** → First nominate them to ClaimDOC (portal.claim-doc.com or 888-330-7295) so they "Pave the Way." Then book the appointment. There's no network, so you can pick anyone.

**…a medical bill shows up in the mail?** → Don't pay yet. Wait for the EOB from HealthSCOPE. Pay only the "Patient Responsibility" on the EOB. If the bill is higher → it's a balance bill → send to balancebills@claim-doc.com. Never argue with the provider yourself.

**…someone is sick at night/weekend (not an emergency)?** → Teladoc: 800-835-2362 for a phone/video doctor. Or use a local urgent care (nominate it to ClaimDOC if you can).

**…it's a real emergency?** → Call 911 / go to the nearest ER. Don't wait. Deal with billing afterward (the EOB/balance-bill rules still apply — ClaimDOC handles it).

**…we need a prescription or a refill?** → Use optumrx.com. For daily/maintenance meds, switch to 90-day home delivery to save money and trips.

**…we need a cleaning, filling, or the kids need braces?** → Dental (Unum): unumdentalcare.com / 888-400-9304. Preventive is 100%. Confirm whether we're on PPO or MAC.

**…someone needs an eye exam or glasses?** → VSP: vsp.com (Choice network) / 800-877-7195. Exam is a $10 copay. Use in-network to save. Buy online at eyeconic.com.

**…someone has an accident (broken bone, ER, stitches)?** → Get treated first. Then file an accident claim at sunlife.com/us / 800-247-6875 for a cash payout — separate from and on top of the medical plan.

**…a doctor's office says "we don't take your insurance"?** → Tell them it's open-access, no network, and have them call ClaimDOC at 888-330-7295 (or HSB provider line 844-600-0921). ClaimDOC handles reluctant offices all the time.
