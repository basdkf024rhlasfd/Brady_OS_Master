# Bills, EOBs, Balance Bills & Using HealthSCOPE + ClaimDOC

## A. Set up your HealthSCOPE account (do once)

1. Go to healthscopebenefits.com or download the HealthSCOPE Benefits app.
2. Click Register / Create HealthSafe ID and enter the info from your medical ID card.
3. Once in: see your digital ID card, check what's covered, see deductible progress, track claims, view EOBs.
4. Add the ID card to Apple Wallet / Google Wallet.

## B. Register with the ClaimDOC portal (do once)

1. Go to portal.claim-doc.com. (Before the plan's start date, use portal.claim-doc.com/guest, PIN S31792.)
2. Create an account → add all family members on the plan → ready to nominate providers.

## C. "Nominate" a provider BEFORE the first visit ("Pave the Way")

Do this any time you'll see a new doctor, specialist, or facility:

1. In the ClaimDOC portal, submit a provider nomination (name + office). Or email membersupport@claim-doc.com or call 888-330-7295.
2. A ClaimDOC advocate calls the provider, explains the plan, and confirms they can bill HealthSCOPE.
3. Result: appointment and billing go smoothly with no surprises.

Tip: Nominate any provider with an appointment within 30 days of a new plan starting.

## D. When a bill arrives — the EOB check (THE most important habit)

1. Do NOT pay yet. Wait for the EOB from HealthSCOPE.
2. Compare the EOB to the provider's bill.
3. Confirm the plan's payment was applied and the bill matches the "Patient Responsibility."
4. Pay ONLY the Patient Responsibility amount.

### Worked example (urgent care for a hurt wrist)

| | EOB (HealthSCOPE) | Provider's invoice |
|---|---|---|
| Urgent care visit | $300 | Billed: $300 |
| Insurance reduction | –$162 | $0 |
| Plan payment | –$118 | $118 |
| **You owe** | **$20** | "Amount due": $182 ← balance bill |

You owe **$20, not $182**. The $182 is a balance bill — do not pay it.

## E. If you get a balance bill

1. Don't pay the inflated amount and don't argue with the provider.
2. Send it to ClaimDOC: balancebills@claim-doc.com or call 888-330-7295.
3. If the provider keeps sending bills or letters, forward those to ClaimDOC too. They handle all of it — including legal backup if ever needed.

## F. What ClaimDOC advocates do for us (all free)

- Find a doctor, specialist, lab, or imaging center.
- Find a lower-cost option for non-emergencies (e.g., an imaging center instead of a hospital — "the bigger the building, the bigger the bill").
- Introduce the plan to a provider before a visit.
- Review confusing bills and EOBs.
- Quote benefits / how much deductible is left.
- Fight and resolve balance bills.
