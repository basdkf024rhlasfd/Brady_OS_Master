# Vision — Sun Life / VSP (Plan 3) · Policy #956845

- **Find a doctor:** vsp.com → choose the **Choice network** · or call 800-877-7195 · or the VSP Benefit Tools app.
- **At the visit:** just say you're a VSP member — no ID card or forms needed in-network.
- **Buy online:** eyeconic.com (virtual try-on; links the benefit).
- Dependents covered to age 26.

## What it covers (in-network)

| Benefit | How often | You pay |
|---|---|---|
| Eye exam (WellVision) | 1 / 12 months | $10 copay |
| Lenses (single/bifocal/trifocal) | 1 / 12 months | $25 (lenses & frame copay) |
| Frames | 1 / 24 months | $150 allowance + 20% off the rest (Walmart selection; $80 at Costco) |
| Contacts (instead of lenses) | 1 / 12 months | $60 fitting + $150 allowance |
| Premium / custom progressive lenses | — | $95–$105 / $150–$175 copay |
| Laser vision correction (LASIK) | 1/eye/lifetime | 15% off regular (5% off promo) at contracted centers |

**Out-of-network warning:** you pay the full bill, then submit a claim for a smaller reimbursement (exam up to $45, frames up to $70, etc.). Stick with VSP doctors to save.
