# Pharmacy (Optum Rx) & Teladoc

## Pharmacy — Optum Rx

- Website/app: optumrx.com + Optum Rx app. Register using the member ID card.
- **Retail:** fill at most major pharmacies (93%+ of U.S. pharmacies).
- **Home delivery (best for daily meds):** 90-day / 3-month supply of long-term "maintenance" medications (blood pressure, cholesterol, etc.) mailed with free shipping — often cheaper than the pharmacy counter. Arrives within ~5 business days. Auto-refills and reminders in the app.
- Sign up: optumrx.com / app, ask the doctor to e-prescribe to Optum Rx, or call the number on the ID card.
- **Formulary:** "Premium" formulary with tiers — Tier 1 = lowest cost. Ask for generics/preferred drugs to save.
- **Prior authorization:** some drugs need approval first; the doctor submits it, or start it on optumrx.com.
- **Preventive meds:** certain ACA-preventive medications are $0 (no copay/deductible).
- Pharmacy spending counts toward the medical out-of-pocket max (integrated).
- Confused by a term? Plain-language definitions at justplainclear.com.

## Teladoc — a doctor by phone/video, 24/7

Great for nights, weekends, travel, or when you don't want to drag a sick kid to a clinic.

- **What it treats:** non-emergencies — flu, colds, sinus infections, bronchitis, pink eye, rashes, etc. They can send a prescription if needed.
- **How:** create an account, request a visit, a provider contacts you by phone or video.
- **Contact:** 800-835-2362 · TeladocHealth.com · Teladoc app.
- Not available internationally. Not for emergencies — for those, call 911.
