# Glossary & One-Time Setup Checklist

## Plain-English glossary

- **Self-funded / self-insured:** 1915 South pays the claims itself (instead of buying a traditional insurance policy). HealthSCOPE just administers it.
- **Reference-based pricing (RBP / DirectAccess+):** The plan pays providers a fair benchmark price instead of a negotiated "network" rate. It's why bills can look high at first but you actually owe less.
- **EOB (Explanation of Benefits):** The statement from HealthSCOPE showing what the plan paid and the real amount you owe. Always wait for this before paying.
- **Patient Responsibility:** The only number you should pay — shown on the EOB.
- **Balance bill:** When a provider bills you for more than your Patient Responsibility. You don't owe it. Send it to ClaimDOC.
- **Nominate a provider / Pave the Way:** Telling ClaimDOC about a doctor before your visit so they prep the office.
- **Deductible:** What you pay out of pocket before the plan starts sharing costs.
- **Coinsurance:** Your percentage share after the deductible (e.g., you pay 20%).
- **Out-of-pocket maximum:** The most you'll pay in a year; after that the plan pays 100%.
- **Copay:** A flat fee for a service (e.g., $25 for a visit).
- **Formulary:** The list of medications the pharmacy plan covers, grouped in price tiers.
- **Maintenance medication:** A drug you take regularly — best filled as a 90-day home-delivery supply.

## One-time setup checklist

- [ ] Create HealthSCOPE account (app + web) and add ID card to phone wallet
- [ ] Register on the ClaimDOC portal and add all family members
- [ ] Nominate our current doctors/dentist so they're "paved" before the next visit
- [ ] Set up Optum Rx account; move daily meds to 90-day home delivery
- [ ] Save all the numbers from the Emergency quick-reference card into both our phones
- [ ] Confirm the coinsurance % and monthly premium (HR/payroll or 844-600-0920) — the only two medical numbers still open
- [ ] Confirm which dental plan (PPO vs MAC) we elected
- [ ] Put a reminder to claim the $50 Sun Life wellness benefit after each annual physical
