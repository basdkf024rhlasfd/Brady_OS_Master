# The Golden Rules of Our Medical Plan

Source: 1915 South Family Benefits Handbook (July 2026). These five rules prevent 95% of all problems.

Our medical plan is **self-funded with reference-based pricing (DirectAccess+)**. There is no insurance network, and bills sometimes look scary at first. A free advocate team called **ClaimDOC** does the fighting for us.

1. **NEVER pay a medical bill the moment it arrives.** First wait for the EOB ("Explanation of Benefits") from HealthSCOPE. The EOB tells you the real amount you owe.
2. **Only pay the "Patient Responsibility" amount shown on the EOB** — not whatever the provider's invoice says. If the provider's bill is higher, that extra amount is a "balance bill" and you do not owe it.
3. **If a bill and the EOB don't match, call ClaimDOC (888-330-7295).** They fight the provider for you, for free. Don't argue with the provider yourself.
4. **Before seeing a NEW doctor, "nominate" them to ClaimDOC first** (portal.claim-doc.com). ClaimDOC calls the office ahead of time so billing goes smoothly. This is called "Pave the Way."
5. **There is no network.** You can use any doctor, specialist, clinic, or hospital. All covered care is paid at the same (highest) level — as long as the provider agrees to bill HealthSCOPE.

## How the money flows

1. We pay 100% of costs until we reach the deductible ($2,500 per person / $5,000 family).
2. After the deductible, the plan pays most of the cost and we pay a coinsurance share.
3. When we hit the out-of-pocket max ($7,000 per person / $14,000 family), the plan pays 100% for the rest of the plan year.
4. Pharmacy counts toward the same out-of-pocket max (it's "integrated").
5. The plan year resets every **October 1**.
6. There are no flat office/ER copays — everything runs through deductible + coinsurance.

## Why this plan is actually good for us

The plan pays a fair reference price instead of an inflated "list" price, so the family usually pays less out of pocket. Example from the plan materials — a 6-day hospital stay billed at $67,841: a traditional network plan would pay $41,257 (our 20% share: $8,251.40); our plan pays $16,641.86 (our 20% share: $3,328.37). The family saved ~$4,923.
