# Medical Plan — Self-Funded, Reference-Based Pricing (DirectAccess+)

## What kind of plan is this?

1915 South pays medical claims itself (**self-funded**), using **DirectAccess+ reference-based pricing** powered by ClaimDOC. Instead of pre-negotiated "in-network" discounts, the plan pays providers a fair, reference-based price. Two companies make it work:

- **HealthSCOPE Benefits (HSB)** = the plan administrator. They process claims, issue EOBs, track the deductible, and confirm coverage.
- **ClaimDOC** = our member advocates. They find providers, introduce the plan to doctors, review bills, and handle balance bills or pushback. Free, and on our side.

## How "open access" works

- Use any provider — PCP, specialist, lab, imaging center, hospital.
- No out-of-network penalties. Everything covered is paid at the top level.
- The only requirement: the provider agrees to submit the claim to HealthSCOPE. Most do. If one hesitates, ClaimDOC handles it.
