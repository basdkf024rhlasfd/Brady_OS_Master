  • Named target operator pool (public sources only). 15 named candidates across the Southeast, screened on geography, format, ownership posture, and adjacency to the 1915 South footprint.
  • Highest-priority adjacent target. Ashley HomeStores of Southwest Virginia — 8–9 stores Roanoke through Bluefield, dual-brand operator with an independent Mechanicsville chain (Home-Makers Furniture, since 1988). Reverse carve-out precedent. Adjacent to the Danville footprint.
  • FRG real-estate map. Southeast vacant-box inventory across FL/GA/AL/MS/NC/SC/TN/VA, with named addresses where public.

Sell-side / capital-in — what's included

  • Valuation anchors. Multiple sequencing options modeled — 5.5×–7× operating EBITDA range plus strategic premium considerations.
  • Capital partner universe. 20+ named sponsors and structured-debt providers across furniture and broader specialty retail, with thesis fit notes.
  • Strategic sequence. Recommended order of operations across organic growth, tuck-in M&A, capital-in, and partnership levers.

Why now

  • Manufacturer cover. Ashley corporate's AI mandate creates top-down support for showcase licensees who move on operating-model modernization in tandem with footprint expansion.
  • Once-a-decade real-estate window. Box-for-box availability in the 1915 South footprint is the highest it has been in 5+ years and unlikely to persist at this scale.
  • Operator-pricing reset. Distressed-operator transaction comps have re-anchored what franchisee tuck-ins should clear at — often well below the prior multiple regime.
