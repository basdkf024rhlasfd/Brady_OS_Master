- In 2025 we generated $25M in EBITDA — up from $17.5M in 2024.
- YTD in 2026 we are tracking about flat to LY on top line although deliveries have become more challenging with supply chain issues tied to the Iran war so Q2 could see some declines. That said EBITDA expanded in Q1 from strong expense control and a good start to the year in January.
- As we fold this first acquisition into the company, with no top line improvement we are adding $70M to the business and I believe we have clear line of sight to ~14% EBITDA on that business — taking the company to ~$35M in annualized EBITDA. This year is probably looking more like somewhere between $25-28M in EBITDA given deal timing and time to implement new ways of working.
- We have a clear path to 2 additional stores (not shown below) that likely add another $15M in top line. The LOI on these is signed and they will also be acquired from Ashley. Beyond that we have a new store opening in Florida in Q1 2028, an acquisition opportunity in Columbus, MS for one store and a 3-store acquisition in Beaumont, TX that could happen in 2028 (possibly sooner). We'll see one existing store close in Jacksonville at the end of this year. All in that puts us at 48 stores within 18 months or so. There is growth beyond that too.

### Comp Structure Proposal

- Low base — the lower this is set, the more flexibility exists on bonus comp
- Bonus calculated as a % of EBITDA and paid quarterly
- Long term incentive issued annually and earned out in equal increments over 5 years
- Intent of the comp structure is to tie personal growth to company growth and performance — the team has the influence and autonomy to drive top line growth and EBITDA improvement
- I have an idea in mind on what these numbers could look like but want to give some more thought to it and also understand how you are thinking you'd want it structured
