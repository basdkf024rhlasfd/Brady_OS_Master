- **Below target** → focus on pipeline / delivery / collections

---

## The 30/70 Rule

Every dollar of consulting income splits at the door:

- **30% → tax reserve account** (separate, untouchable — federal + SE tax)
- **70% → operating account** (funds the three spending buckets)

If gross is treated as available cash, the budget fails silently. Discovery happens in April at an ugly number.

**Tax caveat:** 2026 blended rate may be ~38% (fed + SE 15.3% + AR state). 30% reserve may be ~8 points light. Verify with Stephen Butler (CPA, Miller Butler PLLC, 479-621-0006) once Q1 2026 income is known.
