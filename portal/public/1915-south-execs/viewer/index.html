Engagement context

  • Inbound conversation. Justin Woods initiated the conversation in April 2026, framing the opportunity around organizational redesign tied to AI-enabled ways of working — including a CFO-shaped role with technology and applied AI in scope.
  • Manufacturer-level AI mandate. Ashley Furniture Industries (Todd Wanek, CEO) has publicly stated businesses have "3–5 years to figure out AI or get left behind," committed $2B to innovation, and operationalized a GMROI Excellence Framework for licensees (Dec 2024). 170+ AI projects identified inside Ashley corporate.
  • Roll-up posture. Two acquisitions in three years and a stated path to ~48 stores within 18 months. Southeast operator distress (FRG / Badcock / Conn's / American Freight) has cleared substantial regional retail capacity from 2024–2025.
  • Family ownership. Russell Turner, 4th-generation family owner, stepped off the executive board in January 2025 — handing operational keys to Justin while retaining decision authority on anything material. Continuity and culture preservation are first-order constraints.
