What We Do
Built by shelter people, for shelter people
There are very few people in the world who completely understand what it's like to work at an open-access shelter or as an animal control officer. The only ones who really can are your fellow workers in this field.
⚖️

Advocacy & Legislation

We are active participants in the Comprehensive Animal Laws work group and advocate for legislation that protects animals and supports the organizations that serve them. During session, we send time-sensitive legislative alerts so members can act fast.

📋

Training & Best Practices

Regional workshops, shared protocols, and peer-to-peer knowledge transfer. We make high-quality training accessible to shelters that can't afford national conferences — because the animals in rural Virginia deserve the same standard of care.

🤝

Collaboration & Support

Collaborative programming among members — spay/neuter campaigns, adoption events, transfer partnerships. When one shelter is overwhelmed, others step up. We share resources so no shelter fights alone.

Veterinary Access Bill Passes GA — Awaiting Governor's Signature

A new bill would create a pathway to connect veterinarians with animal shelters in need of support by allowing vets applying for licensure in Virginia to indicate interest in serving shelters. If signed, it takes effect July 2026.

Membership
There are no membership dues
You have already paid for the cost of membership through your dedication to serving the animals in Virginia.

Full Member

FREE — Open-access & municipal shelters in Virginia

Full voting privileges, committee participation, legislative alerts, regional workshop access, and peer support network. Open to any open-access or municipal animal shelter operating in the Commonwealth.

Associate Member

FREE — By invitation & committee vote

For organizations, agencies, or individuals who have demonstrated support of and worked with open-access shelters. Voting privileges and committee eligibility. Requires sponsorship by a member in good standing.

Affiliate Member

FREE — Contiguous state shelters

For shelters in neighboring states seeking training, communication, and resource-sharing. No voting or legislative involvement — focused on cross-border collaboration and professional development.
