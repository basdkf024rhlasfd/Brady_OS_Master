## Major National Builders in Orlando

### Lennar
- **Profile**: One of the largest builders nationally. Known for "Everything's Included" model
- **Everything's Included**: Most upgrades (appliances, blinds, smart home) included in base price
- **Orlando communities**: Storey Park, EverBe, Sunbridge, multiple Horizon West villages
- **Price range**: Townhomes from $350K, single-family from $400K-$600K+
- **Reputation**: High volume, consistent quality, not luxury. Good value for features included.
- **Incentives**: Often offers closing cost assistance, rate buydowns

### D.R. Horton
- **Profile**: America's largest homebuilder by volume. Known for affordability.
- **Sub-brands**: Express Homes (entry-level), Emerald Homes (upscale), Freedom Homes (55+)
- **Orlando communities**: Multiple across all four counties
- **Price range**: Express from $280K, standard $350K-$500K, Emerald $450K-$650K
- **Reputation**: Most affordable new construction option. Quality is functional, not premium.
- **Best for**: First-time buyers seeking lowest entry price in new construction

### Pulte Homes / PulteGroup
- **Profile**: Known for thoughtful floor plans ("Life Tested" designs)
- **Brands**: Pulte Homes (move-up), Del Webb (55+), Centex (entry-level)
- **Orlando communities**: EverBe, The Grow (near UCF), multiple Horizon West locations
- **Price range**: Centex from $300K, Pulte from $385K-$600K
- **The Grow**: Unique community with 9-acre working farm, near UCF
- **Reputation**: Above-average quality for production builder. Good customer satisfaction.

### Toll Brothers
- **Profile**: Premium national builder. Luxury positioning.
- **Orlando communities**: Woodside Preserve, Haven Oaks, select Lake Nona locations
- **Price range**: From $590K to $1M+
- **Reputation**: Higher quality finishes, more customization than other production builders
- **Best for**: Buyers wanting luxury new construction without going full custom

### Taylor Morrison
- **Profile**: National builder with strong Florida presence
- **Orlando communities**: Horizon West, Lake Nona area, Winter Garden
- **Price range**: $400K-$700K
- **Reputation**: Good quality, strong in Florida market. Known for design options.

### Meritage Homes
- **Profile**: Focus on energy efficiency ("zero energy-ready" homes)
- **Orlando communities**: Multiple Horizon West, Clermont area
- **Price range**: $380K-$600K
- **Reputation**: Most energy-efficient production builder. Lower utility bills.

### M/I Homes
- **Profile**: Quality-focused builder, smaller than Lennar/DR Horton
- **Orlando communities**: Lake Nona area, Horizon West
- **Price range**: $400K-$650K
- **Reputation**: Good customer service, quality construction

### Ashton Woods
- **Profile**: Design-forward builder emphasizing personalization
- **Orlando communities**: Select locations in south Orlando, Lake Nona corridor
- **Price range**: $400K-$600K
- **Reputation**: More design flexibility than most production builders

### Dream Finders Homes
- **Profile**: Jacksonville-based, growing rapidly in Central Florida
- **Orlando communities**: Multiple communities across metro
- **Price range**: $350K-$550K
- **Reputation**: Regional builder with strong local knowledge. Competitive pricing.

### KB Home
- **Profile**: "Built to Order" — more customization than most production builders
- **Orlando communities**: Limited Orlando presence, more in Tampa
- **Price range**: $320K-$500K

## Major Master-Planned Communities

### Lake Nona (Southeast Orlando)
- **Size**: 17 square miles (11,000 acres)
- **Developer**: Tavistock Development
- **Character**: Technology-forward, wellness-oriented, master-planned city-within-a-city
- **Key features**:
  - Medical City: 650 acres with VA Hospital, UCF Medical, Orlando Health, Nemours
  - Town Center: Restaurants, shops, Boxi Park (shipping container food hall)
  - USTA National Campus (United States Tennis Association)
  - 44 miles of trails, parks, sports complexes
  - Lake Nona Wave Hotel (wellness-focused)
- **Sub-communities**: Laureate Park (Craft homes, innovation hub), VillageWalk, Eagle Creek
- **Price range**: Townhomes $350K-$450K, single-family $500K-$1M, estates $1M-$3M+
- **Builders**: Pulte, Ashton Woods, Taylor Morrison, Mattamy, others
- **CDD fees**: $2,500-$4,000/year
- **Why buy here**: Medical/tech jobs, future growth, appreciation potential
- **Why not**: Far from west side, CDD fees, still building out, premium pricing

### Horizon West (West Orange County)
- **Size**: 38,000 acres when fully built out
- **Developer**: Multiple — overseen by Orange County specific area plan
- **Character**: Orlando's fastest-growing submarket. Family-oriented, amenity-rich.
- **Villages/Communities**:
  - **Hamlin**: Town center with shops/restaurants, Publix, West Orange YMCA
  - **Lakeside**: Resort-style amenities, lakefront
  - **Bridgewater**: Family-focused, trails, parks
  - **Waterleigh**: Lakeshore living, boat launch
  - **Summerlake**: More affordable Horizon West option
  - **Lakeshore**: New community by Mattamy
- **Price range**: Townhomes from $350K, single-family $450K-$800K
- **Builders**: Lennar, Pulte, Taylor Morrison, Meritage, M/I Homes, Mattamy
- **CDD fees**: $2,000-$3,500/year
- **Schools**: New schools being built rapidly. Mix of ratings as they establish.
- **Why buy here**: New everything, family amenities, relatively affordable new construction
- **Why not**: Traffic is terrible (Horizon West Connector under construction). Far from downtown. No character yet — everything is new and identical. CDD fees add $200-$300/month.

### Sunbridge (East of Lake Nona)
- **Size**: 24,000 acres — one of Florida's largest master-planned communities
- **Developer**: Tavistock (same as Lake Nona)
- **Character**: Next-gen Lake Nona. Conservation-focused, outdoor living.
- **Key features**: Extensive trails, green spaces, Del Webb 55+ community, innovation district
- **Sub-communities**: Del Webb Sunbridge (55+), Weslyn Park
- **Price range**: Del Webb from high $300Ks, single-family $400K-$700K
- **CDD fees**: $2,000-$3,500/year
- **Why buy here**: Ground floor of massive growth area. Lake Nona adjacency.
- **Why not**: Very early stage. Limited commercial/dining. Far from everything currently.

### Celebration
- **Size**: 4,900 acres
- **Developer**: Originally Walt Disney Company (1994). Sold to Lexin Capital (2004).
- **Character**: Neo-traditional town design. White picket fences, town center, community events.
- **Key features**: Downtown with shops/restaurants, golf course, walking trails, pools
- **Price range**: Townhomes from $400K, single-family $500K-$900K, estates $1M+
- **CDD fees**: Moderate
- **HOA**: Very strict architectural standards
- **Why buy here**: Beautiful community, Disney proximity, strong community
- **Why not**: Prices declining (-10% YoY). Aging infrastructure. Strict HOA. CDD + HOA costs.

### Storey Park (Narcoossee Corridor)
- **Size**: 1,600 acres
- **Developer**: Lennar primarily
- **Character**: Family-oriented, mixed-use, between Lake Nona and airport
- **Price range**: Townhomes from $350K, single-family $400K-$600K
- **CDD fees**: $2,000-$3,000/year

### Randal Park
- **Size**: 1,000+ acres near Lake Nona
- **Developer**: Multiple builders
- **Character**: Established master-planned, walkable to retail
- **Price range**: $450K-$700K
- **Mostly built out**: Limited new construction remaining

## New Construction Process & Tips

### Timeline (Contract to Close)
- **Spec/inventory homes**: 30-60 days (already built or nearly complete)
- **To-be-built (pre-construction)**: 6-12 months depending on builder/market
- Current build times have improved from pandemic peak of 14-18 months

### Pricing Structure
- **Base price**: Starting price for model and lot
- **Lot premium**: $5,000-$50,000+ for preferred lots (lakefront, conservation, corner, cul-de-sac)
- **Structural options**: Must decide before construction (extra bedroom, extended lanai, etc.) — $10K-$80K
- **Design center upgrades**: Cabinets, countertops, flooring, fixtures — $15K-$100K+ depending on taste
- **Realistic budget**: Add 10-20% to base price for lot premium + options + upgrades

### Current Builder Incentives (2025-2026)
- **Rate buydowns**: 1-2 point buydowns (saving $200-$400/month)
- **Closing cost credits**: $5,000-$20,000
- **Free upgrades**: Appliance packages, flooring upgrades
- **Design center credits**: $10,000-$30,000 toward upgrades
- Many incentives require using builder's preferred lender
- Incentives are **most aggressive** when inventory is high — current market favors buyers

### Buyer's Agent for New Construction
- Always bring your own agent — builder's on-site agent represents the builder
- Some builders require agent to register on first visit
- Buyer's agent can negotiate on your behalf and review contracts
- New construction contracts are builder-drafted (not FAR/BAR) — more builder-favorable

### What to Inspect
- **Always get an independent home inspection** even on new construction
- Consider inspections at multiple stages: pre-drywall, pre-closing, 11-month warranty
- Common new construction issues: paint touch-ups, caulking, grading/drainage, minor drywall imperfections
- Structural issues are rare but possible — independent inspector catches what builder's QC misses

### Warranties
- **Florida statutory warranty**: 1 year general workmanship, 3 years mechanical (plumbing, electrical, HVAC), 10 years structural
- Most builders also offer their own warranty programs
- Many builders use third-party warranty companies (e.g., 2-10 Home Warranty)
- **Document everything** during warranty period

## 55+ / Active Adult Communities

### Del Webb Communities (PulteGroup)
- **Del Webb Sunbridge** (St. Cloud): 27,000 sqft clubhouse, pools, pickleball, tennis. From high $300Ks.
- **Del Webb Oasis** (Winter Garden): Near Disney, 12,000 sqft clubhouse. Mid $400Ks+.
- **Del Webb Minneola** (near Clermont): Newest Orlando-area Del Webb. From $400Ks.
- **Brand reputation**: Gold standard of 55+ communities. Strong social programming.

### Latitude Margaritaville (Daytona Beach)
- Not in Orlando metro but close enough to mention — 45 min from Orlando
- Jimmy Buffett-themed. Resort lifestyle.
- From mid $300Ks. Very popular.

### Other 55+ Options
- **Solivita** (Poinciana/Kissimmee) — Large, established, AV Homes/Taylor Morrison. From $300Ks.
- **Four Seasons at Orlando** — Smaller, boutique 55+ option
- **Various builder 55+ sections** within master-planned communities (Horizon West, Sunbridge, etc.)

### 55+ Buyer Considerations
- At least one resident must be 55+ (per federal Fair Housing Act)
- No minimum age for additional residents, but restrictions on children under 18
- Lower HOA fees than resort communities
- CDD fees still apply in newer communities
- Florida's no-income-tax and homestead exemption make it attractive for retirees
- Healthcare access: Orlando has world-class medical infrastructure (Orlando Health, AdventHealth)

## Construction Quality & Issues

### Standard Orlando Construction
- **Foundation**: Slab-on-grade (no basements — water table too high in Florida)
- **Walls**: Concrete block (CBS) for first floor, sometimes wood frame for second
- **Roof**: Asphalt shingles (15-25 year lifespan) or concrete/clay tile (30-50 years)
- **Exterior**: Stucco over block is dominant. Hardy board/fiber cement growing in popularity.
- **Windows**: Impact-rated or hurricane shutters required by code
- **HVAC**: Central AC is essential. Heat pumps standard for energy efficiency.

### Common Issues to Watch
- **Stucco cracking**: Cosmetic vs structural — hairline cracks are normal, wide cracks need evaluation
- **Settling**: Normal in first 1-2 years on Florida's sandy soil
- **Drainage/grading**: Critical in flat terrain — water must flow away from foundation
- **Chinese drywall**: Historical issue (2001-2009). Should not be present in new construction.
- **Hurricane-rated windows**: Verify rating (Miami-Dade NOA is gold standard)
- **Roof underlayment**: Peel-and-stick recommended over felt paper for hurricane resistance

### Energy Efficiency
- Florida Energy Code mandates minimum efficiency standards
- HERS (Home Energy Rating System) scores: Lower is better. New homes typically 50-65.
- Most builders offer solar-ready or solar-included options
- Meritage Homes leads in energy efficiency among production builders
