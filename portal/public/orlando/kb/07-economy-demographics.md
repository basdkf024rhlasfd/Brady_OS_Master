# Economy & Demographics

Orlando's economic engine and the people who power it.

---

## Population & Growth

### Current Population
- **Orlando-Kissimmee-Sanford MSA**: 2,940,513 (July 2024)
- **City of Orlando proper**: ~320,000
- **Growth rate**: 2.7% (2024) — **highest among the 30 most populous US metros**
- **New residents**: ~76,000 added in 2024 alone
- Since 2020: +267,126 new residents
- ~1,500 new residents per week

### Growth Composition
- **65% of recent growth** from international migration
- Top origin states for domestic migration: New York, New Jersey, California, Illinois, Connecticut
- Top international origins: Latin America (Brazil, Colombia, Venezuela), Caribbean (Puerto Rico, Haiti)
- Orlando growing faster than Miami, Houston, Dallas in recent years

### Population Projections
- Expected to reach **3.5 million by 2030**
- Expected to reach **4+ million by 2035**
- Growth driven by: jobs, no income tax, cost of living, climate, lifestyle

### Demographics
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|

| Median household income | $62,000-$68,000 |

## Major Employers

### Tourism & Hospitality

**Walt Disney World Resort**
- **Employees**: ~75,000 (largest single-site employer in the US)
- **Economic impact**: $40.3 billion for Florida (FY 2022)
- **Jobs generated**: 250,000+ across state (direct and indirect)
- **Properties**: 4 theme parks, 2 water parks, Disney Springs, 25+ resort hotels
- **Impact on RE**: Drives housing demand across south Orange County and Osceola County. Employee housing is a massive market.

**Universal Orlando Resort**
- **Employees**: ~35,000 (pre-Epic Universe)
- **Economic impact**: $44 billion for US economy (2019-2023)
- **Epic Universe**: Opened 2025 — expected to generate **$2 billion** in year one for Florida
  - Created 65,000 construction jobs
  - Adding 17,500+ permanent jobs in first year
  - Fourth theme park plus hotels, dining, retail
- **Impact on RE**: Drives demand in I-Drive corridor, MetroWest, Dr. Phillips area

**SeaWorld Parks & Entertainment**
- **Employees**: ~8,000
- **Properties**: SeaWorld, Aquatica, Discovery Cove
- **Impact on RE**: Supports housing demand in south Orange County

### Healthcare

**AdventHealth (Adventist Health System)**
- **Employees**: ~35,000 in Central Florida
- **Facilities**: Multiple hospitals across metro, flagship campus in Altamonte Springs
- **Impact**: Major employer driving housing demand in Seminole County and north Orange County

**Orlando Health**
- **Employees**: ~25,000+
- **Facilities**: Orlando Regional Medical Center (Level 1 Trauma), multiple community hospitals
- **Impact**: Downtown Orlando and Lake Nona campuses drive local housing demand

**Nemours Children's Hospital**
- Located in Lake Nona Medical City
- 100+ bed pediatric hospital
- Draws medical professionals to Lake Nona corridor

**VA Medical Center (Lake Nona)**
- Serves 400,000+ veterans
- 134-bed hospital + nursing home + domiciliary
- First new VA hospital built in US in 20+ years

### Defense & Aerospace

**Lockheed Martin**
- **Employees**: ~8,000 in Orlando area
- **Focus**: Missiles and Fire Control division headquartered in Orlando
- **Impact**: High-paying jobs ($80K-$150K+) driving demand in east Orlando, UCF corridor

**Northrop Grumman**
- **Employees**: ~3,000 in Orlando
- **Focus**: Defense electronics, simulation systems

**L3Harris Technologies**
- **Employees**: ~7,000 in Melbourne/Orlando corridor
- **Focus**: Defense communications, space systems

**Naval Air Warfare Center (NAWC)**
- Training and simulation center
- Part of Orlando's "Modeling, Simulation and Training" corridor

### Technology & Gaming

**EA (Electronic Arts)**
- Major game development studio (EA Sports — Madden, FIFA/EA FC)
- Tiburon campus in Maitland
- High-paying tech jobs ($80K-$200K+)

**Modeling, Simulation & Training (MS&T) Corridor**
- Orlando is the **world capital of simulation**
- $6+ billion industry
- Military, commercial, and entertainment simulation
- Companies: CAE, Bohemia Interactive Simulations, SAIC, Raytheon

**Emerging Tech**
- **NeoCity** (Osceola County): Semiconductor and advanced manufacturing hub
- BRIDG: Microelectronics manufacturing facility
- Growing fintech, healthtech, and SaaS presence

### Education

**University of Central Florida (UCF)**
- Enrollment: **70,000+** (one of largest US universities)
- Economic impact: Billions annually
- Drives housing demand in east Orlando, Oviedo, Waterford Lakes
- Downtown campus (Creative Village) adding urban housing demand

**Full Sail University**
- Enrollment: ~20,000 (entertainment/media focus)
- Located in Winter Park
- Film, gaming, music, and digital arts

**Valencia College**
- Enrollment: ~60,000
- Multiple campuses across metro
- Workforce development pipeline

## Economic Indicators

### Triple Crown Achievement
Orlando achieved the "Triple Crown" in 2024 — leading the nation in:
1. **Job growth**: 2.5% YoY (37,500 jobs added in 2024)
2. **Population growth**: 2.7%
3. **GDP growth**: Outpacing national average

### Key Employment Sectors by Size
1. Leisure & Hospitality (largest)
2. Professional & Business Services
3. Trade, Transportation & Utilities
4. Education & Health Services
5. Government
6. Construction

### Orlando Economic Partnership Impact
- Over 8 years: facilitated **30,000 new jobs** and **$3.6 billion** in capital investment
- Focus areas: attracting corporate relocations, supporting startups, workforce development

### Unemployment
- Orlando unemployment rate: ~3.5-4.0% (2025)
- Below historical average but slightly above pandemic-era lows
- Tourism sector creates seasonal fluctuations

### Income Trends
- Median household income growing but lagging home price appreciation
- Tech, healthcare, and defense jobs pay above metro median
- Tourism/hospitality wages, while growing, remain below metro median
- Wage growth: ~4-5% annually (2023-2025)

## Tourism Engine

### By the Numbers
- **Annual visitors**: 74+ million (2023) — one of the most visited destinations on Earth
- **Tourism employment**: ~450,000 direct and indirect jobs
- **Convention center**: Orange County Convention Center is 2nd largest in US (7M sqft)
- **Hotel rooms**: 130,000+ across metro

### Impact on Real Estate
- Creates massive workforce housing demand (450K jobs need housing)
- Drives international buyer interest (brand recognition)
- Vacation home market supports prices in south/west metro
- Convention traffic supports STR demand beyond theme parks
- Restaurant Row (Sand Lake Rd) and I-Drive corridor create commercial RE demand

### Epic Universe Impact (2025+)
- Universal's fifth gate — largest expansion in company history
- **$2 billion expected first-year economic impact**
- 17,500+ new jobs created
- Expected to increase total Orlando visitor count by millions
- 4,000+ new hotel rooms in development around I-Drive
- Will increase demand for housing in MetroWest, Dr. Phillips, I-Drive corridor

## Cost of Living

### Orlando vs. National Average
| Category | Orlando vs. US Average |
|----------|----------------------|
| Overall | ~3-5% below national average |
| Housing | ~5-10% below national average |
| Groceries | Near national average |
| Transportation | Near national average |
| Healthcare | 5-10% below national average |
| Utilities | 5-10% above national average (AC costs) |

### No State Income Tax Impact
- Florida has no personal income tax
- For someone earning $100K: saves $5,000-$9,000/year vs. states like NY, CA, NJ
- For someone earning $200K: saves $10,000-$20,000/year
- This effective raise is a major driver of migration
- Also benefits retirees on fixed income
