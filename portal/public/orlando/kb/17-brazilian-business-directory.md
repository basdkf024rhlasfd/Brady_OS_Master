## Community Overview

### Population & Presence
- **~66,000** Brazilian residents in Orlando metro (Census-based estimate, 2024)
- **~190,000** per Brazilian Embassy estimates (includes visa holders and undocumented)
- **300,000+** Brazilian-born residents statewide in Florida
- **100+** Brazilian companies with **115+ branch locations** in Florida
- Orlando is the **second-largest Brazilian community in the US** after the Boston metro (Framingham/Marlborough corridor)

## Key Brazilian Business Corridors

### Geographic Concentration

| Corridor | Primary Business Types | Neighborhoods Served | Median Home Price (2026) |
|----------|----------------------|---------------------|------------------------|
| **International Drive (I-Drive)** | Restaurants, bakeries, groceries, salons, retail | Tourist corridor / Dr. Phillips | $500K–$800K (Dr. Phillips) |
| **South Kirkman Road** | Groceries, casual dining, services | MetroWest | $280K–$400K |
| **Kissimmee / US-192** | Restaurants, markets, churches | Kissimmee residential | $337K |
| **Metrowest Boulevard** | Insurance, financial, professional services | MetroWest | $280K–$400K |
| **Dr. Phillips / Sand Lake Road** | Upscale dining, professional services | Dr. Phillips | $500K–$800K |
| **Hunters Creek** | Residential-adjacent services, churches | Hunters Creek | $380K–$500K |

### Corridor Relevance for Home Buyers

- **Buyers prioritizing Brazilian cultural infrastructure** should target homes within 15 minutes of I-Drive or Kissimmee corridors
- **Dr. Phillips** ($500K–$800K) offers the densest overlap of Brazilian community + upscale dining + strong schools
- **Kissimmee** ($337K) and **Hunters Creek** ($380K–$500K) offer more affordable access to Brazilian services
- **MetroWest** ($280K–$400K) is the most affordable option with direct access to Brazilian groceries and services on S Kirkman Rd
- **For STR investors**: Brazilian business corridors are in Orange County (no STR). STR-eligible areas (Osceola overlay, ChampionsGate) are 20–40 min from core Brazilian community
