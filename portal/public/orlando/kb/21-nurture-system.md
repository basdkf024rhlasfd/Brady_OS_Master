# Post-Close Nurture System

Complete nurture system for Orlando real estate agents — vendor referral lists, check-in scripts, review/referral timing, anniversary touches, and a 12-month post-close calendar.

### Area Clusters

Organize referrals by geographic area — vendors serve local zones, not all of Orlando:

| Cluster | Neighborhoods Included | Key Vendors to Track |
|---------|----------------------|---------------------|
| **Southwest** | Dr. Phillips, Windermere, Horizon West, Hamlin | Pool, lawn, HVAC — larger lots, more maintenance |
| **Southeast / Tourist** | Kissimmee, ChampionsGate, Davenport, Reunion | STR-specific: cleaning, PM, handyman with rapid response |
| **East** | UCF area, Avalon Park, Waterford Lakes | Student housing handyman, pest control, lawn care |
| **North** | Sanford, Oviedo, Winter Springs | HVAC, roof — older homes need more maintenance |
| **Central** | Winter Park, Baldwin Park, MetroWest | Smaller lots, condo-specific vendors |
| **South** | Poinciana, Haines City, St. Cloud | Budget-friendly vendors, bilingual services |

### Vetting Criteria

Before adding a vendor to your referral list:
- **Licensed and insured** — verify on Florida DBPR (myfloridalicense.com)
- **Google reviews**: 4.0+ stars with 50+ reviews
- **Responsiveness**: Returns calls within 4 hours
- **Fair pricing**: Get 3 quotes, compare to ranges above
- **Client feedback**: At least 2 past clients have used and confirmed quality
- **No kickback arrangements** — refer based on quality, not commission splits

## 30-Day Check-In

Call (not text) the client 30 days after closing. This is the most important single touchpoint in the first year — it catches problems early, builds trust, and opens the door for referrals.

### Conversation Script

```
Hi [Name], it's [Agent Name]. I wanted to check in and
see how you're settling into the new house!

1. "How's everything going with the house? Loving it?"
   → Let them talk. Listen for signals.

2. "Have you had any issues — plumbing, AC, anything
   unexpected?"
   → If yes: "Let me send you my [vendor] contact — they're
   great and I've used them for other clients."
   → This is where your vendor list pays off.

3. "Did you get your homestead exemption filed?"
   → If primary residence and haven't filed: "The deadline
   is March 1 — I'll send you the link. It'll save you
   $500–$1,500 a year in property taxes."
   → Link: Orange County (ocpafl.org), Osceola (property
   appraiser.osceola.org), Seminole (scpafl.org)

4. "Have you set up your hurricane supplies? We're
   coming up on season."
   → If June–November: Remind about hurricane kit,
   shutters, insurance deductible awareness.

5. "Any neighbors you've met that are thinking about
   buying or selling?"
   → Soft referral ask. Don't push — just plant the seed.

6. "I'm here if you ever need anything — even if it's
   just a vendor recommendation or a question about
   the neighborhood."

Close with: "I'll check in again in a few months.
Congrats again on the house!"
```

### What to Listen For

| Signal | What It Means | Follow-Up Action |
|--------|--------------|------------------|
| "We love it!" | Happy client — prime for referral | Send review request at day 45 |
| "It's been tough with [issue]" | Problem to solve — chance to add value | Connect with vendor, follow up in 1 week |
| "We're already thinking about..." | Future transaction signal | Note it, follow up appropriately |
| "My friend/family is looking..." | Referral opportunity | Ask for introduction, don't be pushy |
| Doesn't answer/return call | Disengaged | Try text, then email. Don't over-pursue |

## Review & Referral Request

### Timing

| Touchpoint | Days Post-Close | What to Request |
|-----------|----------------|-----------------|
| Google review | Day 45–60 | Written review on Google Business Profile |
| Zillow review | Day 60–75 | Review on Zillow agent profile |
| Referral ask (soft) | Day 30 | Mentioned casually in 30-day check-in |
| Referral ask (direct) | Day 90–120 | Specific ask for introductions |

### Why Not Immediately?

- Day 1–30: Client is overwhelmed with moving, unpacking, utilities, address changes
- Day 30–45: Settling in, starting to appreciate the home
- Day 45–60: **Peak satisfaction** — they've made it their own, problems resolved, ready to share
- Day 90+: Enough time to have conversations with friends/family about their new home

### Google Review Request Template

```
Subject: Quick favor — would mean a lot 🏠

Hi [Name],

I hope you're enjoying [address]! It's been about
[X weeks] since closing, and I wanted to ask a
small favor.

If you had a good experience working with me, would
you mind leaving a quick Google review? Even a few
sentences makes a huge difference for my business.

Here's the direct link: [Your Google Review Link]

No pressure at all — and if there's anything you need
help with on the house, I'm always a call away.

Thanks so much,
[Agent Name]
```

### Zillow Review Request Template

```
Subject: Zillow review?

Hi [Name],

One more small ask — if you're open to it, a review
on my Zillow profile would be really helpful. Many
buyers start their search there, and reviews help me
reach more people.

Link: [Your Zillow Agent Profile URL]

Thanks again for trusting me with your purchase!

[Agent Name]
```

### Direct Referral Ask (Day 90–120)

```
Hi [Name], it's [Agent Name]. Hope things are going
great at [address]!

I have a quick question — in your circle of friends,
family, or coworkers, is there anyone who's been
talking about buying or selling a home? I'm always
happy to help, even if they just have questions.

No pressure — just thought I'd ask since I know you
probably know a lot of people making moves.

Thanks, [Name]!
```

### When NOT to Ask

- Client had a difficult closing experience (wait until resolved)
- Client reported post-close issues that haven't been fixed
- Client seems disengaged or hasn't responded to check-ins
- During holidays or stressful seasons (mid-December, back-to-school)

## Annual Anniversary

### Equity Estimate Template

Send a personalized equity update on the client's purchase anniversary. Uses purchase price + metro/area appreciation to estimate current value.

### How to Calculate

1. **Pull purchase price** from your CRM or closing records
2. **Get area appreciation rate** from `kb/01-market-overview.md` or Zillow ZHVI/Redfin data
3. **Estimate current value**: Purchase Price × (1 + Annual Appreciation Rate)^Years
4. **Calculate equity gained**: Current Value – Purchase Price – Remaining Mortgage Balance

### Appreciation Rates for Estimation (Orlando Metro, 2023–2026 Average)

| Area | Average Annual Appreciation |
|------|----------------------------|
| Metro-wide | 4.0–5.5% |
| Lake Nona / Medical City | 5.5–7.0% |
| Horizon West / Hamlin | 5.0–6.5% |
| Winter Park | 4.5–6.0% |
| East Orlando / UCF | 4.0–5.5% |
| Dr. Phillips / Windermere | 4.0–5.5% |
| Kissimmee | 3.5–5.0% |
| Poinciana | 3.0–4.5% |
| Tourist corridor (STR) | 3.0–5.0% (volatile) |

## Nurture Calendar

Complete 12-month post-close communication schedule. Every touchpoint has a purpose and a template reference.

### Month-by-Month Schedule

| Month | Touchpoint | Channel | Template/Content | Purpose |
|-------|-----------|---------|-----------------|---------|
| **Month 1** | Move-in package | In-person/mail | Vendor referral card + welcome note | Immediate value, brand impression |
| **Month 1** | 30-day check-in | Phone call | 30-Day Check-In script (above) | Problem resolution, relationship building |
| **Month 2** | Google review request | Email/text | Review Request template | Build online reputation |
| **Month 2** | Zillow review request | Email | Zillow Review template | Expand review presence |
| **Month 3** | Homestead reminder | Email/text | Reminder with filing link (if before March 1) | Tax savings, add value |
| **Month 3** | Referral ask | Text/call | Direct Referral Ask script | Generate referral business |
| **Month 4** | Market update | Email | Market snapshot from `kb/01-market-overview.md` | Stay top of mind, demonstrate expertise |
| **Month 5** | Hurricane prep (if May–Nov) | Email | Hurricane prep checklist, insurance reminder | Seasonal relevance, safety |
| **Month 6** | 6-month check-in | Phone/text | "How's everything? Any maintenance needs?" | Relationship maintenance |
| **Month 7** | Market update | Email | Quarterly market stats | Market education |
| **Month 8** | Vendor check-in | Text | "How was [vendor name]? Need any other recs?" | Vendor feedback loop |
| **Month 9** | Community event invite | Email/social | Local event, client appreciation event, or holiday gathering | Build community, face-to-face time |
| **Month 10** | Property tax reminder | Email/text | "FL property tax bills are out — pay by Nov for 4% discount" | Save client money, demonstrate expertise |
| **Month 11** | Market update + year-end prep | Email | Year-in-review market stats, CPA reminder for investors | Year-end planning, tax prep reminder |
| **Month 12** | Anniversary equity update | Email/call | Anniversary Message template (above) | Major touchpoint, equity update, referral ask |

### Cadence Summary

| Channel | Frequency | Total Year 1 Touches |
|---------|-----------|---------------------|
| Phone call | 3 (months 1, 6, 12) | 3 |
| Email | 7 (months 2, 3, 4, 7, 9, 10, 11) | 7 |
| Text | 3 (months 2, 3, 8) | 3 |
| In-person/mail | 1 (month 1 welcome package) | 1 |
| **Total** | | **14 touches** |

### Year 2+ Cadence

After the first year, transition to a lighter cadence:

| Frequency | Touchpoint | Channel |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Monthly | Market update email (from Sphere system) | Email |
| Quarterly | Personal check-in (call or text) | Phone/text |
| Annually | Anniversary equity update | Email |
| Annually | Holiday card or gift | Mail |
| As needed | Vendor referrals, market questions | Text/call |

### Tools & Automation

| Task | Manual or Automated | Tool Suggestion |
|------|-------------------|-----------------|
| 30-day / 6-month / anniversary reminders | Automated (CRM task) | Follow Up Boss, LionDesk, or calendar reminders |
| Review request emails | Semi-automated (template + manual send) | Email template, sent personally |
| Market update emails | Automated (drip campaign) | Mailchimp, CRM email, or curated newsletter |
| Vendor referral delivery | Manual (personal touch) | Text or email with specific recommendations |
| Referral ask | Manual (relationship-dependent) | Call or text — never automated |
| Property tax / homestead reminders | Automated (seasonal) | Batch email to applicable clients |
