# Luxury Market

Orlando's million-dollar neighborhoods, custom builders, and ultra-luxury communities.

---

## Luxury Market Overview

### Current Statistics (2025)
- **Median luxury sale price**: $1.37 million (metro Orlando, April 2025)
- **Year-over-year change**: +7.4% (outpacing national average)
- **Market dynamic**: Rising prices but lower transaction volume — affluent buyers cautious
- **Pending and completed sales down** even as prices rise
- **Days on market**: 80-120+ days for $1M+ properties

### What Drives Orlando Luxury
- **No state income tax**: Attracts wealth from NY, NJ, CA, CT, IL
- Florida added **5,700+ new million-dollar earners** in 2021-2022
- Remote work enables high earners to relocate from HCOL states
- Athletes, executives, celebrities seeking privacy + amenities
- International wealth (Latin American, European, Canadian)
- Theme park executives and entertainment industry professionals

## Ultra-Luxury Neighborhoods

### Isleworth (Windermere)
- **Price range**: $2M-$20M+
- **What it is**: Ultra-exclusive gated golf community on Butler Chain of Lakes
- **Residents**: Pro athletes, CEOs, celebrities (Tiger Woods' former community)
- **Amenities**: 18-hole Arnold Palmer golf course, 540 acres of lakefront, tennis, fitness
- **Homes**: Custom estates on large lots, many lakefront
- **Entry**: Club membership required. Properties rarely hit public market.
- **HOA**: Premium — full-service community

### Golden Oak at Walt Disney World
- **Price range**: $2M-$12M+
- **What it is**: Luxury residential community INSIDE Walt Disney World property
- **Residents**: Disney enthusiasts with deep pockets, international buyers
- **Amenities**: Exclusive access to Disney parks, private clubhouse (Summerhouse), concierge, golf (Four Seasons)
- **Homes**: ~400 home sites, custom homes by Disney-approved builders
- **Unique**: Only residential community within Disney World
- **HOA**: $2,000-$3,000+/month (includes substantial amenity access)

### Winter Park Estates (Interlachen, Via Tuscany, Genius Drive)
- **Price range**: $1M-$10M+
- **What it is**: Historic luxury enclaves in Winter Park's toniest areas
- **Residents**: Old Florida money, corporate executives, Rollins College affiliated
- **Notable streets**: Interlachen Avenue, Palmer Avenue, Genius Drive, Via Tuscany
- **Character**: Lakefront estates, historic homes, mature oak canopy
- **Walkability**: Many within walking distance to Park Avenue shops/restaurants

### Windermere Lakefront (Non-Isleworth)
- **Price range**: $1M-$8M+
- **What it is**: Lakefront estates on the Butler Chain, Wauseon Bay, Lake Down, etc.
- **Residents**: Executives, athletes, business owners
- **Character**: Large lots (0.5-5+ acres), private docks, lake views
- **Key areas**: Windermere proper, Keene's Pointe, Lake Butler Sound

### Lake Nona Estates
- **Price range**: $1M-$3M+
- **What it is**: Premium custom-home section of Lake Nona
- **Residents**: Medical professionals, tech executives, Lake Nona corporate leaders
- **Character**: Modern architecture, technology-forward, new construction
- **Growth**: Active luxury development — more new luxury inventory than Winter Park/Windermere

### Bay Hill (Dr. Phillips Area)
- **Price range**: $800K-$3M+
- **What it is**: Arnold Palmer's Bay Hill Club & Lodge community
- **Residents**: Golfers, retirees, executives
- **Character**: Country club lifestyle, PGA Tour event venue
- **Amenities**: 27 holes of golf, lodge, dining, spa

### Custom Build Process in Orlando
- **Land**: Custom lots in luxury communities or lakefront parcels
  - Windermere/Winter Park lots: $300K-$2M+ (lakefront premium)
  - Lake Nona estate lots: $200K-$800K
- **Build cost**: $250-$500+/sqft for custom construction
- **Timeline**: 12-24 months from design to completion
- **Permitting**: Orange County permitting can be slow — factor 2-4 months for permits
- **Architecture**: Mix of Mediterranean, modern, transitional, and Florida vernacular styles

### Winter Park Condos
- Limited luxury condo inventory
- Select boutique buildings near Park Avenue
- Typically $400K-$1.5M
- Strong demand, limited supply

### Key Considerations for Luxury Condos
- HOA fees: $600-$1,500+/month for luxury buildings
- Insurance: Condo association master policy + individual HO-6 policy
- Reserves: Verify adequacy per SB 4-D requirements
- Rental restrictions: Some luxury condos restrict or prohibit rentals
- Resale value: Condos in Orlando generally appreciate slower than single-family

## Luxury Market Trends

### International Luxury Buyers
- **Brazil**: Largest international buyer group — attracted by Orlando lifestyle, investment potential, no income tax
- **Colombia/Venezuela**: Political and economic instability driving wealth migration
- **UK/Canada**: Vacation home demand, currency play
- **Northeast US**: Tax migration (NY, NJ, CT high earners)
- **California**: Tech wealth seeking lower cost and taxes

### Post-Pandemic Shifts
- Remote work enabled luxury buyers to prioritize lifestyle over proximity to office
- Orlando's outdoor lifestyle, space, and amenities became more attractive
- Pool homes in high demand — large lot premium increased
- Home office/gym space now expected in luxury properties

### New Luxury Construction
- **Toll Brothers**: Leading luxury production builder in Orlando
- **Tavistock** (Lake Nona): Master-planned luxury with tech integration
- Trend toward modern/contemporary architecture (vs. traditional Mediterranean)
- Smart home integration expected at luxury level (whole-home automation, security)
- Outdoor living emphasis: Summer kitchens, covered lanais, resort-style pools

### Luxury Investment Considerations
- **Appreciation**: Strong in Windermere, Winter Park, Lake Nona (5-8% annually)
- **Carrying costs**: High — taxes, insurance, maintenance on $1M+ homes can be $3,000-$8,000/month
- **Liquidity**: Lower than mid-market — longer days on market, smaller buyer pool
- **Cap rates**: Poor for rental income (2-4%) — luxury is an appreciation play
- **Estate planning**: Florida's homestead protection is powerful for asset protection
  - Unlimited homestead exemption from creditors (for primary residence)
  - Protects home equity from most judgments
  - Major draw for high-net-worth individuals
