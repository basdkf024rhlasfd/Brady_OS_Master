## NRA Brazilian Buyer Profiles (Purchasing from Brazil)

The 6 profiles above are all US residents or citizens. The following 4 profiles represent **non-resident alien (NRA) buyers purchasing from Brazil** — the primary use case for `kb/15-brazilian-buyer-guide.md`.

## 21. Ricardo Martins 🇧🇷

- **Age:** 42
- **From:** Sao Paulo, Brazil (no US residency)
- **Income:** R$850K/year (~$155K USD) from tech consulting firm he co-owns
- **Status:** NRA, Brazilian passport, no ITIN yet
- **Kids:** 2 (ages 10, 7), stay in Brazil with his wife
- **What he wants:** 4BR/3BA pool home, $400–500K. Wants to Airbnb it year-round and use it 2–3 weeks/year for family vacations.
- **Motivation:** Brazilian real estate agent promised 10% annual returns. Wants USD-denominated asset as hedge against BRL depreciation.
- **Notes:** Cash buyer. Has never filed a US tax return. Doesn't know about FIRPTA, estate tax ($60K NRA exemption), or that Orange County bans non-resident STR. **This is the reality-check profile** — needs kb/15 the most.
- **Key issues:** STR zoning verification, estate tax exposure (~$176K on $500K property), entity setup sequence (no ITIN yet), realistic yield expectations (kb/16 Case E), dual compliance (Carne-Leao + 1040-NR).
