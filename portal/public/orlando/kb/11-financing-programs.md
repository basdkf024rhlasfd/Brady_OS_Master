# Financing & Assistance Programs

Loan types, limits, and assistance programs available to Orlando home buyers.

---

## Conventional Loans

### Conforming Loan Limits (2025)
- **Orlando MSA (Orange, Seminole, Osceola, Lake counties)**: $524,225 for single-family
- Same as national baseline — Orlando is not a "high-cost" area
- Properties above this limit require jumbo financing

### Typical Terms
- Down payment: 3-20%+ (5% is most common for conventional)
- PMI required if <20% down (~$100-$300/month on Orlando-priced homes)
- Credit score: 620+ minimum, 740+ for best rates
- Debt-to-income ratio: 43-45% maximum typically
- Interest rates: Market-dependent (6.5-7.5% range as of early 2026)

### Jumbo Loans
- Required for purchases above $524,225 with >80% LTV
- Higher credit requirements (typically 700+ FICO)
- Higher down payment (10-20%+ typically)
- Rates may be higher or comparable to conforming depending on lender
- Important for Lake Nona, Winter Park, Windermere purchases

## FHA Loans

### Orlando FHA Limits (2025)
| Property Type | FHA Limit |
|--------------|-----------|
| 1-unit | $524,225 |
| 2-unit | $671,200 |
| 3-unit | $811,275 |
| 4-unit | $1,008,300 |

### FHA Features
- Down payment: **3.5%** with 580+ FICO score
- Down payment: **10%** with 500-579 FICO score
- Upfront MIP: 1.75% of loan amount (can be rolled into loan)
- Annual MIP: 0.55% of loan balance (for life of loan with <10% down)
- More lenient credit requirements than conventional
- Allows seller concessions up to 6% of purchase price
- **Limitation**: Property must meet FHA minimum property standards
  - Can be challenging with older Orlando homes
  - No peeling paint, must have working systems, no safety hazards

### FHA for Orlando Buyers
- Very popular for first-time buyers
- $400K home with 3.5% FHA = $14,000 down payment
- Monthly payment (P&I + MIP): ~$2,600-$2,800 (not including taxes/insurance)
- Total monthly with taxes/insurance: ~$3,800-$4,200
- **Condo restriction**: FHA only approves specific condo projects — verify FHA approval before offering on condo

## VA Loans

### Eligibility
- Active duty military, veterans, eligible surviving spouses
- Certificate of Eligibility (COE) required
- **Orlando has significant military/veteran population** (defense industry, former bases, VA Medical Center)

### VA Loan Features
- **Zero down payment** (no maximum loan amount for eligible borrowers with full entitlement)
- No PMI/MIP
- VA funding fee: 1.25-3.3% (waived for disabled veterans)
- Competitive interest rates (often lowest available)
- No prepayment penalty
- More flexible credit requirements

### VA in Orlando Market
- Zero-down on a $400K home saves $14,000-$80,000 vs. FHA/conventional
- Monthly payment advantage of ~$200-$400/month vs. FHA (no MIP)
- VA appraisals can be strict — property must meet VA Minimum Property Requirements
- Some sellers resist VA offers due to appraisal/inspection reputation (though declining practice)

## USDA Loans

### Eligibility
- Rural areas as defined by USDA
- Income limits apply (115% of area median income)
- **Orlando proper** does not qualify — too urban
- **Eligible areas near Orlando**:
  - Parts of Apopka (far north)
  - Parts of Clermont/Groveland/Minneola
  - Parts of St. Cloud (east)
  - Parts of Geneva/Chuluota (Seminole County)
  - Parts of Poinciana (far south)
  - Most of Lake County outside Clermont core

### USDA Features
- **Zero down payment**
- Guarantee fee: 1% upfront + 0.35% annual
- Lower mortgage insurance than FHA
- Income limits: ~$103,500 for 1-4 person household (Orlando MSA)
- Must be primary residence

## Down Payment Assistance Programs

### City of Orlando DPA Program
- **Amount**: Up to **$45,000** in down payment and closing cost assistance
- **Structure**: Deferred, forgivable loan
- **Forgiveness**: Forgiven over **10 years** of owner-occupancy
- **Income limit**: Household income at or below **50% of AMI** for maximum amount
- **Requirements**: Must be first-time homebuyer, property in City of Orlando limits
- **Credit**: No minimum stated but must qualify for underlying mortgage
- **Property**: Must be owner-occupied primary residence

### Orange County DPA Program
- **Amount**: Tiered by income:
  | Income Level | Assistance Amount |
  |-------------|-------------------|
  | Very low income | Up to **$70,000** |
  | Low income | Up to **$40,000** |
  | Moderate income | Up to **$10,000** |
- **Requirements**:
  - First-time homebuyer
  - Minimum 640 credit score
  - Must contribute greater of $1,000 or 1.75% of purchase price from own funds
  - Property in Orange County
- **Structure**: Deferred loan, forgiven over time with continued occupancy

### Florida Housing Finance Corporation (FHFC) Programs

**Florida Assist**
- Up to **$7,500** toward down payment and closing costs
- Deferred, 0% interest second mortgage
- Repaid when home is sold, refinanced, or no longer primary residence
- Available with FHA, VA, or USDA first mortgages

**HLP (Homebuyer Loan Program)**
- Up to **$10,000** as 15-year, 3% interest second mortgage
- Monthly payments required
- Available with FHA, VA, or USDA first mortgages

**FL First Program**
- 30-year fixed-rate first mortgage at below-market rates
- Combined with Florida Assist or HLP second mortgage
- Income and purchase price limits apply
- Available through approved lenders statewide

### Osceola County Programs
- Various down payment assistance programs available
- Contact Osceola County Housing Division for current offerings
- Programs change frequently based on funding availability

### Seminole County Programs
- Limited county-specific DPA
- State programs (FHFC) available to Seminole County residents
- Check Seminole County Community Services for current programs

## First-Time Buyer Programs

### Florida Definition of "First-Time Buyer"
- Have not owned a home in the past **3 years**
- Veterans are often exempt from first-time buyer requirements for certain programs
- Some programs expand definition to include those who haven't owned in current county

### Federal Tax Benefits
- **Mortgage interest deduction**: Up to $750K of mortgage debt (itemized deductions)
- **Property tax deduction**: Up to $10,000 combined state/local taxes (SALT cap)
- **In Florida**: Since there's no state income tax, more of the SALT cap can be used for property taxes

### Homebuyer Education
- Most DPA programs require homebuyer education course
- Available online or in-person through HUD-approved counseling agencies
- Orlando-area HUD-approved agencies provide free or low-cost courses
- Typical course: 4-8 hours covering budgeting, mortgages, home maintenance

## Creative Financing Options

### Builder Financing Incentives
- Many Orlando builders offer **rate buydowns** (1-2 points, saving $200-$400/month)
- **Closing cost credits**: $5,000-$20,000
- Often require using builder's **preferred lender**
- Compare preferred lender terms vs. outside lender — preferred lender rate may be higher but overall package may be better
- Negotiate: Builders are offering generous incentives in current market

### Assumable Mortgages
- FHA, VA, and USDA loans are **assumable** (buyer takes over seller's loan)
- With rates having risen from 3% to 7%, sellers with sub-4% rates have valuable assumable mortgages
- Buyer must qualify with the lender
- Gap between loan balance and purchase price must be covered (down payment or second mortgage)
- Growing interest in assumptions in Orlando market

### Seller Financing
- Not common but possible, especially in:
  - Luxury market (cash sellers willing to finance)
  - Investor-to-investor transactions
  - Properties that struggle with traditional financing
- Terms are fully negotiable between buyer and seller
- Title company or attorney should handle documentation

## Cost Comparison: Renting vs. Buying in Orlando

### Typical Comparison ($400K Purchase vs. $2,200/month Rental)
| Monthly Cost | Buying | Renting |
|-------------|--------|---------|
| Mortgage P&I (5% down, 7%) | $2,525 | — |
| PMI | $175 | — |
| Property tax | $295 | — |
| Insurance | $375 | $25 (renter's) |
| HOA | $200 | — |
| Maintenance reserve | $300 | — |
| Rent | — | $2,200 |
| **Total monthly** | **~$3,870** | **~$2,225** |

### Break-Even Analysis
- With 3% annual appreciation: Break-even at ~5-7 years vs. renting
- With 0% appreciation: Break-even at ~10-12 years
- **Factors favoring buying**: Appreciation, equity buildup, tax benefits, homestead exemption, SOH cap
- **Factors favoring renting**: Lower monthly cost, flexibility, no maintenance responsibility, no insurance risk
- Orlando's strong population growth supports long-term appreciation thesis
