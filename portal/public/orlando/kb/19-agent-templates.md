## Lead Scoring

### Intake Questionnaire

Ask these questions during the first contact (call, text, or online form). Each answer feeds the scoring rubric below.

| # | Question | Why It Matters |
|---|----------|---------------|
| 1 | Are you currently renting, owning, or living with family? | Indicates urgency and equity position |
| 2 | Is your current lease ending? If so, when? | Hard deadline = higher urgency |
| 3 | Have you been pre-approved for a mortgage? By whom? | Pre-approval = serious. No pre-approval = early stage |
| 4 | What is your comfortable monthly payment (PITI)? | Reveals budget realism vs. aspiration |
| 5 | Are you a first-time homebuyer? | Affects financing options and education needs |
| 6 | Are you relocating to Orlando? If so, when and from where? | Relocation = high urgency, employer timeline |
| 7 | Do you currently own a home that needs to sell first? | Contingent buyer = longer, more complex timeline |
| 8 | What neighborhoods or areas are you interested in? | Tests market knowledge and flexibility |
| 9 | What are your must-haves? (beds, bath, pool, garage, etc.) | Determines search parameters |
| 10 | What are your deal-breakers? | Saves time on wrong properties |
| 11 | Are you buying as a primary residence or investment? | Changes financing, tax strategy, search areas |
| 12 | If investment: long-term rental, short-term rental, or flip? | Routes to correct KB content |
| 13 | Are you a US citizen/resident or international buyer? | Routes to Foreign Buyer flow if international |
| 14 | Have you been looking online? If so, for how long? | Time browsing = readiness indicator |
| 15 | Are you working with another agent currently? | Qualification gating question |
| 16 | What is your ideal move-in timeline? | Anchors the transaction timeline estimate |
| 17 | Do you have a real estate attorney or CPA? | Indicates sophistication and preparation |
| 18 | How did you hear about us? | Lead source tracking |
| 19 | On a scale of 1–10, how urgently do you need to move? | Self-reported urgency |
| 20 | What questions do you have for me? | Reveals concerns, priorities, engagement level |

### Lead Scoring Rubric

Score each factor and total. Routes the lead to the appropriate response cadence.

| Factor | Hot (+3) | Warm (+2) | Nurture (+1) | Cold (+0) |
|--------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| Pre-approval | Pre-approved, letter in hand | Started application | "Planning to" | No plans |
| Timeline | Under 60 days | 2–4 months | 4–6 months | 6+ months or "just looking" |
| Motivation | Relocating, lease ending, life event | Wants to buy, flexible timing | Casually exploring | No clear reason |
| Budget clarity | Knows monthly payment + down payment | Knows general range | "Whatever we qualify for" | No idea |
| Market knowledge | Knows areas, visited Orlando | Researching online 2+ months | Just started looking | No research |
| Agent commitment | Not working with another agent | "Talked to a few" | Signed with another (expired) | Under contract with another |
| Responsiveness | Replies within hours, asks questions | Replies within a day | Slow responses | Ghosting |

### Score Interpretation

| Total Score | Category | Response Cadence |
|-------------|----------|-----------------|
| 18–21 | **Hot** | Call within 1 hour. Schedule showing within 48 hours. |
| 12–17 | **Warm** | Call within 4 hours. Set up saved search. Follow up every 3 days. |
| 7–11 | **Nurture** | Email/text within 24 hours. Add to monthly drip campaign. Check in monthly. |
| 0–6 | **Cold** | Auto-responder + quarterly market update. No active pursuit. |

## Timeline Estimate Decision Tree

Estimate time from first contact to closing based on buyer type and pre-approval status.

### Decision Flow

```
START: What type of buyer?
│
├─ First-time buyer
│  ├─ Pre-approved? → YES → 45–75 days to close
│  └─ Pre-approved? → NO
│     ├─ Stable income + good credit → 60–90 days (add 2–4 weeks for pre-approval)
│     └─ Credit issues or self-employed → 90–180 days (credit repair, doc gathering)
│
├─ Move-up buyer (owns current home)
│  ├─ Current home already sold/under contract → 45–60 days
│  ├─ Current home listed → 75–120 days (contingent offer risk)
│  └─ Current home NOT listed → 120–180 days (must sell first or bridge loan)
│
├─ Relocating (employer-driven)
│  ├─ Relo package + pre-approved → 30–60 days (fastest)
│  └─ No relo package → 45–90 days
│
├─ Investor (cash)
│  ├─ Funds verified in US account → 21–30 days (cash close)
│  └─ Funds need to transfer (international) → 45–75 days
│
├─ Investor (financed)
│  ├─ Conventional investor loan → 45–60 days
│  └─ DSCR or portfolio loan → 30–45 days (faster underwriting)
│
├─ Foreign buyer (NRA)
│  ├─ Has ITIN + US bank account + funds → 45–75 days
│  └─ Needs ITIN, entity, fund transfer → 90–180 days
│
└─ Downsizer / retiree
   ├─ Cash from home sale → 30–45 days
   └─ Needs to sell first → 90–150 days
```

### Timeline Factors That Add Time

| Factor | Additional Time | Notes |
|--------|----------------|-------|
| No pre-approval | +14–28 days | Must complete before making offers |
| Contingent on home sale | +30–90 days | Depends on current market for their home |
| International fund transfer | +7–21 days | IOF processing, wire clearing |
| ITIN application | +49–98 days (7–14 weeks) | Apply ASAP — do not wait |
| Credit repair needed | +60–180 days | Work with lender on credit improvement plan |
| New construction (pre-build) | +120–360 days | Builder timeline, not buyer's |
| Estate/trust/entity purchase | +14–30 days | Additional documentation and title work |

## Escalation / Counter Strategy

Decision tree for structuring offers based on competition level, DOM, and seller signals.

### Offer Strategy Decision Tree

```
START: What is the competitive situation?
│
├─ MULTIPLE OFFERS EXPECTED (low DOM, seller's market, popular area)
│  ├─ Budget allows escalation clause?
│  │  ├─ YES → Offer at list price with escalation clause
│  │  │         Cap at max comfortable price (typically 3–5% above list)
│  │  │         Waive or shorten contingencies if comfortable
│  │  └─ NO → Offer at max budget, strong terms (quick close, larger EMD, flexible closing date)
│  └─ Is the property likely to appraise?
│     ├─ YES → Standard appraisal contingency OK
│     └─ NO → Offer appraisal gap coverage (up to $___) to strengthen offer
│
├─ SINGLE OFFER LIKELY (moderate DOM, balanced market)
│  ├─ Seller motivation score
│  │  ├─ LOW (0–3) → Offer 1–3% below list
│  │  ├─ MODERATE (4–7) → Offer 3–5% below list, ask for closing cost credit
│  │  └─ HIGH (8+) → Offer 5–8% below list, request concessions
│  └─ Standard contingencies: inspection, appraisal, financing
│
├─ STALE LISTING (DOM > stale threshold)
│  ├─ Price reductions already?
│  │  ├─ YES (2+ reductions) → Offer 8–12% below CURRENT list
│  │  └─ NO (stubbornly priced) → Offer at AVM value, expect counter
│  └─ Include all contingencies — you have leverage
│
└─ OFF-MARKET / POCKET LISTING
   ├─ No comparable offers → Offer at AVM value ± 3%
   └─ Use standard contingencies, normal timeline
```

### Counter Offer Strategy

| Scenario | Counter Approach |
|----------|-----------------|
| Seller counters within 2% of your offer | Accept or split the difference — deal is close |
| Seller counters at list price (ignoring your offer) | Re-offer with data (comps, DOM, feedback). Move up only if data supports it |
| Seller asks to remove contingencies | Negotiate shortened timelines instead of full removal |
| Seller asks for rent-back | Acceptable if short (under 30 days) with rent and deposit |
| Multiple counter (seller countering multiple buyers) | Best and final — go to max or walk |

## Negotiation Leverage Analysis

Score buyer leverage based on observable market signals. Present to client to set expectations before offering.

### Leverage Scoring

| Signal | Score Range | How to Assess |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| Days on Market vs. area average | 0–4 | At average = 0, Double average = 4 |
| Price reductions | 0–3 | None = 0, One = 1, Two+ = 3 |
| Active inventory in area (same bracket) | 0–3 | Low (<10) = 0, Moderate (10–25) = 1, High (25+) = 3 |
| Season | 0–2 | Peak (Mar–Jun) = 0, Shoulder (Jul–Sep) = 1, Off (Oct–Feb) = 2 |
| Seller motivation signals | 0–4 | From Seller Motivation scoring above |
| Interest rate environment | 0–2 | Rates falling = 0 (more buyers), Rising = 2 (fewer buyers) |
| Property condition | 0–2 | Move-in ready = 0, Needs work = 2 |

### Leverage Interpretation

| Total Score | Leverage Level | Negotiation Posture |
|-------------|---------------|-------------------|
| 0–4 | **Weak** — seller has the upper hand | Offer at or near list. Compete on terms, not price. |
| 5–9 | **Moderate** — fair negotiation expected | Offer 3–5% below list. Ask for 1–2 concessions. |
| 10–14 | **Strong** — buyer has significant leverage | Offer 5–10% below list. Request concessions, credits, repairs. |
| 15–20 | **Very strong** — distressed or stale situation | Aggressive offer warranted. Expect seller to counter, but you set the terms. |

### Objection Categories and Patterns

Track feedback over time. When a pattern emerges, take action:

| Category | If 3+ Buyers Flag This | Action |
|----------|----------------------|--------|
| **Price too high** | Market is telling you the price is wrong | Reduce — see Price Adjustment Rules |
| **Condition / needs updates** | Offer repair credit or reduce price to reflect | Consider pre-listing inspection credit |
| **Layout doesn't work** | Can't fix layout — target different buyer segment | Adjust marketing to highlight who it's FOR |
| **Location / neighborhood** | Can't fix location — price must compensate | Reduce or highlight location advantages differently |
| **HOA/CDD too high** | Can't change HOA/CDD — affects affordability | Ensure marketing materials show true monthly cost |
| **School zone** | Can't change schools — target non-family buyers or investors | Adjust marketing and target audience |
