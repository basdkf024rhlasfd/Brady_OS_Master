## Go-To Meals (rotation favorites)

> Derived from Walmart+ auto-ship items (see 14-walmart-subscriptions.md). These are starting suggestions based on ingredients the family already receives — not confirmed favorites. Brady: correct or rank these with `remember:` to make them real.

1. **Chicken & Rice Bowls** — Tyson chicken breasts (weekly) + ready-to-heat rice (biweekly) + broccoli florets (every 3 wks). Season to taste.
2. **Carnitas Tacos** — HERDEZ carnitas (monthly) + fajita flour tortillas (monthly) + shredded cheddar (biweekly) + fire roasted salsa (monthly). Top with romaine, tomato, avocado.
3. **Hot Dog Night** — Ball Park beef hot dogs (monthly) + Wonder Bread buns (every 3 wks). Side of green beans or chips.
4. **Chicken Ramen Upgrade** — Maruchan chicken ramen 12-pack (monthly) + sliced chicken breast + green beans or broccoli. Quick weeknight fallback.
5. **PB Sandwiches + Fruit** — Peanut butter (monthly) + sourdough bread (biweekly) + bananas (weekly) + strawberries (weekly). Zero-cook lunch.
6. **Pancake Dinner** — Buttermilk pancake mix (monthly) + eggs (biweekly) + butter (weekly). Strawberries on top.
7. **Chicken Quesadillas** — Chicken breasts + fajita tortillas + shredded cheddar + salsa. Quick assembly, kid-friendly.
8. **English Muffin Pizzas** — Thomas' sourdough English muffins (biweekly) + tomato bisque as sauce (monthly) + turkey pepperoni (monthly) + shredded cheddar. Broil 5 min.
9. **Japanese Curry Night** — S&B Golden Curry mix (every 6 wks) + chicken breasts + rice + potatoes (every 6 wks). Stretch with broccoli.
10. **Tomato Soup & Grilled Cheese** — Marketside tomato bisque (monthly) + sourdough bread + butter + shredded cheddar. Classic comfort combo.
11. **Breakfast-for-Dinner** — Pillsbury toaster scrambles (biweekly, 2 varieties) + eggs + sourdough toast with butter. Fast and filling.
12. **Chip & Dip Snack Plate** — Hummus (biweekly) + tortilla chips (biweekly) + avocado + tomato + romaine lettuce wraps. Light Friday option.

## Walmart+ Defaults

- **Delivery window**: Tuesdays, 10am–12pm
- **Substitution preferences**: Accept store-brand substitutions for Great Value items. Decline substitutions for name-brand items (HERDEZ carnitas, S&B curry, Thomas' muffins, Ball Park hot dogs, Maruchan ramen, Larabar). When in doubt, skip rather than substitute.
- **Regular restock items**: See 14-walmart-subscriptions.md for the full 67-item auto-ship list. Key proteins: chicken (weekly), carnitas + hot dogs + pepperoni (monthly). Key carbs: rice (biweekly), bread (biweekly), tortillas (monthly). Key produce: bananas + strawberries (weekly), broccoli (every 3 weeks).
