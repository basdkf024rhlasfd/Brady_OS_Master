## The Rule That Makes This Work

Every dollar of consulting income splits at the door:

- **30% → tax reserve account** (separate, untouchable, for federal + SE tax)
- **70% → operating account** (funds the three spending buckets below)

If gross is treated as available cash, the budget fails silently. The discovery happens in April at an ugly number.
