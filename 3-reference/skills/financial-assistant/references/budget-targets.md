## The Target

**$24,000 gross per month.** That's the number.

| Metric | Amount |
|--------|--------|
| Weekly gross target | **$5,539** |
| Monthly gross target | **$24,000** |
| Annual gross target | $288,000 |
| Monthly net (after 30% tax reserve) | $16,800 |
| Slack | ~$79/mo |

This is the minimum consulting revenue target to cover the declared baseline without drawing further from the HELOC. Anything less means the gap gets financed by debt.

- **Below target** → focus energy on pipeline / delivery / collections
- **At target** → maintain current rhythm
- **Above target** → allocate the surplus explicitly (don't let it drift into lifestyle creep)

---

## The Rule That Makes This Work

Every dollar of consulting income splits at the door:

- **30% → tax reserve account** (separate, untouchable, for federal + SE tax)
- **70% → operating account** (funds the three spending buckets below)

If gross is treated as available cash, the budget fails silently. The discovery happens in April at an ugly number.
