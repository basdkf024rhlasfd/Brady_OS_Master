## Aflac Group Critical Illness — $40,000 Plan Benefit Schedule

Plan year 01/01/2025–12/31/2025. Employee-pay-all voluntary. Pays a lump-sum **Initial Diagnosis Benefit** = $40,000 × applicable percentage on first diagnosis of a covered critical illness.

### Initial Diagnosis Benefit (% of $40,000 face amount)

| Covered Critical Illness | % | Lump Sum |
|---|---|---|
| **Cancer** (internal/invasive) | 100% | $40,000 |
| **Heart Attack** (myocardial infarction) | 100% | $40,000 |
| **Stroke** (ischemic or hemorrhagic) | 100% | $40,000 |
| **Kidney Failure** (end-stage renal failure) | 100% | $40,000 |
| **Bone Marrow Transplant** (stem cell transplant) | 100% | $40,000 |
| **Sudden Cardiac Arrest** | 100% | $40,000 |
| **Major Organ Transplant** (25% if listed only, 100% if performed) | 25–100% | $10,000–$40,000 |
| **Coma** | 100% | $40,000 |
| **Paralysis** | 100% | $40,000 |
| **Loss of Sight** | 100% | $40,000 |
| **Loss of Hearing** | 100% | $40,000 |
| **Loss of Speech** | 100% | $40,000 |
| **Benign Brain Tumor** | 100% | $40,000 |
| **Coronary Artery Bypass Surgery** | 25% | $10,000 |
| **Non-Invasive Cancer** | 25% | $10,000 |
| **Metastatic Cancer** (after first cancer benefit) | 25% | $10,000 |

### Recurrence Benefit
100% of original benefit if same critical illness recurs after **12 consecutive months** of separation.

### Additional Diagnosis Benefit
100% if a different covered critical illness is diagnosed at least 1 month after the prior.

### Skin Cancer Benefit
**$250 per calendar year** for diagnosis of skin cancer (separate from main benefit).

### Health Screening Benefit (both plans, NOT Texas)
**$50 per calendar year** for prevention exams (mammogram, colonoscopy, PSA, stress test, lipid panel, etc.).

### Successor Insured Benefit
If employee dies and spouse is covered: spouse becomes the new primary insured. Coverage continues for spouse + children at no additional premium. Strong feature for family income protection.

### Waiver of Premium
If employee becomes totally disabled before age 65, after 90 days disability premiums are waived for up to 24 months while disability persists. (TX has different application — see COC.)

### Progressive Diseases Rider — Specified Diagnosis (% of face)

| Disease | % |
|---|---|
| Advanced Alzheimer's Disease | 100% |
| Advanced Parkinson's Disease | 100% |
| Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS) | 100% |
| Sustained Multiple Sclerosis (MS) | 100% |
| Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD) | 25% |
| Crohn's Disease | 25% |

### Tier I Specified Disease Rider (25% of face)
Adrenal Hypofunction (Addison's), Cerebrospinal Meningitis (bacterial), Diphtheria, Encephalitis, Huntington's Chorea, Legionnaires' Disease, Lyme Disease, Malaria, Muscular Dystrophy, Myasthenia Gravis, Necrotizing Fasciitis, Osteomyelitis, Poliomyelitis (Polio), Rabies, Sickle Cell Anemia, Systemic Lupus, Systemic Sclerosis (Scleroderma), Tetanus, Tuberculosis.

### Tier II Specified Disease Rider — based on hospital confinement
Bird Flu/H1N1, Ebola, Human Coronavirus, Influenza, Pneumonia. Pay-out tied to length of hospital confinement:
- 10% if confined 4–9 days
- 25% if confined ≥ 10 days
- 40% if confined to ICU

### Key Exclusions (Critical Illness)
Self-inflicted, suicide, illegal acts, war, military duty, intoxication. Diagnosis/treatment must be made in the U.S. Pre-existing condition rules apply (read COC pages 8–11 for state-specific PCC clauses).

## How to File a Claim

**Online:** myaflac.aflac.com → New Claim
**Phone:** 1-800-433-3036 (Aflac Group Customer Service)
**Mail (Notice of Claim & Proof of Loss):** PO Box 84075, Columbus GA 31993-9103
**Mail (Premium Payments / Continuation of Coverage):** PO Box 84069, Columbus GA 31908-4069
**Texas customer service:** 888-515-1942
**Group insurance website:** aflacgroupinsurance.com

What you'll need to file:
- Cert # CER0002539005 + DOB + SSN last 4
- Date of incident, type of injury/illness
- Provider name(s) and treatment dates
- Itemized bills, EOBs, ER report, doctor's notes (copies — Aflac doesn't return originals)

**Reminder:** Aflac is a SUPPLEMENTAL plan. It pays on top of UHC. Even when UHC denies/discounts a claim, you can still collect Aflac benefits as long as the underlying event qualifies. File both — don't choose.
