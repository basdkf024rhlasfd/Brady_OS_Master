## HELOC Strategy — Capital Facility, Not Liability

**Philosophy:** HELOC is a low-cost revolving credit line for appreciating home investments, not a debt to eliminate aggressively. Draw period runs ~9.5 more years (expires ~2034). Only interest required monthly during draw period.
