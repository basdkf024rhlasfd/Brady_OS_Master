## Key Facilities & Supply Chain

- **Virginia Beach plant:** 150 acres, 1.4M sq ft, operating since 1970s. 100+ models manufactured. $60M+ invested in battery manufacturing through 2025.
- **Component sourcing:** 60% from local US suppliers — critical tariff advantage.
- **Distribution:** 11 regional branches/distributors → 10,000+ authorized dealers.
- **Dealer model (THE MOAT):** Products sold ONLY through authorized dealers. No Amazon, no mass retail. In-person delivery required. Dealers provide maintenance, repair, safety training. Margin protection = dealer loyalty = service network moat.
