## Key Facilities & Supply Chain

- **Bear Creek, WI:** World's largest sauerkraut plant. $13M expansion underway (20% capacity increase, 20M lb). 90%+ cabbage sourced from Wisconsin family farms.
- **New York facility:** Second manufacturing plant
- **Co-manufacturing:** Domestic and international partners. 100% North American manufacturing.
- **Taylor Farms:** Strategic minority equity investor. Largest fresh vegetable grower/processor in US. Optimizes FFH supply chain.
- **Distribution:** 100% refrigerated (except Silver Floss shelf-stable and new Bubbies shelf-stable formats). Cold chain is core infrastructure.
