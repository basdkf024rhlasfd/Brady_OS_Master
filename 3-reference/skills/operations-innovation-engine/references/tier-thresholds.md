# Tier Thresholds

Every surviving idea gets a tier based on composite score. Tier determines how much
space the idea gets in the deliverable.

## Bands

| Tier | Composite | Name | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | ≥ 4.0 | High Conviction | Full page; highlighted in executive summary Top 5 |
| **Tier 2** | 3.5 - 3.9 | Strong | Full page; normal treatment |
| **Tier 3** | 3.0 - 3.4 | Worth Exploring | Condensed: name + one paragraph + first move only |
| **Cut** | < 3.0 | — | Not in deliverable; recorded in `learning-log.yml` with reason |

## Target Distribution Per Run

| Tier | Target | Signal if off |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | 6-10 | <5 → methods didn't fit problem set; >12 → scoring too lenient |
| Tier 2 | 10-15 | Typical fat middle |
| Tier 3 | 5-10 | Lower bound of "worth naming at all" |
| **Total surviving** | **25-35** | Outside this range → re-examine generation quality |

## Tier 1 vs Tier 2 — The Distinction

Tier 1 ideas share at least one of:
- **Precedent at scale** — Proven by ≥2 comparable operators with public performance data
- **Structural impact** — Changes the cost curve, unit economics, or addressable market
- **Reversible pilot** — Can go live in <90 days with a clear kill criterion

Tier 2 ideas are strong but usually missing one of the three above. Most often: the
precedent is thin (only one operator has done it, or precedent is in an adjacent
industry only) or the pilot takes 3-9 months to get data.

## Tier 3 — What Qualifies

Tier 3 is "worth naming but not worth detailing." Include if:
- The idea names a real problem even if the solution is ambitious
- The idea is a natural follow-on to a Tier 1/2 idea (pair them)
- The idea opens a strategic door (partnership, format, channel) that Brady wants
  documented even if execution is far away

Cut if the idea is redundant with a higher-tier idea OR there's no clear next step.

## Rules

- **No ties across tiers for the same problem.** If two ideas address the same P# with
  similar approaches, pick one and cut the other. The learning log records the cut.
- **Composite score is calculated, not negotiated.** Don't fudge anchors to push an idea
  into a tier. Adjust the scoring anchors themselves if they're consistently off.
- **Tier 1 caps at ~10.** If more than 10 ideas score ≥4.0, re-apply the rubric more
  strictly. The top of the list should feel rare.
