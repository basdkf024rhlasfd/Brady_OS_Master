# The AI *Value Inversion*

Most companies invest in AI projects first and hope people follow.
The best companies invest in people first — and everything else accelerates.

---

##### The Problem

## 95% of Organizations See No Real AI ROI

- **$200B+ invested** in enterprise AI since 2022
- Only **~5% of companies** report meaningful EBIT impact
- 79% of companies using gen AI have *not redesigned a single workflow*
- The gap isn't technology — it's behavior change

*McKinsey State of AI 2025 (n=1,933)*

## Two Approaches

**The Default**
Build the tech. Hope people follow.

- 75-85% traditional workflows
- 5-15% AI-enabled work
- 1-5% AI platforms
- Long payback, high failure rate

**The Inversion**
Build the people. Tech follows faster.

- 25-35% traditional workflows
- 45-55% AI-enabled work
- 15-25% AI platforms
- Faster ROI, compounding returns

---

##### Evidence

## The Numbers That Matter

| Metric | Average | Best-in-Class |
|--------|---------|---------------|
| Traditional work (no AI) | 75-85% | 25-35% |
| AI-enabled daily work | 5-15% | 45-55% |
| AI platforms (scalable) | 1-5% | 15-25% |
| Weekly time saved | 2.2 hrs | 4+ hrs |

*Estimates synthesized from McKinsey, St. Louis Fed, Worklytics 2025*
