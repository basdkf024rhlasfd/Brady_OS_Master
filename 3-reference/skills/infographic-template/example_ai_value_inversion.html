Point of view

"The ROI on AI projects is 2–4 years. The ROI on AI fluency is tomorrow morning."

"88% of companies say they use AI. 5% get real value. The difference isn't technology — it's whether people changed how they work."

"Your best people are already using AI. They're just not telling you. That's not adoption — that's a shadow workforce you can't see or scale."

"You don't have an AI strategy problem. You have a behavior change problem disguised as a technology investment."
