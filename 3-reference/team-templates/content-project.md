## Purpose

Producing a content deliverable — article, case study, landing page, book chapter, thought leadership piece, or similar. Can live under any program.

## Roles Needed

| Role | Description | Suggested Draft From |
|------|-------------|---------------------|
| Lead | Owns the brief, voice, and final approval. | Claudine or Bo |
| Writer | Produces the content. Owns first draft through revision. | marketing/content-strategist, marketing/copywriter, or Burt (for voice-matched content) |
| Reviewer | Edits for quality, coherence, and accuracy. | Phil (for coherence) or marketing/editor |
| Designer | Visual layout, graphics, or page design if needed. | design/ agents |

Minimum viable team: Lead + Writer.

## Interaction Rules

- Lead provides brief before Writer starts (audience, goal, constraints, voice)
- Writer delivers drafts to Lead, not directly to publish
- Reviewer gets one full pass before final approval — feedback is specific, not vague
- If Writer and Reviewer disagree, Lead decides
- No publishing without Lead sign-off

## Goals & Success Criteria

- Content matches the brief (audience, goal, voice)
- Published on target platform by deadline
- Achieves stated purpose (generate leads, establish authority, support sales, etc.)

## Authority

- **Day**: Writer makes word-level and structure decisions within the brief.
- **Cycle**: Lead can adjust brief, timeline, or scope.
- **ARC**: Brady approves topics, platforms, and anything public-facing that represents the brand.
