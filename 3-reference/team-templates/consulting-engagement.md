## Purpose

Delivering a paid advisory or consulting engagement — from scoping through deliverable handoff. Lives under the Consulting program.

## Roles Needed

| Role | Description | Suggested Draft From |
|------|-------------|---------------------|
| Lead | Owns client relationship, scope management, and delivery timeline. | Bo or Claudine |
| Strategist | Develops the core analysis, frameworks, or recommendations. | Claudine (Chat mode) or strategy/ agents |
| Builder | Creates deliverables (decks, reports, models, specs). | Claudine (Code mode) or relevant specialist |
| Reviewer | Pressure-tests recommendations before client delivery. | Phil |

Minimum viable team: Lead + Strategist (can be same agent if scope is small).

## Interaction Rules

- Lead owns all client-facing communication — no agent sends anything externally without Lead approval
- Strategist delivers recommendations to Lead, not directly to client
- Reviewer sees final deliverable before it goes to client
- Builder produces artifacts to spec, does not freelance on scope
- Debrief after engagement closes: what worked, what to reuse, what to change

## Goals & Success Criteria

- Deliverable meets agreed scope and timeline
- Client satisfied (would engage again or refer)
- Revenue collected per agreement
- Reusable frameworks or templates captured in Layer 3 for future engagements

## Authority

- **Day**: Builder makes formatting/execution decisions. Strategist makes analytical decisions.
- **Cycle**: Lead can adjust deliverable scope within client agreement.
- **ARC**: Brady approves new engagements, pricing, and scope changes that affect the client relationship.
