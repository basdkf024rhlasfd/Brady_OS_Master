# Example Pitch Deck Outline: Verdant Analytics

A fictional property management analytics company used to illustrate the 12-section framework.

---

## 1. Purpose & Vision
Every property manager makes data-driven decisions without hiring a data team.

## 2. Target Audience
Mid-market property management companies (50-500 units) in the US Southeast. The buyer is the General Manager or VP of Operations — the person who gets blamed when occupancy drops but doesn't have the tools to see it coming.

## 3. The Problem
- **Financial:** $2.4M average annual waste from manual lease processing and reactive maintenance across a 200-unit portfolio
- **Operational:** GMs spend 12 hours/week compiling reports from 4+ disconnected systems (PMS, accounting, maintenance, CRM)
- **Personal:** Promoted for gut instinct. Punished when the gut is wrong. No early warning system.

## 4. The Solution
- Automated lease processing → saves $1.8M/year per portfolio
- Unified dashboard replaces manual reports → recovers 10 hours/week per GM
- Predictive occupancy alerts → GM acts 45 days before vacancy hits

## 5. Commercial Model
| Tier | Price | Target |
|------|-------|--------|
| Starter | $500/mo | <100 units |
| Growth | $1,500/mo | 100-500 units |
| Enterprise | Custom | 500+ units, multi-site |

- 85% gross margin
- 14-month CAC payback
- $18K average ACV

## 6. Market Analysis
- **TAM:** 320K US property management companies × $18K avg annual software spend = $5.8B
- **SAM:** 48K mid-market companies in Southeast target regions = $864M
- **SOM:** 2% capture in 3 years = $17.3M ARR

## 7. Market Viability
- 5 funded competitors in adjacent segments (Entrata, AppFolio, Buildium) — validates demand
- New HUD reporting requirements (2027) create compliance urgency
- 73% of GMs surveyed (n=340) want to replace current reporting tools
- PropTech funding up 22% YoY despite broader market contraction

## 8. Evidence of Success
- $380K ARR (growing 18% MoM)
- 22 paying customers, 3 enterprise pilots closing Q2
- 180% net revenue retention (expansion within existing accounts)
- NPS: 72
- Average implementation: 14 days (vs. industry average 90+ days)

## 9. Future Growth
| Year | ARR | Milestone |
|------|-----|-----------|
| Y1 | $800K | Southeast market penetration |
| Y2 | $3.2M | Enterprise tier launch + Mid-Atlantic expansion |
| Y3 | $9.6M | National + platform marketplace revenue |

Gross margin expands 78% → 88% as infrastructure amortizes. Break-even at $2.8M ARR (mid-Y2).

## 10. Unique Advantage
- Proprietary dataset: 2.1M lease transactions → prediction accuracy competitors can't replicate without 3+ years of market presence
- Integration depth: Connected to top 4 PMS systems (Yardi, RealPage, AppFolio, Entrata) — 18-month head start on integration buildout
- Switching cost: Once occupancy predictions inform staffing and maintenance budgets, removing the system means going blind

## 11. Horizon Mapping
- **H1 (Now → 12 months):** Deepen Southeast. Ship enterprise tier. Hit 50 customers.
- **H2 (12-24 months):** Expand Mid-Atlantic and Texas. Launch marketplace for third-party analytics modules. Add maintenance prediction.
- **H3 (24-48 months):** Platform play — become the analytics layer for the property management stack. License prediction engine to PMS vendors.

## 12. The Team
- **CEO:** 12 years in property management technology. Built and sold PMS to Yardi (2019).
- **CTO:** Former ML lead at Zillow. Built Zestimate v3 (valuation accuracy improved 14%).
- **VP Sales:** 8 years at AppFolio. Built mid-market sales team from 0 → $12M ARR.
- **Advisor:** Former COO of Greystar (managed 500K units). Opened first 5 enterprise pilots.
- **Hiring:** VP Engineering (Q2), Head of Customer Success (Q3).
